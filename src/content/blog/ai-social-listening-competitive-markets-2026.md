### 🔍 Step 1: Define Your Battlefield (Not Just Your Keywords)

Most teams start with brand names or competitor mentions. That’s too narrow.

In 2026, you must listen to the **entire problem space**.

#### Example:
Instead of just tracking “Salesforce” or “HubSpot,” monitor:
- “best CRM for enterprise sales teams”
- “how to automate outbound sequences”
- “AI email tools for B2B sales”

These are **intent-rich phrases**—not just mentions.
