Instantly.ai's pricing model disrupted the cold email market by eliminating per-seat charges. While competitors like Lemlist and Woodpecker charge per connected email account, Instantly offers unlimited email accounts at every paid tier. But Instantly's pricing has expanded beyond a single sending product — the platform now sells separate subscriptions for its B2B Lead Finder database, creating a two-product pricing structure that is more complex than it initially appears.

## Instantly Sending Platform Pricing (June 2026)

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Active Leads | Email Accounts | Key Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Growth | $37 | $30 | 1,000 | Unlimited | Warmup, basic campaigns |
| Hypergrowth | $97 | $77.6 | 25,000 | Unlimited | A/B testing, global block list |
| Light Speed | $358 | $286.3 | 500,000 | Unlimited | API access, priority support |

### What "Active Leads" Means

This is a commonly misunderstood metric. "Active leads" refers to the number of contacts currently enrolled in active email sequences — not total contacts in your database. A contact is "active" while their sequence is running and becomes "inactive" after the sequence completes. This means you can process far more contacts than the active lead limit over time, as completed sequences free up slots for new contacts.

## Instantly B2B Lead Finder Pricing

Instantly sells its contact database as a separate subscription:

|---|---|---|---|
| Growth Leads | $47/mo | 1,000 leads | $0.047 |
| Supersonic Leads | $97/mo | 5,000 leads | $0.019 |
| Hyperleads | $197/mo | 25,000 leads | $0.008 |

### Total Cost: Sending + Data

Most teams need both the sending platform and the lead database:

| Combined Stack | Sending Plan | Lead Finder Plan | Total Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter Setup | Growth ($37) | Growth Leads ($47) | **$84/month** |
| Scaling Setup | Hypergrowth ($97) | Supersonic ($97) | **$194/month** |
| Full Scale | Light Speed ($358) | Hyperleads ($197) | **$555/month** |

At $84/month for the starter bundle, Instantly remains competitively priced. But the scaling math reveals that by the time you need serious volume, you are spending $194-555/month — in line with more feature-rich platforms.

## Hidden Costs Beyond Instantly

Even with both Instantly subscriptions, a complete outbound stack requires additional tools:

| Need | Tool | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Email verification | ZeroBounce or NeverBounce | $16-50 |
| Phone data | Lusha or Cognism | $49-1,000+ |
| CRM | HubSpot or Salesforce | $0-100+ |
| LinkedIn outreach | Expandi or Dripify | $59-99 |
| **Total additional costs** | | **$124-1,249+** |

The "affordable" $37/month Instantly plan often sits at the center of a $200-500/month tool stack once you add the components needed for a professional outbound operation.

## Who Gets Maximum Value

Instantly's pricing delivers best value for:
- **Agencies** managing multiple client sending domains (unlimited accounts = massive savings vs per-seat tools).
- **Solo founders** doing founder-led outbound with the $37 Growth plan.
- **Teams that already have a data source** and just need sending infrastructure.
