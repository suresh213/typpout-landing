## Proven Tactics (With Examples & Data)

### Tactic 1: The “Recent Post” Hook

**What to do**: When a prospect posts about a pain point, respond within 1–2 hours with a **context-aware message**.

**Example**:
> **Prospect post**: “Switched to a new CRM and now our sales team is drowning in data.”
> **Your AI-generated reply**:
> “I hear you—CRM data overload is brutal. We helped [Client X] cut reporting time by 52% in 30 days. Want to see how?”

**Why it works**:
- **47% higher reply rate** when referencing a recent post (Social Selling Labs, 2025).
- Shows you’re listening, not just blasting.

### Tactic 2: The Competitor Gap Play

**What to do**: When a prospect mentions a competitor (especially a negative way), jump in with a value-based alternative.

**Example**:
> **Prospect tweet**: “Our current agency keeps missing deadlines. Not a good look.”
> **Your AI reply**:
> “Ouch—that’s rough. We’ve helped agencies like yours cut turnaround times by 60%. Happy to share how.”

**Data**: Competitor mentions trigger **3x more engagement** than generic outreach (Gartner, 2025).

### Tactic 3: The “Social Proof” Close

**What to do**: Use AI to pull a case study or result from a similar client and inject it into your message.

**Example**:
> “We just helped [Agency Y] increase pipeline by 200% in 90 days using this exact playbook. Want to see if it fits?”

**Why it works**:
- **Social proof increases reply rates by 68%** (Cialdini’s principle in action).
