Choosing a B2B email finder tool often comes down to one question: how much does it actually cost per usable lead? Skrapp.io uses a credit-based pricing model that looks affordable on the surface, but the real cost depends on factors most pricing pages do not advertise — accuracy rates, credit consumption rules, and the hidden expense of needing supplementary tools. Here is a full breakdown of Skrapp.io pricing in 2026, including the numbers they do not put on the landing page.

## Skrapp.io Pricing Plans (June 2026)

Skrapp.io offers five tiers, all based on monthly credit allocations:

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price (per month) | Credits/Month | Users Included | Cost Per Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | 50 | 1 | $0.00 |
| **Starter** | $49 | ~$39 | 1,000 | 2 | $0.049 |
| **Seeker** | $99 | ~$79 | 5,000 | 5 | $0.020 |
| **Enterprise** | $199 | ~$159 | 20,000 | 8 | $0.010 |
| **Global** | $299 | ~$239 | 50,000 | 15 | $0.006 |

Annual billing typically saves around 20%, and unused credits roll over month-to-month — a genuinely useful feature for teams with inconsistent prospecting volumes.

## How Skrapp.io Credits Actually Work

Understanding credit consumption is critical before committing to a plan:

**One credit = one email lookup attempt.** Whether you search via the LinkedIn Chrome extension, the domain search tool, or a bulk CSV upload, each individual email discovery consumes one credit.

**Fair credit policy:** Skrapp.io does not charge credits for results marked "Invalid" or "Unknown." You only consume credits when the tool returns a "Valid" or "Catch-all" email address. This sounds fair, but catch-all emails are a gray area — a catch-all domain accepts all emails sent to it, making it impossible to verify whether the specific address actually belongs to a real person.

**Bulk search multiplier:** When running bulk CSV uploads, each row in your spreadsheet consumes one credit per lookup. A 500-row CSV file will consume 500 credits regardless of the match rate. For teams doing large-scale list building, credits evaporate quickly.

## The Hidden Cost: Accuracy Adjustments

Here is the pricing detail that matters most. Skrapp.io claims a 92% success rate, but independent 2026 benchmarks report real-world deliverable rates between 42.8% and 75%. Let us calculate what that means for your actual cost per usable lead:

| Scenario | Credits Used | Deliverable Rate | Usable Emails | Effective Cost (Starter Plan) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Best Case** | 1,000 | 75% | 750 | $0.065 per email |
| **Average Case** | 1,000 | 65% | 650 | $0.075 per email |
| **Worst Case** | 1,000 | 50% | 500 | $0.098 per email |

At the worst-case scenario, you are paying nearly **10 cents per usable email** on the Starter plan. That is comparable to tools like Apollo.io or ZoomInfo that also include phone numbers, intent data, and built-in sequencing — features Skrapp.io does not offer at any price tier.

## What Skrapp.io Pricing Does NOT Include

This is where the true cost analysis gets important. Skrapp.io is exclusively an email finder. Your monthly subscription does not include:

- **Phone numbers or mobile data** — You need a separate tool (Lusha at $49+/month, Cognism at custom pricing) to get direct dials.
- **Email verification** — While Skrapp marks emails as "valid," experienced outbound teams always re-verify through a dedicated service like ZeroBounce ($16/month) or NeverBounce before sending campaigns.
- **Email sequencing** — You need Instantly ($37/month), Lemlist ($59/month), or Smartlead ($39/month) to actually send outreach.
- **Intent data** — No buying signals, funding alerts, or hiring triggers are included.
- **CRM** — While Skrapp integrates with HubSpot and Salesforce, you need those subscriptions separately.

When you stack these supplementary tools, the actual monthly cost for a functional outbound workflow using Skrapp.io as the data layer looks like this:

| Tool | Purpose | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Skrapp.io Starter | Email finding | $49 |
| ZeroBounce | Email verification | $16 |
| Instantly | Email sequencing | $37 |
| Lusha Starter | Phone data | $49 |
| **Total Stack Cost** | | **$151/month** |

For $151 per month across four separate tools, you get a fragmented workflow with manual CSV exports between each platform. Compare that to all-in-one platforms that bundle these capabilities into a single subscription.

## Who Gets Good Value from Skrapp.io Pricing?

The pricing structure works well for a specific profile:

1. **Solo freelancers** who need fewer than 50 emails per month (the free plan is genuinely useful here).
2. **Recruiters** doing targeted LinkedIn outreach who only need professional email addresses, not phone data.
3. **Early-stage startups** with one founder doing founder-led sales and a budget under $50/month for lead generation tools.

## Who Should Look Elsewhere

If you match any of these criteria, Skrapp.io pricing becomes poor value:

- **You need more than 1,000 verified emails per month** — The cost compounds quickly, especially after accuracy adjustments.
- **Your SDR team runs multi-channel sequences** — Paying for email data separately from phone data and sequencing tools creates inefficiency.
- **You want intent-driven outreach** — Skrapp gives you contact data with zero context about whether the prospect is actually in-market.
