## Why LinkedIn Voice Messages Outperform Other Channels

Busy executives don’t just ignore emails—they *dislike* them. Studies show that **70% of executives prefer video or voice-based communication** over text-based outreach. Here’s why voice messages stand out:

| **Channel**          | **Response Rate** | **Time to Engage** | **Personalization Potential** | **Trust Factor** |
|----------------------|------------------|---------------------|-------------------------------|------------------|
| Cold Email           | 3-5%             | 2-5 days            | Low (unless hyper-personalized) | Medium           |
| LinkedIn InMail      | 10-15%           | 1-3 days            | Medium                        | Medium           |
| **LinkedIn Voice Msg** | **20-35%**      | **Same day**        | **High (tone, pacing, emotion)** | **High**         |
| Cold Call            | 1-3%             | Immediate           | Low                           | Low              |

### The Science Behind Voice Messages
1. **Tone Matters**: The human voice conveys **emotion, confidence, and urgency**—elements that text simply can’t replicate.
2. **Scarcity & FOMO**: Executives receive **hundreds of messages daily**. A voice message feels **exclusive** and **worth listening to**.
3. **Lower Cognitive Load**: Listening is **faster and easier** than reading, especially on mobile.

> **Pro Tip**: LinkedIn Voice Messages are **not just for cold outreach**. They work brilliantly for **follow-ups, nurturing sequences, and even closing deals** once you’ve established initial contact.

## Step-by-Step: How to Craft a High-Converting LinkedIn Voice Message

Sending a voice message isn’t as simple as hitting record and winging it. **Strategy matters.** Follow this framework to maximize your response rates:

### Step 1: **Identify Your Ideal Prospect**
Not every executive is a good fit for voice messages. Use **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** to filter for:
- **Job titles** (e.g., CTO, CFO, VP of Sales)
- **Industry** (tech, SaaS, finance)
- **Seniority level** (Director+)
- **Recent engagement** (liked/commented on posts in your niche)

> **Typpout Insight**: Use **real-time social listening** to track executives discussing topics relevant to your solution. This ensures your voice message **feels timely and relevant**.

### Step 2: **Personalize Beyond the Name**
Generic voice messages get ignored. **Hook them in the first 5 seconds.** Here’s how:

#### **Template 1: The "Pain Point" Opener**
> *"Hi [First Name], I noticed you recently posted about [specific challenge in their industry]. We’ve helped companies like [similar company] solve [specific problem] in just [timeframe]. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call to explore if this could work for you?"*

#### **Template 2: The "Social Proof" Opener**
> *"Hi [First Name], [Mutual Connection] recommended I reach out because you’re leading [specific initiative] at [Company]. We’ve helped teams like yours [achieve X result]. Would you be open to a quick chat?"*

#### **Template 3: The "Curiosity Gap" Opener**
> *"Hi [First Name], quick question—what’s your biggest challenge with [relevant pain point] right now? I might have a quick solution that could save you [time/money]."*

> **Pro Tip**: Keep it **under 30 seconds**. Executives have short attention spans.

### Step 3: **Deliver the Value Proposition Clearly**
After the hook, **state your value in one sentence**. Avoid jargon. Example:
> *"We help [ideal customer] [achieve X result] without [common pain point]."*

### Step 4: **Make the Ask Simple & Low-Commitment**
Busy executives hate long calls. **Position your ask as a "quick chat."** Example:
> *"Would you be open to a 15-minute call next week to see if this makes sense for you?"*

> **Alternative Asks (if they hesitate)**:
> - *"Would you be open to a quick intro email?"*
> - *"Could I send over a case study for you to review?"*

### Step 5: **End with a Clear CTA**
End your message with **one of these**:
- *"Let me know a time that works for you—I’m happy to accommodate."*
- *"If now isn’t a good time, no worries—I can follow up in a few weeks."*
- *"Just reply ‘yes’ and I’ll send over a calendar link."*

## Advanced Tactics: Multi-Touch Voice Message Sequences

One voice message isn’t enough. **Follow up with a sequence** to maximize response rates. Here’s a **proven 3-touch sequence**:

| **Touch** | **Channel**               | **Example Message**                                                                 | **Response Rate** |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| 1         | LinkedIn Voice Message    | *"Hi [Name], saw your post on [topic]. We’ve helped [similar company] [solve X]. Would love to share how—15 mins?"* | 20-30%           |
| 2         | LinkedIn Comment (Public) | *"@[Name]—quick follow-up in case you missed my voice message. Always happy to help if [pain point] is a priority."* | 10-15%           |
| 3         | LinkedIn InMail           | *"Hi [Name], since voice messages aren’t getting through, I’ll keep this short: [1-line value prop]. Would you be open to a quick call?"* | 5-10%            |

## Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

| **Mistake**                          | **Why It Fails**                          | **How to Fix It**                          |
|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| **Too long (over 45 seconds)**        | Executives tune out                      | Keep it **under 30 seconds**               |
| **Generic hook (no personalization)**| Feels like spam                            | Reference **their specific pain point**    |
| **Overly salesy pitch**               | Triggers skepticism                        | Focus on **their needs, not your features**|
| **No clear CTA**                      | Leaves them confused                       | End with a **simple ask**                  |
| **Sending at wrong time**             | Gets buried in notifications               | Send **Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 2-4 PM** |
