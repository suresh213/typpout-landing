## How AI Turns Comments into High-Intent Leads

AI doesn’t just read comments—it **understands intent, qualifies leads, and engages them automatically.**

Here’s how it works in four steps:

### 1. **Real-Time Comment Scraping & Filtering**
YouTube’s API (or third-party tools) can pull comments in real time. But not all comments are valuable.

Use **AI-powered intent detection** to filter for:
- **High-intent keywords:** "how to," "best tool," "recommend," "pricing," "integration"
- **Question-based queries:** Comments ending with "?" often signal active research
- **Competitor mentions:** "vs. [Competitor]" or "alternative to [Tool]"

📌 **Example:**
A comment like *"Is [Your Tool] better than HubSpot for startups?"* scores high on intent and product fit.

### 2. **Sentiment & Intent Scoring**
Not all comments are equal. AI can classify sentiment and intent using NLP (Natural Language Processing):

| **Intent Level** | **Example Comment** | **Likelihood to Convert** |
|------------------|---------------------|----------------------------|
| High | "I need a CRM that integrates with Slack ASAP—any suggestions?" | 95% |
| Medium | "Does anyone use this tool for email automation?" | 60% |
| Low | "Great video!" | 5% |

**AI models (like BERT or custom fine-tuned LLMs)** can score each comment in milliseconds, prioritizing the most promising leads.

### 3. **Automated Personalized Responses**
Once a high-intent comment is detected, AI can **generate and post a response instantly**—tailored to the user’s question and aligned with your value prop.

💡 **Example Response:**
> *"Hi [Name], great question! Our tool integrates seamlessly with Slack and automates lead routing—many startups see a 40% faster response time. Would you like a quick demo?"*

This isn’t spam. It’s **contextual, helpful, and conversion-optimized.**

Pro Tip: Use **Typpout’s AI Outreach** to auto-engage high-intent commenters with personalized messages, then seamlessly transition them into your CRM or calendar booking flow.

### 4. **Lead Qualification & Pipeline Integration**
The best AI systems don’t just respond—they **qualify and route leads** to your sales team.

Here’s how Typpout does it:

1. AI detects a high-intent comment
2. Instantly scrapes the user’s profile (if public) for company, role, and location
3. Scores intent and assigns a **lead grade (A-F)**
4. Pushes qualified leads to your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.)
5. Schedules a meeting or sends a follow-up sequence

📊 **Result:** Your sales team gets **pre-qualified, high-intent leads**—not cold calls or spam.

→ AI responses must sound human. Use templates but add context.

❌ **Not responding fast enough**
→ Intent decays fast. Aim for under 5 minutes.
