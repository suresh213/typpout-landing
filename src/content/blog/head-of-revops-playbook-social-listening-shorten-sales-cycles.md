## A 5-Step Playbook for RevOps Leaders

### Step 1: Build Your Social Listening Layer (The Foundation)

You need **three layers of data**:

1. **Owned Channels**
   - Track mentions of your brand, competitors, and key topics on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Reddit, and industry forums.
   - Tools: Hootsuite Insights, Brandwatch, Sprout Social

2. **Employee Amplification**
   - Monitor when your AEs, SDRs, or executives are tagged or quoted.
   - Example: A prospect tags your CEO in a post about scaling GTM → **instant trigger**

3. **Intent Keywords & Competitor Mentions**
   - Keywords: “Need a better CRM,” “Looking to switch from HubSpot,” “Evaluating revenue intelligence tools”
   - Competitor mentions: “Salesloft vs. Outreach,” “Gong alternatives”

> 🔧 **Pro Tip:** Use **Boolean searches** to reduce noise:
> ```
> ("need CRM" OR "CRM alternatives" OR "best sales tool") AND ("looking" OR "evaluating" OR "considering")
> ```

---

### Step 2: Detect Intent in Real Time (AI-Powered Filtering)

Not all signals are equal. Use **AI-driven intent scoring**:

| Signal | Score | Action |
|-------|-------|--------|
| “We’re frustrated with our current CRM” | 95 | Immediate outreach |
| “Considering a new sales engagement tool” | 80 | Follow up in 24h |
| “Just saw a demo of Competitor X” | 60 | Nurture sequence |

**Typpout Tip:** Integrate your social listening tool with your CRM (via webhooks) to auto-create leads with **intent confidence scores**.

---

### Step 3: Trigger Hyper-Personalized Outreach (Don’t Be Creepy—Be Relevant)

Once intent is detected, **respond within 15–30 minutes** with:

- **Context:** Reference the exact post or conversation
- **Value:** Offer a tailored insight (e.g., “We helped [Company X] reduce cycle time by 30% with [Feature Y]”)
- **Next Step:** Book a 15-minute call or demo

> ⚠️ Avoid: Generic “Let’s chat” messages.
> ✅ Do: “I saw your post about struggling with sales cycle length—we solved that for [Similar Company] with [Tool]. Can I show you a 60-second demo?”

**Data Waterfall Model:**
```
Social Mention → Lead Created → Intent Scored → Personalized Sequence → Meeting Booked
```
