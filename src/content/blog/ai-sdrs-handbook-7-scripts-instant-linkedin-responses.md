## The 7 Scripts for Instant LinkedIn Comment Replies

Not all comments are created equal. The best replies depend on:
1. **The type of comment** (question, objection, praise, neutral).
2. **The prospect’s intent** (are they just browsing, or are they a potential lead?).
3. **Your goal** (qualify, educate, or book a meeting).

Below are **7 high-converting scripts**, categorized by comment type, along with when to use them and why they work.

---

### **1. The "Agreement + Question" Script (For Neutral/Positive Comments)**
**Best for:** Prospects who agree with your post but haven’t shown explicit interest.

**Example Comment:**
*"Great insights! I’ve been struggling with [topic] for months."*

**Script:**
> *"Thanks for reading, [First Name]! I appreciate the kind words—what’s the biggest challenge you’re facing with [topic] right now? We’ve helped similar teams [achieve X result], and I’d love to share how we did it."*

**Why it works:**
- Acknowledges their comment (builds rapport).
- Asks an open-ended question (qualifies their pain point).
- Positions you as a helpful expert (not a salesperson).

**Pro Tip:** Use this to **qualify leads** before pitching. If they engage further, follow up with a personalized DM or meeting request.

---

### **2. The "Objection Handling" Script (For Skeptical Comments)**
**Best for:** Prospects who challenge your post or express doubt.

**Example Comment:**
*"This sounds too good to be true. How do you actually measure ROI?"*

**Script:**
> *"Great question, [First Name]! ROI measurement varies by use case, but our customers typically see [X]% improvement in [Y metric] within [Z timeframe]. Would you be open to a quick chat to see if this aligns with your goals?"*

**Why it works:**
- Validates their concern (reduces defensiveness).
- Provides a specific example (builds credibility).
- Ends with a soft CTA (meeting request).

**Avoid:** Generic responses like *"Let me know if you have questions!"*—they kill momentum.

---

### **3. The "Industry Expert" Script (For Thought Leadership Comments)**
**Best for:** Prospects who engage with your content but don’t ask questions.

**Example Comment:**
*"This is exactly what we need at [Company]!"*

**Script:**
> *"That’s awesome to hear! We’ve seen [similar companies] reduce [pain point] by [X]% using our approach. If you’re open to it, I’d love to share a case study or hop on a quick call to discuss how we could help [Company]. No pressure—just happy to help!"*

**Why it works:**
- Reinforces your authority (social proof).
- Offers value upfront (case study).
- Low-pressure CTA (reduces friction).

**Pro Tip:** Pair this with a **LinkedIn article or case study link** for even higher response rates.

---

### **4. The "Curiosity Gap" Script (For High-Intent Comments)**
**Best for:** Prospects who hint at a need or pain point.

**Example Comment:**
*"We’re looking to [solve X] by Q3."*

**Script:**
> *"That’s a big goal! Most of our clients hit [Y milestone] by [Z timeframe] using [method]. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat to see if we can help you get there faster?"*

**Why it works:**
- Highlights urgency (Q3 timeline).
- Positions your solution as a shortcut.
- Short, conversational CTA (easy to say yes to).

**Avoid:** Over-explaining in the comment—save the details for the call.

---
### **5. The "Social Proof" Script (For Generic Praise)**
**Best for:** Prospects who compliment your post but don’t engage further.

**Example Comment:**
*"This is really helpful!"*

**Script:**
> *"Thank you! We actually have a few customers who saw [specific result] after implementing this. If you’re curious, I’d be happy to share how they did it—just let me know if you’d like the link!"*

**Why it works:**
- Reinforces your expertise (social proof).
- Gives them an easy next step (no commitment).
- If they take the link, they’re warming up to you.

**Pro Tip:** Follow up in 24 hours if they don’t click the link.

---
### **6. The "Direct CTA" Script (For High-Value Engagers)**
**Best for:** Prospects who are clearly interested (e.g., they ask for a demo).

**Example Comment:**
*"This looks interesting—can we see a demo?"*

**Script:**
> *"Absolutely! I’d love to set up a quick demo for you. What’s your availability this week for a 15-minute walkthrough? Here’s my calendar: [Calendly link]."*

**Why it works:**
- Direct and to the point (no fluff).
- Uses a self-serve booking link (reduces friction).
- Positions you as responsive and professional.

**Avoid:** Over-explaining in the comment—save details for the demo.

---
### **7. The "Follow-Up" Script (For Prospects Who Engage but Don’t Reply)**
**Best for:** Prospects who liked/commented but didn’t respond to your first reply.

**Comment Example:**
*They liked your post but didn’t reply to your first response.*

**Script:**
> *"Thanks for the engagement, [First Name]! If you have any questions about [topic], feel free to ask—I’m happy to help. Also, here’s a [resource link] we put together for folks in your position. Hope it’s useful!"*

**Why it works:**
- Keeps the conversation alive (no pressure).
- Provides value (resource link).
- Soft CTA (encourages further engagement).

**Pro Tip:** Use this to **re-engage** before your next outreach sequence.
