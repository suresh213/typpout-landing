Having accurate contact data is critical for any outbound sales motion. Lusha and Apollo.io are two of the most popular platforms for finding B2B email addresses and phone numbers.

## Apollo: Large-Scale List Building

Apollo offers a massive database and is highly cost-effective for building large lists. It also includes basic sequencing tools, making it a popular all-in-one platform for startups.

## Lusha: Premium Direct Dials

Lusha is known for having higher accuracy, particularly for direct mobile phone numbers and European contact data. Its Chrome extension is widely used by SDRs to find contact details while browsing LinkedIn profiles.
