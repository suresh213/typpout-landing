## The AI Outreach Script Framework (That Works in 2026)

The best AI outreach scripts follow a **3-step framework**:

1. **Hook** (Grab attention in 3 seconds)
2. **Value Proposition** (Why should they care?)
3. **Call to Action (CTA)** (What’s the next step?)

Let’s break it down.

### Step 1: The Hook (Make Them Stop Scrolling)
The first 2 lines of your message determine whether it gets read. AI helps craft **data-driven hooks** like:

🔹 **"I saw your recent [article/LinkedIn post] on [topic], and I thought you’d appreciate this..."**
🔹 **"Most [industry] companies struggle with [pain point]. Here’s how [competitor] fixed it..."**
🔹 **"I ran a quick analysis on your [website/LinkedIn] and noticed [gap]. Does that resonate?"**

**Pro Tip:** Use **Typpout’s real-time social listening** to find recent posts, funding rounds, or hiring trends to personalize hooks instantly.

### Step 2: The Value Proposition (Show, Don’t Tell)
Instead of listing features, **lead with a pain point** and offer a quick win.

Examples:
🔹 **"We helped [similar company] reduce [metric] by 30% in 90 days. Here’s the exact playbook we used..."**
🔹 **"Most [industry] teams waste 10+ hours/week on [inefficient process]. We automated it—here’s how..."**
🔹 **"Your competitors are using [trend] to [achieve result]. We can help you get there faster."**

**Key:** Tie your solution to **their specific challenges** (not yours).

### Step 3: The CTA (Make It Easy to Say Yes)
End with a **low-friction ask** that doesn’t require a big commitment.

Examples:
🔹 **"Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see if this fits?"**
🔹 **"I’ve put together a quick case study on how [similar company] achieved [result]. Does that sound useful?"**
🔹 **"If this isn’t a priority now, no worries—I’ll circle back in a few weeks."**

**Avoid:**
❌ **"Let’s schedule a call."** (Too vague)
❌ **"Are you the right person to talk to?"** (Puts them on the spot)

## Real AI Outreach Templates (Copy-Paste & Adapt)

Here are **5 high-converting templates** you can use today. Each is designed to **maximize personalization** and **minimize friction**.

### **Template 1: LinkedIn DM for Founders (Acquisition Focused)**
**Hook:** *"I saw your post on [topic]—really insightful. We recently helped [Company X] [achieve result], and I’d love to share how we did it."*

**Value Prop:**
*"Most startups waste 20+ hours/month on [inefficient process]. We automated it using AI—here’s the 3-step framework we use with clients like yours."*

**CTA:**
*"Would you be open to a 10-minute call to see if this aligns with your goals? If not, no pressure—I can send over a quick Loom video instead."*

*"We audited [Company Y]’s tech stack and found 3 redundancies costing them $20K/year. Fixed it in 48 hours—here’s the exact playbook."*

**CTA:**

### **Template 4: Follow-Up Email (After No Response)**
