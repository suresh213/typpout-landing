### **Step 3: Real-Time Social Listening & Engagement**

AI doesn’t just send messages—it **listens and responds** in real time.

**How It Works:**
1. **Track prospect activity** (likes, shares, comments on LinkedIn)
2. **AI detects engagement opportunities** (e.g., a prospect comments on a post about lead gen)
3. **Automatically engage** (with a relevant reply or follow-up)

**Example:**
> *Prospect comments:* "Struggling with LinkedIn outreach."
> *AI replies:* "Hey [Name], we helped [Company] 3X their response rate—happy to share a case study if you’re open to it!"

**Why This Works:**
- **Higher response rates** (you’re engaging when they’re active)
- **Builds trust** (you’re not just another sales bot)
