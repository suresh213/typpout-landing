LinkedIn automation is a high-risk, high-reward sales strategy. Expandi and LinkedHelper are two of the most popular tools, but they utilize very different tech stacks.

## LinkedHelper: Desktop Automation

LinkedHelper is a desktop application. It runs locally on your computer, opening a dedicated browser instance that physically clicks on LinkedIn elements. It is highly configurable and affordable, but requires your computer to be turned on to run.

## Expandi: Cloud-Based Automation

Expandi is a cloud-based platform. It runs on remote servers with dedicated proxies, meaning your campaigns run 24/7 without needing a desktop client. It is widely considered one of the safest and most user-friendly options on the market.

## The Intent-First Alternative

Both tools send automated connection requests and message sequences, which can feel spammy. [Typpout](/) only contacts prospects who have expressed active buying intent, leading to higher response rates and a lower risk of account suspension.

**Verdict:** Use LinkedHelper for budget-friendly desktop automation, Expandi for safe cloud-based automation, and [Typpout](/pricing) for intent-driven outreach.
