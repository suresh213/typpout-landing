### Step 2: Set Up AI-Powered Alerts (Not Just Keywords)

Keyword matching is table stakes. **AI-powered social listening in 2026 goes deeper:**

#### **Intent Signals to Track:**
✅ **Job Postings** (e.g., "Looking for a RevOps leader to fix our data chaos")
✅ **Funding Announcements** (e.g., "Series A startup hiring for sales ops")
✅ **Pain-Point Language** (e.g., "Our CRM is a dumpster fire—any recommendations?")
✅ **Competitor Mentions** (e.g., "Tired of HubSpot’s limitations—switching to [Your Competitor]")

**Pro Tip:**
- Use **Typpout’s AI data waterfalls** to **filter noise** (e.g., exclude job posts older than 7 days).
- Prioritize **high-intent leads** (e.g., someone posting about a problem your product solves **today**).

### Step 3: Engage with AI-Personalized Outreach

**Rule #1 in 2026:** **No more generic cold emails.**

AI-powered social listening lets you **craft hyper-personalized messages** based on **real-time data.**

#### **Example Workflow:**
1. **Listener detects:** A marketing director posts, *"Struggling with lead gen—our LinkedIn ads aren’t converting."*
2. **Typpout’s AI analyzes:** Their stack (HubSpot, Salesforce), recent hires (RevOps role), and pain points.
3. **Outreach message:**
   > *"Hey [Name],*
   > *Saw your post about LinkedIn ads not converting. We helped [Similar Company] 3X their MQLs in 90 days by [Your Solution].*
   > *Would you be open to a quick chat this week? Even if just to brainstorm."*
4. **Reply handling:** Typpout’s AI **automatically books the meeting** or schedules a follow-up if they engage.

**Result:**
- **Reply rates jump from 5% → 20%+**
- **Meetings booked in hours, not weeks**

**60% of sales happen after the 5th touch.**
