Kickfire is an IP address intelligence and B2B firmographic data provider. It is widely used to power website personalization, account-based marketing, and lead routing.

## The Challenge with IP Intelligence

IP intelligence tools tell you that an enterprise account is visiting your site. However, with remote work, IP-to-company matching accuracy has declined, and you still don't know *which* person in the company is interested.

## Typpout: Social Sourcing for Modern Reps

[Typpout](/) bypasses IP lookup limitations by sourcing lead data directly from public social posts. If a VP of Product posts a recommendation request on LinkedIn, Typpout identifies them, enriches their contact details, and writes a personalized outreach message.
