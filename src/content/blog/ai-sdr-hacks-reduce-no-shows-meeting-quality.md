### 🔍 Hack 1: Real-Time Social Listening to Book at the Right Time

Most SDRs rely on stale data or fixed sequences. But the best time to reach a prospect isn’t based on a calendar template—it’s based on **what they’re talking about right now**.

**How it works:**
- Typpout integrates with LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and industry forums.
- Our AI monitors prospect activity in real time (e.g., posts, comments, reactions).
- When a prospect engages with content about your industry or pain point, we trigger an **instant, contextually relevant outreach**.

**Example:**
A CFO posts: *“Struggling with cash flow visibility in our SaaS stack.”*
Our AI detects this, matches it to your solution, and sends:
> *“Hey [Name], saw your post on cash flow challenges—our customers reduce DSO by 30% with [Your Product]. Would 2pm Tuesday work to discuss?”*

📌 **Result:** Prospects respond 3x faster, and meetings booked via real-time signals have a **70% lower no-show rate** than cold outreach.

### 🧠 Hack 2: Intent-Based Qualification Using Data Waterfalls

Not all leads are equal. Some are tire-kickers. Others are ready to buy—but only you’ll know if you’re looking at the right signals.

**Typpout’s Data Waterfall Model:**

| Signal Type | Strength | Weight in Score |
|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Website visits (pricing page, demo) | High | 30 pts |
| LinkedIn engagement (comments on relevant posts) | Medium | 20 pts |
| Email opens (in last 7 days) | Low | 10 pts |
| Job title (e.g., CFO, RevOps) | Context | 20 pts |
| Company size (e.g., 500–2000 employees) | Context | 20 pts |

Leads scoring **above 80** get prioritized outreach. Leads below 50 are deprioritized or routed to nurture.

📌 **Result:** We’ve seen a **45% increase in meeting show-up rates** by focusing only on high-intent leads.

### 📅 Hack 3: AI-Driven Optimal Scheduling (No More 3pm Ghosts)

The timing of your meeting matters as much as the message.

Most teams use static calendar links (e.g., “Book a 15-minute slot”). But what if the prospect’s calendar is free only at 8am or 6pm? Or they’re traveling?

**Our AI Scheduler does three things:**
1. **Analyzes prospect availability patterns** (e.g., they only book meetings 9–11am on Tuesdays).
2. **Avoids low-energy slots** (e.g., Fridays after 3pm).
3. **Sends dynamic calendar links** that update in real time.

📌 **Result:** Meetings scheduled via AI scheduling have a **50% lower no-show rate** than generic Calendly links.

---

### 💬 Hack 4: AI Reply Handling That Keeps Prospects Engaged (No More Dead Ends)

A common cause of no-shows? The prospect stops responding mid-sequence.

Typpout’s AI doesn’t just send emails—it **converses**.

- It analyzes reply sentiment (positive, neutral, negative).
- It triggers follow-up messages based on tone:
  - “Great to hear you’re exploring this—let’s lock in a time.”
  - “Not the right fit? I can share a case study if helpful.”
- It surfaces objections (e.g., “I don’t have budget”) and routes them to reps for personalized responses.

**Example AI Response:**
> *Hi [Name],*
> *Thanks for the reply. I noticed you mentioned budget constraints. Many of our customers started with a pilot and scaled. Would you be open to a 15-minute discovery call to explore fit?*
> *—AI SDR*

📌 **Result:** Leads that engage with AI replies are **60% more likely to show up** to booked meetings.

---

### 📊 Hack 5: Post-Meeting AI Follow-Up That Reduces Last-Minute Cancellations

No-shows aren’t just about the initial booking—they happen at the last minute too.

Our AI monitors:
- Calendar cancellations
- Email opens (are they still engaging?)
- Website activity (are they researching competitors?)

If engagement drops, we trigger a **preemptive win-back message**:
> *“Hi [Name], saw you’re considering [Competitor X]. Before you decide, let’s clarify how we help customers avoid [common risk]. Can we reschedule to tomorrow at 10am?”*

📌 **Result:** We recover **25% of potential no-shows** with late-stage engagement nudges.
