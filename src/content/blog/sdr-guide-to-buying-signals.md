Not all leads are created equal. Sales Development Representatives (SDRs) who target prospects based on active buying signals achieve much higher conversion rates than those running generic outbound sequences.

## 1. Classify Intent Signals

* **High Intent:** Direct posts asking for software recommendations or competitor alternatives.
* **Medium Intent:** Company hiring spikes, new funding rounds, or job changes in your target accounts.
* **Low Intent:** Generic social posts discussing industry trends without specific needs.

## 2. Personalize Based on the Signal

When reaching out to a prospect, reference the exact signal that triggered the outreach. If they posted about outbound pain on LinkedIn, start your email by addressing that specific post.
