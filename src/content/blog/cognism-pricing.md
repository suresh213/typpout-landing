Cognism, like ZoomInfo, does not publish pricing on its website. Prospective customers must book a demo and go through a sales process to receive a quote. Based on aggregated user reports and industry analysis from 2026, here is what Cognism typically costs and how to evaluate whether the investment delivers ROI for your team.

## Estimated Cognism Pricing (2026)

| Component | Estimated Cost |
|---|---|
| Platform access (per seat) | $1,000 - $1,500/year per user |
| Diamond Data credits | Bundled in subscription (volume varies by plan) |
| Bombora intent data | Included on standard plans |
| Minimum contract | 12 months (annual billing) |
| Typical small team package (3 seats) | $15,000 - $25,000/year |
| Mid-market package (10 seats) | $30,000 - $50,000/year |

### How Diamond Data Credits Work

Cognism allocates Diamond Data credits as part of your subscription. Each credit reveals a phone-verified contact's full information. The number of Diamond Data credits included depends on your plan tier and negotiated contract terms.

Standard credits (non-Diamond) reveal contact data from Cognism's broader database without the human phone verification. These credits are more plentiful but deliver lower phone accuracy — similar to competitors like Apollo or Lusha.

### The Credit Trade-Off

Higher Diamond Data allocations cost more but deliver significantly better phone accuracy (80%+ vs 30-50% for standard data). Teams that rely heavily on cold calling should negotiate for maximum Diamond Data allocation, even if it increases the annual contract value — the SDR productivity gains from accurate phone numbers typically justify the premium.

## ROI Calculation Framework

The key question: does Cognism's data quality translate into enough additional pipeline to justify the premium over cheaper alternatives?

| Metric | Apollo.io ($49/user/mo) | Cognism (est. $100/user/mo) |
|---|---|---|
| Phone accuracy | 30-45% | 80%+ (Diamond) |
| Dial-to-connect rate | 3-5% | 8-12% |
| Connects per 100 dials | 3-5 | 8-12 |
| Meetings per 100 dials | 0.5-1 | 1.5-3 |

For a team making 100 cold calls per day, Cognism's accuracy advantage produces 1-2 additional meetings per day per SDR. If each meeting has a 20% close rate and $10,000 average deal value, those additional meetings generate $2,000-4,000 in expected revenue per SDR per day — easily justifying the $50-100/month premium over Apollo.

The math only works for teams with significant cold calling volume. For email-first teams, the phone accuracy premium provides minimal incremental value.

## When Cognism's Pricing Delivers ROI

- Cold calling generates more than 30% of your pipeline.
- Your team makes 50+ dials per SDR per day.
- You sell into European markets where GDPR compliance is mandatory.
- Average deal values exceed $5,000 per sale.

## When to Choose Cheaper Alternatives

- Email is your primary outbound channel.
- Your team is under 5 people.
- Annual tool budget is under $10,000.
- Your market is US-only.
