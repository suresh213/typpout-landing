## The 4-Step AI Playbook for Turning Visits Into Pipeline

Here’s how top growth teams structure their approach:

### Step 1: Capture Visits in Real-Time (Without Waiting for LinkedIn Alerts)
LinkedIn’s native notifications are slow and unreliable. Instead, use **AI-powered social listening** to:
- Track **anonymous visitors** (even if they don’t connect)
- Log **visits by company, role, and seniority**
- Trigger **automated workflows** instantly

**Example Workflow:**
| Trigger | Action | Tool |
|---------|--------|------|
| Profile Visit | Log in CRM + Enrich with company data | Typpout |
| High-Intent Visit (e.g., CEO of a target account) | Send personalized InMail | AI Outreach Tool |
| Repeat Visit (3+ times) | Escalate to SDR for direct call | Sales Engagement Platform |

> **Pro Tip:** Many visitors are researching competitors. Use **intent data** (like job postings, funding rounds) to prioritize high-value accounts.

### Step 2: Enrich Visitor Data with AI-Driven Insights
Generic outreach fails because it lacks context. AI solves this by:
- **Matching visitors to CRM records** (even if they’re not in your database)
- **Pulling firmographics** (company size, tech stack, hiring trends)
- **Identifying triggers** (recent funding, product launches, job changes)

**Example Personalization Snippet:**
> *"Hi [First Name], I noticed you visited our profile after checking out [Competitor’s] pricing page. We’ve helped 20+ [Industry] teams reduce costs by 30%—would you be open to a quick chat?"*

**Tools to Use:**
- **Typpout** (real-time LinkedIn visit tracking + CRM sync)
- **Clearbit** (firmographic enrichment)
- **Apollo.io** (intent data)

### Step 3: Automate Multi-Touch Sequences That Adapt to Engagement
One-off messages don’t work. Growth teams use **AI-driven sequences** that:
- **Escalate based on engagement** (e.g., if they click a link, send a follow-up)
- **Adjust tone based on their LinkedIn activity** (e.g., if they engage with your content, reference it)
- **Book meetings automatically** when prospects show intent

**Example Sequence:**
1. **Day 1:** Personalized InMail referencing their visit
2. **Day 3:** LinkedIn connection request + message
3. **Day 7:** Case study or ROI calculator (if they viewed your website)
4. **Day 10:** Calendar link (if they engaged)

**Response Rates Improve By:**
| Approach | Average Response Rate |
|----------|-----------------------|
| Generic InMail | 5-10% |
| AI-Personalized + Multi-Touch | 25-40% |

> **Key Insight:** The best sequences **sound human but scale effortlessly**—AI handles the heavy lifting while maintaining personalization.

### Step 4: Close the Loop with CRM Integration & Pipeline Tracking
Most teams lose track of visitors after the first touch. AI-powered GTM platforms ensure:
- **Visits → CRM Records → Pipeline Stages** (all automated)
- **Reply handling** (AI drafts responses, SDRs refine)
- **Meeting booking** (no more back-and-forth emails)

**Example Typpout Workflow:**
1. A CFO visits your profile → Typpout logs it in HubSpot.
2. AI drafts a personalized message → SDR reviews and sends.
3. If they reply, AI suggests next steps (e.g., "Send calendar link").
4. Meeting booked → Deal created in CRM.

## Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

1. **Treating Visits as One-Off Events**
   - ❌ *Send one message and move on.*
   - ✅ *Use a multi-touch sequence that escalates based on engagement.*

2. **Over-Automating Without Personalization**
   - ❌ *Use generic templates for everyone.*
   - ✅ *Let AI pull CRM data to craft hyper-relevant messages.*

3. **Ignoring Anonymous Visitors**
   - ❌ *Assume only connected visitors matter.*

4. **Not Closing the Loop in CRM**
   - ❌ *Let leads go cold after the first touch.*
   - ✅ *Automatically log visits, replies, and meetings in your CRM.*
