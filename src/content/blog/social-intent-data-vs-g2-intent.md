Buyer intent data is a cornerstone of modern B2B sales. While G2 Buyer Intent is a popular option, social intent data is emerging as a faster, more actionable alternative.

## G2 Intent: Account-Level Interest

G2 Intent tracks when users look at your product profile or compare you to competitors on G2. It is highly accurate for identifying accounts that are in-market, but it only provides account-level data, not the contact details of the specific reader.

## Social Intent: Real-Time Individual Sourcing

Social intent data tracks when specific individuals post their requirements on LinkedIn, Reddit, or X. It tells you exactly *who* is looking for help, provides their contact info, and gives you a clear reason to reach out immediately.
