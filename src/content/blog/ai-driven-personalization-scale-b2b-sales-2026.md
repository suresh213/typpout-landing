### Step 4: Automate Reply Handling & Meeting Booking

By 2026, **AI will qualify 60% of inbound replies**—freeing SDRs to focus on high-value conversations.

**How It Works:**

1. Inbound reply arrives (email, LinkedIn, form)
2. AI analyzes tone, intent, and keywords
3. Classifies as:
   - **Positive** (e.g., “Yes, let’s talk”)
   - **Neutral** (e.g., “Who else uses this?”)
   - **Negative** (e.g., “Not interested”)
4. For positive/neutral:
   - AI drafts a response
   - Sends calendar link (Calendly, Chili Piper)
   - Books meeting automatically
5. For negative:
   - Adds to suppression list or triggers soft nurture sequence

> **ROI:** Teams using AI reply handling book **3x more meetings** from the same number of replies.
