### Step 2: AI-Powered Data Waterfalls (Qualify Before You Outreach)
Not all leads are equal. Typpout’s **Data Waterfall** filters prospects in **3 stages** before they ever see your message:

1. **Intent Check:** Does this prospect have a real need? (e.g., job title, recent hiring, tech stack)
2. **Engagement Check:** Are they active in relevant communities? (e.g., posting in Slack groups)
3. **Fit Check:** Do they match your ICP? (e.g., company size, funding stage)

**Result:** Only the **hottest leads** get outreach—**cutting CAC by 40%+.**

### Step 3: AI Outreach That Feels Human (But Scales Forever)
Cold outreach sucks because it’s **generic.** Typpout’s AI crafts messages that **sound like they’re from a real person**—because they *are.*

**How?**
- **Dynamic personalization** (e.g., referencing their recent tweet or Slack post)
- **Multi-channel engagement** (email, Twitter DM, LinkedIn—in parallel)
- **Automated follow-ups** (but smarter—no spammy “did you see my email?”)

**Example Workflow:**
1. Prospect tweets about a pain point → Typpout detects it.
2. AI drafts a reply: *“Hey [Name], saw your tweet about [pain point]. We helped [similar company] reduce [metric] by 30%. Mind if I share how?”*
3. If they engage, Typpout books a meeting **automatically** via Calendly.

**No SDRs. No manual follow-ups. Just **consistent, high-conversion outreach.**
