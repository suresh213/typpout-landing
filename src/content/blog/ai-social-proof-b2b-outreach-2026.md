- **Objection:** *"We’re happy with our current provider."*

## Proven AI Social Proof Strategies (With Examples)

### Strategy 1: The "Peer at a Peer" Approach

**What it is:** Showing that a **peer at a similar company** (same role, industry, or tech stack) has engaged with you.

**Example (Typpout in Action):**
> *"Hey [First Name],*
>
> *I noticed you’re scaling your [Team] at [Company]—[Peer at Similar Company] just booked a demo with us to streamline their [Process]. They saw a 22% reduction in [Metric] within 3 months.*
>
> *Curious if this could help your team too?"*

**Why it works:**
- **Trust by association** (if they’re similar, their success is replicable).
- **Low cognitive load** (no need to parse a long case study).

**Data:** Typpout customers using this approach see **3x higher reply rates** than generic outreach.

### Strategy 2: The "Live Activity" Trigger

**What it is:** Using **real-time signals** (e.g., someone from their company visiting your pricing page) to craft a message.

**Example:**
> *"Hi [First Name],*
>
> *Your teammate [Name] was just checking out our [Pricing] page—we’re offering a limited-time pilot for teams in your org. Happy to chat if this aligns with your goals?"*

**Tools to enable this:**
- **Typpout’s real-time alerts** (integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, and LinkedIn).
- **Gong/Chorus** for call recording insights.

**Result:** **40% higher meeting booking rates** for teams using live triggers.

---

### Strategy 3: The "Competitor Comparison" Social Proof

**What it is:** Highlighting that **competitors’ customers** are switching to you.

**Example:**
> *"Hi [First Name],*
>
> *Did you know [Competitor]’s customers are moving to us for [Key Benefit]? [Peer Company] reduced costs by 30% after switching—happy to share their full story."*

**How to find these signals:**
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** (filter by "People who left [Competitor]").
- **TechCrunch articles** (search for "acquired by [Your Company]").
- **G2 reviews** (look for mentions of competitors).

**Pro Tip:** Pair this with a **free competitive audit** (e.g., *"We’ll show you how your stack compares to [Competitor]’s—no strings attached."*).
