Instantly.ai and Woodpecker are key players in cold email outreach, but they support different volume strategies.

## Instantly: Bulk Volume

Instantly allows you to connect unlimited sending accounts for a flat fee, making it highly cost-effective for large-scale campaigns.

## Woodpecker: Human Simulation

Woodpecker prioritizes deliverability safety, sending emails in irregular patterns to mimic natural human behavior.
