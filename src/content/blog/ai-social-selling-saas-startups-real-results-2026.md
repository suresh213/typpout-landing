## The 5-Step AI Social Selling Framework for SaaS Startups in 2026

Here’s the exact playbook we use at Typpout—scaled for startups with lean teams.

---

### Step 1: Build Your AI-Powered Social Listening Engine

**Problem:** SDRs waste hours scrolling LinkedIn, missing critical intent signals.

**AI Solution:**
- Use AI tools to scan LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Reddit, and forums for keywords like:
  - “Better alternative to [Competitor]”
  - “Struggling with [Pain Point]”
  - “Looking for [Feature] tool”
- Rank prospects by **intent score** (frequency, emotion, urgency)

✅ **Action:** Set up automated alerts for high-intent keywords using AI platforms like **Typpout, Apollo, or Crayon**.

---

### Step 2: Score & Prioritize Leads Using AI Intent Models

Not all leads are equal. AI helps you **predict who’s ready to buy**—not who just visited your site.

**How it works:**
- AI analyzes:
  - Social engagement (likes, shares, comments)
  - Website behavior (pages visited, time on page)
  - CRM activity (past emails opened, demo requests)
- Assigns a **Predictive Buyer Score (PBS)** from 0–100

📊 **Benchmark:**
- Teams using AI intent scoring see **70% higher conversion rates** from SQL to closed-won.

✅ **Action:** Integrate tools like **6sense, Demandbase, or Typpout** to auto-score leads.

---

Generic messages don’t work. AI enables **one-to-few personalization at scale**.
