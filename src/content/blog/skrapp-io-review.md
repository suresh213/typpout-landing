Skrapp.io has been a fixture in the B2B email finder market since 2016. Originally built as a lightweight LinkedIn email extraction tool, it carved out a loyal user base among solo SDRs and small sales teams who needed affordable access to professional email addresses. But the sales intelligence landscape has shifted dramatically since then. In this honest 2026 review, we break down whether Skrapp.io still holds up against modern GTM platforms that bundle intent data, waterfall enrichment, and autonomous outreach.

## What Skrapp.io Does Well

Credit where it is due — Skrapp.io does a few things competently:

**LinkedIn Chrome Extension:** The browser extension remains its strongest feature. It overlays a clean panel on LinkedIn profiles and Sales Navigator search results, letting you extract email addresses with a single click. The UX is snappy, and the extension rarely breaks after LinkedIn UI updates — which is more than you can say for many competitors.

**Domain Search:** Enter a company domain and Skrapp returns a list of employees with their predicted email addresses. The pattern-matching algorithm does a reasonable job for mid-size companies with standard naming conventions like firstname.lastname@company.com.

**Credit Rollover Policy:** Unlike many competitors who reset unused credits monthly, Skrapp.io rolls unused credits forward. For teams with inconsistent prospecting volume, this prevents waste.

**Clean Interface:** The dashboard is minimal and functional. There is no bloatware, no overwhelming feature set — you log in, search, and export. For teams that just want emails without the learning curve of a full CRM platform, this simplicity is genuinely refreshing.

## Where Skrapp.io Falls Short in 2026

The problems become apparent when you benchmark Skrapp.io against what modern B2B sales teams actually need:

### Accuracy Concerns

Skrapp.io's marketing materials reference a 92% success rate, but independent benchmarks paint a different picture. Real-world tests in 2026 report verified deliverable rates between **42.8% and 75%**, depending on the industry and company size. For niche verticals, smaller companies, or markets outside North America, the accuracy drops further.

The root cause is architectural: Skrapp.io relies primarily on its own internal database and web crawling algorithms rather than using a **waterfall enrichment approach** that queries multiple data providers sequentially. This single-source dependency means that when Skrapp's database does not have a match, you get no result — or worse, an outdated one.

### No Phone Numbers or Mobile Data

In 2026, multi-channel outbound is standard. SDRs combine emails with cold calls, LinkedIn DMs, and even WhatsApp messages. Skrapp.io provides zero phone data. No direct dials, no mobile numbers, no switchboard numbers. Teams using Skrapp need a separate tool like Lusha or Cognism just for phone data, which adds cost and integration complexity.

### No Intent Signals

Skrapp.io gives you contact data, but it tells you nothing about whether that contact is currently in-market. There are no funding round triggers, no hiring signals, no technology adoption alerts, and no social intent indicators. You are essentially cold-prospecting blind.

### No Built-In Sequencing

After extracting emails, you need to export them to a separate tool — Lemlist, Instantly, Smartlead, or your CRM — to actually send outreach. This creates a fragmented workflow where data lives in multiple systems, making attribution and follow-up tracking harder to manage.

### Pricing Gets Expensive at Scale

The Starter plan costs $49/month for 1,000 credits ($0.049 per email). That sounds affordable until you factor in the accuracy problem. If only 65% of those emails are actually deliverable, your effective cost per usable email jumps to roughly $0.075. At 5,000 credits per month on the Seeker plan ($99/month), teams running high-volume outbound will find the cost-per-contact competitive with more fully-featured platforms that also include phone data and sequencing.

## Who Should Use Skrapp.io?

Skrapp.io makes sense for a narrow use case: **solo freelancers, recruiters, or early-stage founders** who prospect exclusively on LinkedIn, need fewer than 1,000 emails per month, and have no need for phone data, intent signals, or automated sequencing. If you fit that profile, it is a clean, affordable, no-frills tool.
