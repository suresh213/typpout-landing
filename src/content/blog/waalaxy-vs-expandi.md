Waalaxy and Expandi are two modern cloud-based automation platforms designed to help sales teams run LinkedIn and email outreach campaigns.

## Waalaxy: Ease of Use

Waalaxy is famous for its simple, gamified user interface and its Chrome extension that makes importing contacts easy. It is popular among founders and marketers who want a plug-and-play tool.

## Expandi: Enterprise Safety

Expandi is built for scale and security. It offers dedicated IP proxies, advanced smart sequences, and deep integrations with CRMs and webhook providers. It is preferred by agencies and larger sales teams.
