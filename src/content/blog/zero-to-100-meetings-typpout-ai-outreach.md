## How Typpout’s AI Outreach Actually Works: A Step-by-Step Breakdown

### 1. **Real-Time Social Listening: Turning Signals Into Intelligence**
Typpout doesn’t just scrape LinkedIn profiles—it **listens across the entire digital footprint** of your ICP.

- **Sources Monitored**: LinkedIn posts, Twitter/X threads, GitHub activity (for dev tools), Slack communities, Reddit AMAs, and even newsletter mentions.
- **Signal Detection**: Uses NLP to identify intent signals like:
  - “We’re evaluating a new CRM”
  - “Frustrated with [Competitor]”
  - “Hiring for sales ops”
  - “Just launched our Series B”
- **Scoring Engine**: Each signal gets a **real-time intent score** (1-100), based on recency, relevance, and authority of the poster.

> 🔍 *Example*: A CRO posts, “Our outbound isn’t scaling. Anyone using AI for SDR automation?” That gets a **92 intent score**. Typpout flags it immediately.

👉 *Result*: You’re not guessing—you’re **engaging when the prospect is actively talking about your solution**.

---

### 2. **Predictive Personalization: The AI That Writes Your Message**
Once a signal is detected, Typpout doesn’t send a generic email. It **generates a hyper-personalized message** in real time using:

- **Contextual Data**: The signal source (e.g., LinkedIn post), job title, company size, tech stack, recent funding.
- **LLM-Powered Copywriting**: Our fine-tuned LLM analyzes the signal and crafts a message that:
  - Acknowledges the signal (“Love your post on outbound scaling—many of our customers faced the same issue”)
  - Connects to your solution (“We helped [SimilarCompany] 2x their meetings in 90 days with AI-driven SDR”)
  - Ends with a **low-pressure CTA** (“Want to see how we did it?”)

> ✨ *Personalization at Scale*: Each message is **unique**, but generated in seconds. No more writer’s block or template fatigue.

---

### 3. **Automated Outreach: Omni-Channel, Multi-Touch**
Typpout doesn’t just send one email. It orchestrates a **multi-channel sequence** based on prospect behavior:

| Channel | Trigger | When It’s Used |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| **LinkedIn DM** | Detected signal | High-intent signals (e.g., “hiring SDRs”) |
| **Cold Email** | No reply in 24h | Default fallback |
| **Twitter/X Reply** | Public tweet | If prospect is active on X |
| **Follow-Up Email** | New signal detected | E.g., “Saw your company raised funding—congrats!” |
| **Meeting Booking** | Positive reply | Auto-trigger Calendly or Chili Piper |

> 📊 *Data-Driven Channels*: Typpout adapts based on **reply rates by channel** for your ICP. For tech founders, Twitter replies often outperform email.

---

### 4. **AI Reply Handling: Turning Conversations Into Meetings**
Here’s where most tools fail: **they don’t respond fast enough**.

Typpout uses **real-time NLP** to:
- Detect **intent in replies** (e.g., “tell me more”, “schedule a call”)
- Auto-reply with **contextual answers** or **next-step CTAs**
- Escalate **qualified leads** to your team (Slack/Teams integration)

> 🚀 *Example Autoresponse*:
> **Prospect**: “How does your pricing work?”
> **Typpout AI**: “Great question! We have 3 tiers based on team size. Here’s a [link to a case study] from [SimilarCompany]. Want to jump on a 15-min call to see if it fits your stack?”

**Result**: 3x faster response time → **higher meeting conversion**.

---

### 5. **Meeting Booking: Closing the Loop**
Once a prospect shows interest, Typpout **auto-books a meeting** via:
- Built-in Calendly/Chili Piper integration
- **Time-slot optimization** based on prospect availability
- **Confirmation + reminder sequence** (email + SMS)

> 📅 *Under the Hood*: Typpout syncs with your calendar in real time to avoid double-booking and respect DST/holidays.
