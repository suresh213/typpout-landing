For sales reps doing manual prospecting on LinkedIn, Chrome extensions like Kaspr and Lusha are essential tools. Both allow you to reveal phone numbers and email addresses instantly, but they differ in features and pricing.

## Lusha: The Market Leader

Lusha is an established platform with a large database and high phone number accuracy. It is highly reliable and integrates with all major CRMs, but can be expensive for small teams.

## Kaspr: Value and Social Focus

Kaspr is a newer alternative that offers a highly competitive free tier and features like automated LinkedIn workflows. It is popular among startup founders and solo SDRs looking for budget-friendly tools.
