# Typpout vs. Expandi: Which AI Sales Automation Tool Should You Choose in 2026?

The B2B sales landscape in 2026 is more competitive than ever. Buyers are inundated with generic outreach, while sales teams struggle to cut through the noise with hyper-personalized, timely engagement. The rise of AI-driven sales automation tools has been a game-changer, but choosing the right platform is critical to scaling your outbound efforts effectively.

Two names dominate the conversation in AI sales automation: **Typpout** and **Expandi**. Both promise to revolutionize your sales workflow, but they take fundamentally different approaches. One focuses on real-time social listening and AI-driven outreach, while the other leans into automation sequences and multichannel campaigns.

In this comprehensive comparison, we’ll break down the strengths, weaknesses, and ideal use cases for Typpout and Expandi. By the end, you’ll know exactly which tool (or combination) aligns with your **GTM strategy, team size, and revenue goals** in 2026.

## Why AI Sales Automation Matters in 2026

Before diving into the comparison, let’s ground this in the **core challenges** plaguing B2B sales teams today:

1. **Information Overload**: Buyers receive hundreds of messages daily. Generic emails or LinkedIn messages are ignored.
2. **Timing is Everything**: The best outreach is **contextual and timely**. AI tools must react to real-time signals (e.g., a company raising funding, hiring a new executive, or launching a product).
3. **Scalability vs. Personalization**: Teams want to scale outreach without sacrificing relevance.
4. **Data-Driven Decisions**: Sales leaders need **actionable insights** to refine messaging and targeting.

This is where **Typpout and Expandi** come in—but they solve these problems in distinct ways.

## Expandi: Multichannel Automation with a Focus on Sequences

Expandi is a **LinkedIn automation tool** that has expanded into multichannel outreach (email, Twitter, etc.). It’s built for teams that want **scalable sequences** with a focus on **LinkedIn automation**.

### **Key Features of Expandi**

| Feature | Description | Why It Matters |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| **LinkedIn & Multichannel Sequences** | Automates LinkedIn connection requests, follow-ups, and email drip campaigns. | Good for **high-volume outreach**. |
| **Personalization Tokens** | Inserts merge fields (e.g., first name, company) into messages. | Basic personalization. |
| **Analytics Dashboard** | Tracks open rates, reply rates, and sequence performance. | Helps refine messaging. |
| **A/B Testing** | Tests different message variations within sequences. | Optimizes conversion rates. |
| **Safety Features** | Limits actions to stay within LinkedIn’s automation policies. | Reduces account risks. |

### **Who Should Use Expandi?**
- **SDRs and BDRs** who need **automated LinkedIn outreach** at scale.
- **Teams with large prospect lists** that prioritize **sequence-based automation**.
- **Sales teams that rely heavily on LinkedIn** for outreach.

### **Limitations of Expandi**
- **No real-time signals**: Outreach is **time-based**, not event-based.
- **Basic personalization**: Relies on merge fields rather than **contextual triggers**.
- **Limited data enrichment**: Doesn’t provide **deep intent or technographic data** like Typpout.

### **Example Workflow with Expandi**
1. **Upload List**: Import a CSV of prospects.
2. **Create Sequence**: Set up a LinkedIn connection request → follow-up message → email drip.
3. **Run Campaign**: Expandi automates the outreach.
4. **Track Performance**: Monitor replies and adjust sequences.

**Result**: Good for **volume**, but less effective for **high-intent, real-time outreach**.
