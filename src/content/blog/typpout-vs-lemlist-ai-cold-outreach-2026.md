## 🧠 How AI Messaging Works: Real-Time vs. Template-Driven

Here’s where **Typpout and Lemlist diverge most**.

### Lemlist: AI-Powered, But Still Template-Heavy
Lemlist uses **AI to enhance templates** — generating variations of your messages based on inputs like company size, role, and past interactions.

✅ Pros:
- Fast setup with AI-generated subject lines and body copy.
- Great for teams that want control over messaging tone.

❌ Cons:
- **No real-time context** — messages are based on static data or past interactions.
- Personalization is limited to merge tags (e.g., `{first_name}`).
- Lower reply rates in competitive niches because messages feel recycled.

> Example: Lemlist might say, “Hi {first_name}, great to connect — saw {company} is hiring in sales. Interested in learning more?”

But in 2026, buyers expect **something fresher**.

### Typpout: AI Meets Real-Time Social Listening
Typpout doesn’t just generate messages — it **listens** before it speaks.

🔧 How it works:
1. **Scans LinkedIn, Twitter, and company websites** daily.
2. Detects **intent signals** (e.g., funding round, product launch, hiring).
3. **Crafts tailored messages** based on real-time events — not just demographic data.
4. **Sends via email, LinkedIn, or Twitter** depending on the prospect’s activity.

> Example: Typpout detects that a CFO at a Series C fintech just announced a new payment feature. It crafts a message like:
>
> *“Hi [Name], saw you launched [Feature X] — we helped [Similar Company] cut payment processing time by 40%. Wondering if timing is right to explore? Happy to share a quick case study.”*

💡 Result: **3x higher reply rates** in industries like fintech and SaaS.
