Running cold outreach campaigns requires strict adherence to international data privacy laws. Violating these regulations can result in heavy fines and permanent damage to your brand.

## Key Regulations to Watch

1. **CAN-SPAM Act (US):** Requires you to include a physical address in emails, provide a clear opt-out link, and avoid deceptive subject lines.
2. **GDPR (EU):** Requires a "legitimate interest" to contact EU citizens, the ability to delete prospect data upon request, and compliant data enrichment.
3. **CCPA (California):** Grants California residents the right to opt-out of the sale of their personal data.
