Woodpecker and Lemlist are two pioneers in the sales engagement space. Both tools are highly regarded, but they target different styles of outreach.

## Woodpecker: Reliable Automation

Woodpecker is designed for simple, reliable cold email sequences. It has a strong focus on deliverability, automatically adjusting send times to mimic human behavior and protect your domain reputation.

## Lemlist: Advanced Personalization

Lemlist became famous for its custom image and video personalization features. It allows you to dynamically insert the prospect's name, logo, or website screenshot into images and landing pages, resulting in highly engaging emails.

## Sourcing Intent First

Both tools require you to source your own leads. [Typpout](/) eliminates the sourcing bottleneck by finding leads who are currently expressing buying intent on Reddit, X, and LinkedIn. It then drafts custom outreach based on their specific social post.
