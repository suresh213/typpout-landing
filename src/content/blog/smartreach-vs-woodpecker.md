Smartreach and Woodpecker are highly regarded for cold email deliverability. Both tools help reps manage multiple domains and protect their sending reputation.

## Smartreach: Custom Settings

Smartreach offers granular control over deliverability, inbox rotation, and CRM mapping, making it ideal for teams with complex RevOps setups.

## Woodpecker: Automated Management

Woodpecker features an automated engine that adapts send times to mimic human patterns and protect domain reputation with minimal manual setup.
