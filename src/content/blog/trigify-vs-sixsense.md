Intent data is one of the biggest trends in B2B sales. Trigify.io and 6sense are two tools that help sales teams leverage intent, but they operate at different scales.

## 6sense: Enterprise Account Intelligence

6sense is an enterprise platform that captures anonymous website visits and searches to identify when *accounts* are in-market. It is excellent for account-based marketing (ABM) and planning large sales campaigns, but lacks contact-level social sourcing.

## Trigify.io: Signal-Based Prospecting

Trigify.io focuses on individual signals, such as when a person comments on a specific LinkedIn post or changes jobs. It is much more tactical and actionable for individual SDRs.
