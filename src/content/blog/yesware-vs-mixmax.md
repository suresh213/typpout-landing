Yesware and Mixmax are two top email productivity add-ons for Gmail. Both help reps track opens, insert templates, and schedule meetings.

## Yesware: Simple Tracking

Yesware is a lightweight extension focused on email tracking, custom templates, and basic mail merges. It integrates directly with Salesforce, making it a favorite for enterprise reps.

## Mixmax: Rich Media and Automation

Mixmax offers more advanced features, including interactive email polls, custom landing pages, and complex sequence automation. It is ideal for teams wanting highly engaging emails.
