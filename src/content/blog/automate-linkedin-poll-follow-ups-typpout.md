## **Step 1: Set Up Poll Listening with Typpout**

Before automation, you need a way to **detect poll responses in real-time**. Typpout’s **Social Listening** feature does exactly that.

### **How to Configure Poll Tracking**
1. **Connect Your LinkedIn Account**
   - Go to Typpout’s [Dashboard](/) → **Integrations** → **LinkedIn**
   - Authorize Typpout to access your profile (read-only permissions)

2. **Set Up Poll Triggers**
   - Navigate to **Automation → Poll Follow-Ups**
   - Click **"Add New Poll"**
   - Paste the LinkedIn poll URL or search by keyword (e.g., "AI tools," "SaaS pricing")

3. **Define Your Audience**
   - Filter respondents by:
     - Job title (e.g., "Founder," "Head of Sales")
     - Industry (e.g., "Tech," "Finance")
     - Location (e.g., "US, Canada")

> ✅ **Pro Tip:** Target polls with **high engagement** (500+ votes) for maximum reach.

## **Step 2: Automate Follow-Ups with AI-Powered Personalization**

Once a prospect engages with your poll, Typpout **instantly triggers a follow-up sequence** based on their response.

## **Step 3: Qualify Leads and Book Meetings Automatically**

Not all poll respondents are qualified. Typpout’s **data waterfall** filters leads *before* sending follow-ups.

### **How Typpout Qualifies Leads**
1. **Real-Time LinkedIn Data**
   - Job title, company size, industry
   - Recent posts, skills, and interests

2. **Behavioral Triggers**
   - Did they click your poll link?
   - Did they engage with similar content before?

3. **AI Scoring**
   - Assigns a **lead score** (1-100) based on fit and intent
   - Only high-scoring leads get meeting bookings

### **Meeting Booking Workflow**
- Typpout sends a **Calendly/HubSpot Meetings** link in the first follow-up
- If the prospect books, Typpout **syncs the meeting** to your CRM
- If they don’t respond, Typpout **retries with a different angle**

> 📊 **Result:** **50% faster qualification** and **30% higher meeting conversion** vs. manual outreach.
