### 1. Real-Time Social Listening: Catch the Signal Before It Fades

#### **What It Solves:**
- Missed intent signals in noisy social feeds.
- Delayed responses that kill conversion.
- Manual monitoring that doesn’t scale.

#### **How Typpout Does It:**

We integrate with **Twitter (X), LinkedIn, Reddit, and niche forums** to monitor keywords like:
- “Need help with outbound”
- “GTM automation tools”
- “Sales engagement platform”
- “Founder struggling with scaling”

When a prospect mentions a keyword **within the last 48 hours**, Typpout flags it as a **high-intent lead.**

📌 **Pro Tip:** Filter for **founders, VPs of Sales, or RevOps leaders**—roles most likely to buy GTM tools.

| Criteria | Weight | Example |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Keyword match | 30% | “outbound automation” |
| Role relevance | 25% | Founder, CRO, Head of Sales |
| Timeframe | 20% | Posted in last 24 hours |
| Engagement level | 15% | Replies from peers |
| Company size | 10% | 50+ employees |

> ✅ **Result:** A prioritized list of **hot leads**, updated in real time.

### 2. AI-Powered Intent Qualification: Separate Signals from Noise

Not every mention is a qualified lead.

Some are complaints. Some are jokes. Some are aspirational.

Typpout uses **NLP (Natural Language Processing)** to analyze sentiment, context, and intent—**scoring each lead on conversion likelihood.**

#### **Qualification Filters:**

| Filter | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Problem Acknowledgment** | Prospect admits a struggle | “Our outbound is broken” |
| **Solution Seek** | Open to tools or advice | “Anyone using outbound automation?” |
| **Authority** | Decision-maker role | Founder, Head of Revenue |
| **Recency** | Posted in last 48 hours | High urgency |
| **Engagement** | Has replies from peers | Social proof |

> 🔥 **Result:** Only **3–5% of social mentions** become high-intent leads—but they convert at **3–5x higher rates** than cold outreach.

### 3. Hyper-Personalized Outreach: From Generic to Genuine

Once a lead is qualified, Typpout **auto-generates a personalized message** based on:
- Their exact pain point
- Their recent posts and replies
- Their company and role

#### **Example Workflow:**

**Lead Tweet:**
> “Our outbound is a mess. We’re using HubSpot but it’s not helping us scale. Anyone using something better?”

**Typpout Outreach:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Saw your tweet about struggling with HubSpot + outbound scaling. We went through the same pain at [Company]—switched to Typpout and increased reply rates by 300%.
>
> Would love to show you how. No pitch—just a quick 10-min chat?
>
> Book here: [Calendly link]

#### **Why This Works:**
- **Relevant:** Directly references their tool and problem.
- **Social Proof:** Shares a result (300% increase).
- **Low Pressure:** Asks for a short meeting, not a demo.

> ✅ **Typpout Data:** Leads who receive **contextual, timely outreach** reply **3x more** than cold emails.

### 4. AI-Driven Reply Handling & Meeting Booking

Once you send the message, **Typpout doesn’t stop.**

We monitor replies in real time and **auto-book meetings** using AI-powered conversation handling.

#### **How It Works:**

1. Prospect replies: “Tell me more.”
2. Typpout sends a **calendly link** instantly.
3. If they ask a question, Typpout uses **pre-built response trees** to answer instantly.
4. If they object, Typpout routes to your team for follow-up.

> 🤖 **AI Handles 60% of initial replies**—freeing your team to focus on closing.

#### **Response Rate Comparison:**
