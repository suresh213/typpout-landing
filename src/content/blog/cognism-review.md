Cognism has positioned itself as the compliance-first alternative to American-centric data platforms like ZoomInfo and Apollo. With phone-verified "Diamond Data," integrated Bombora intent signals, and a GDPR compliance framework built from the ground up, Cognism is the preferred choice for enterprise teams selling into European markets. But compliance and data quality come at a premium price — and the platform has limitations that are worth understanding before committing to an annual contract.

## What Makes Cognism Different

**Diamond Data (Phone-Verified Numbers):** Cognism's signature feature. The Diamond Data team manually calls phone numbers to verify they reach the intended contact before adding them to the database. This human verification layer delivers mobile accuracy rates above 80% — compared to 30-50% from algorithmically sourced competitors. For SDR teams that rely on cold calling, this accuracy difference directly translates into more daily conversations.

**GDPR Compliance Framework:** Cognism was designed for European data regulations from day one. The platform maintains a comprehensive Do-Not-Call (DNC) database covering UK, Germany, France, and other EU markets. Every contact includes compliance metadata, and the platform automatically suppresses contacts registered on national DNC lists. For companies with European customers, this compliance layer reduces legal risk significantly.

**Bombora Intent Data:** Cognism integrates Bombora's B2B intent data to identify accounts that are actively researching topics relevant to your product category. This account-level intent data helps prioritize outreach to companies showing buying signals — a meaningful advantage over pure contact data tools.

**Chrome Extension:** The browser extension works on LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, and company websites, revealing contact data including Diamond-verified phone numbers directly on the page.

## Where Cognism Falls Short

### Pricing Opacity and Contract Requirements

Like ZoomInfo, Cognism does not publish pricing on its website. Annual contracts are standard, with estimated starting prices around $1,000-1,500/month for small teams. This pricing excludes bootstrapped startups and solo founders who need data on flexible monthly terms.

### North American Coverage Gaps

Cognism's primary market is EMEA. While the platform has expanded its North American database, users consistently report that US and Canadian contact coverage — particularly for mid-market tech companies — lags behind Apollo.io and ZoomInfo. For US-only sales teams, Cognism may not justify the premium over cheaper, more US-focused alternatives.

### Diamond Data Volume Limits

Phone-verified Diamond Data is available for a subset of Cognism's total database. Not every contact has a Diamond-verified number. The volume of Diamond Data available for your specific ICP (industry, geography, seniority level) may be smaller than expected — particularly for niche industries or emerging markets.

### No Built-In Sequencing

Cognism provides data and intent signals but does not include a native email sequencer or calling platform. You need to integrate with tools like Outreach, Salesloft, or HubSpot for campaign execution — adding cost and complexity to the workflow.

## Who Should Use Cognism

Cognism delivers clear value for **mid-market and enterprise teams** that meet these criteria:
- Primary or significant revenue comes from European markets.
- Legal and compliance teams require vendor DPA documentation and DNC list adherence.
- Cold calling is a primary outbound channel where phone accuracy directly impacts SDR productivity.
- Annual tool budgets support $15,000-40,000/year for data subscriptions.
