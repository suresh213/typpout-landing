ZoomInfo and Lusha sit at opposite ends of the B2B sales intelligence spectrum. ZoomInfo is the enterprise-grade, do-everything platform with deep firmographics, intent data, and conversation intelligence. Lusha is the lightweight, get-started-in-5-minutes contact finder with a clean Chrome extension and accessible pricing. This comparison helps you determine which end of the spectrum fits your team's reality.

## Scale and Depth Comparison

| Dimension | ZoomInfo | Lusha |
|---|---|---|
| **Database size** | Largest in market (undisclosed, estimated 100M+ verified contacts) | 100M+ business profiles |
| **Email coverage** | Strong across all segments | Strong for LinkedIn-sourced contacts |
| **Phone data** | Direct dials + mobile | Direct dials + mobile (higher accuracy for mobile) |
| **Firmographics** | Deep — org charts, revenue, employee count, technographics | Basic — company size, industry |
| **Intent data** | ✅ Sophisticated web-based intent signals | ❌ Not available |
| **Org charts** | ✅ Reporting structure mapping | ❌ Not available |
| **Conversation intelligence** | ✅ Chorus.ai included on Elite plans | ❌ Not available |
| **Sequencing** | ✅ Engage platform included | ❌ Not available |

ZoomInfo provides a complete intelligence layer. Lusha provides contact data with minimal overhead.

## Pricing Accessibility

This is the most dramatic difference:

| Metric | ZoomInfo | Lusha |
|---|---|---|
| Published pricing | ❌ Hidden behind demo | ✅ Transparent on website |
| Free plan | ❌ No | ✅ 50 credits/month |
| Entry price | ~$15,000/year (estimated) | $49/month |
| Contract requirement | Annual (12+ months) | Monthly or annual |
| Per-user cost | $1,500-3,000/year additional | $49-79/user/month |

Lusha's total annual cost for a small team equals roughly one month of a ZoomInfo contract. For startups and SMBs, this accessibility gap is the deciding factor.

## Phone Data Quality

Both platforms provide phone numbers, but with different quality approaches:

**ZoomInfo** aggregates phone data from multiple sources including public directories, partnerships, and web crawling. Phone number accuracy is generally good for enterprise contacts but varies for smaller companies.

**Lusha** has built a reputation specifically on phone data quality. The platform uses community-verified data and proprietary sourcing methods that deliver higher mobile number accuracy for individual contributors and mid-level managers — the people SDRs actually need to reach.

For teams where cold calling is a primary outbound channel, Lusha's phone data quality often matches or exceeds ZoomInfo's at a fraction of the cost.

## When to Choose Each Tool

**ZoomInfo is justified when:**
- Your organization has 20+ salespeople and a dedicated RevOps team.
- You need intent data to prioritize target accounts.
- Mapping buying committees and org structures is essential to your sales process.
- Your annual tools budget exceeds $30,000.
- Enterprise deal values justify the data investment.

**Lusha is the right choice when:**
- Your team has fewer than 10 salespeople.
- You need quick access to contact data from LinkedIn.
- Phone outreach is a primary pipeline channel.
- Your budget is under $500/month for data tools.
- You want to start using the tool today without a multi-week onboarding process.
