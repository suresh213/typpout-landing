Leadfeeder (now Dealfront) is a popular tool that identifies which companies are visiting your website. It is a great way to discover hidden interest, but it has several limitations.

## Sourcing Intent with Typpout

[Typpout](/) focuses on active social intent. Instead of tracking anonymous office IPs, Typpout finds the exact individual who is posting about their needs on LinkedIn, X, or Reddit. This gives your reps the exact contact and a warm reason to reach out.

**Verdict:** Use Leadfeeder for general website traffic analytics. Choose [Typpout](/pricing) to find and pitch the actual buyers expressing intent on social media.
