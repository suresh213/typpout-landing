## Step 3: Engage—But Not Like a Salesperson

The biggest mistake on X (Twitter)? Treating it like a sales channel.

**Instead: Be helpful first.**

### ✅ Do:
- Reply to the **tweet** (not DM) with a **valuable insight** (e.g., “We helped @CompanyX cut meeting bookings by 40%. Happy to share how.”)
- Use **personalization** (mention their name, company, or specific pain point)
- Include a **low-friction CTA** (e.g., “Want a quick demo? DM me ‘suresh’.”)

### ❌ Don’t:
- Pitch in the first reply
- Use a generic “Let’s connect!”
- Send a cold DM without context

✨ **AI-Powered Engagement Tip**:
Use a **pre-built response library** triggered by intent signals. For example:
- If they mention “calendar sync”, reply with: *“Hi [Name], we helped [Similar Company] fix that with AI-powered scheduling. Happy to share a quick tip—no pitch, just value.”*
