## What Is Social Intent Data—and Why It Matters

Social intent data refers to **real-time signals** gathered from public social and professional networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub, Reddit) that indicate a company or individual is researching, evaluating, or preparing to act on a business need.

### Types of Social Intent Signals

| Signal Type | Example | Expansion Indicator |
|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Job Postings** | Hiring for IT, RevOps, or Product roles | Scaling company, likely needs more tools |
| **Content Engagement** | Reading pricing pages, case studies, or blog posts | Evaluating upgrade or new use case |
| **Funding Announcements** | Series B or C funding | Increased budget, expansion readiness |
| **Technology Stack Changes** | New tech hires or open-source contributions | Evaluating integrations or alternatives |
| **Leadership Shifts** | New CRO or CTO | New priorities, potential expansion trigger |

These signals are **public, real-time, and external**—meaning they exist outside your CRM but reveal internal intent.

> 🔥 **Pro Tip**: Social intent data doesn’t replace traditional signals—it **complements** them. The magic happens when you combine CRM data (e.g., usage trends) with social intent (e.g., job postings + pricing page views).

## How to Use Social Intent Data to Identify Expansion Revenue

### Step 1: Build Your Intent Data Waterfall

Not all intent is created equal. Use a **tiered scoring model** to prioritize accounts:

| Score Tier | Criteria | Action |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| **Tier 1 (High Intent)** | 2+ strong signals + high usage + funding announcement | Immediate outreach by AE |
| **Tier 2 (Medium Intent)** | 1 signal + moderate usage | Nurture via automated email + CSM touchpoint |
| **Tier 3 (Low Intent)** | Single weak signal | Monitor, no direct action |
| **Tier 0 (No Intent)** | No signals | Focus on retention/health scoring |

> ✅ **Example**: A customer posts a job for a *Head of Revenue Operations* and visits your pricing page twice in one week. **High intent.** Schedule a call.
