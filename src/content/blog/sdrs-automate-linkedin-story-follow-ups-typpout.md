Typpout’s AI GTM engine doesn’t just automate—it **orchestrates** engagement at scale with real-time intelligence.

Typpout’s **AI-powered waterfall** monitors LinkedIn Stories (and other social signals) from your ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) in real time.

- **Data Sources:** LinkedIn Stories, posts, comments, shares, and even LinkedIn Live streams.
- **Smart Filtering:** Only tracks Stories from prospects matching your ICP (job title, industry, company size, etc.).

### Step 2: AI-Powered Personalization at Scale
Forget generic "Hey [Name], saw your post!" messages.

Typpout’s AI analyzes the **content of the Story** and auto-generates **hyper-personalized follow-ups** based on:

- **Topic:** If the Story is about AI adoption, Typpout crafts a message referencing AI trends.
- **Sentiment:** If the Story is celebratory (e.g., "Just launched X!"), Typpout mirrors the tone.
- **Timing:** Messages are sent within **seconds** of the Story posting.

> **Story:** "Excited to share we’re doubling our team! #Growth"

### Step 3: Automated Reply Handling & Meeting Booking
Typpout doesn’t just send messages—it **converses**.

- **Smart Reply Detection:** If a prospect replies, Typpout routes the conversation to your SDR in real time.
- **AI-Assisted Responses:** Typpout suggests replies based on conversation history (e.g., "Can you share more about your pain points?").
- **Meeting Booking:** Prospect clicks a Calendly link, and Typpout **automatically updates your CRM** (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.).

> 🚀 **Result:** SDRs respond to **high-intent Story engagements in under 30 seconds**—while low-value tasks run on autopilot.
