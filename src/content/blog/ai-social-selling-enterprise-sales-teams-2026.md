## The 5-Step AI Social Selling Framework for Enterprise Teams

### **Step 1: Data Waterfalls – The New Foundation of Social Selling**
In 2026, **static CRM data is dead**. The most advanced teams use **data waterfalls**—a continuous flow of signals from:
- **Social listening** (mentions, shares, comments)
- **Engagement metrics** (time spent, scroll depth, link clicks)
- **Intent data** (job changes, funding rounds, tech stack updates)
- **AI-driven scoring** (predictive intent models)

**Actionable Setup:**
1. **Integrate your social channels** (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Slack communities) with a real-time data pipeline.
2. **Score accounts** based on AI-predicted intent (e.g., a CFO posting about "cost optimization" = high priority).
3. **Feed insights into your CRM** (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) for SDRs to act on.

- **Shared connections** (e.g., "Saw you’re now at [Company]—we helped [Similar Company] with [Solution]")

**How to Execute:**
- Use **AI content generators** (like Typpout’s AI composer) to craft **context-aware messages** in seconds.

### **Step 4: AI-Optimized Outreach Timing & Channel Selection**
Not all social channels perform equally. In 2026, AI helps you **predict the best time, place, and message** for outreach.

**Key AI-Driven Insights:**
- **LinkedIn:** Best for B2B executives (8 AM–10 AM local time).
- **Twitter/X:** Ideal for tech/startup audiences (lunchtime engagement spikes).
- **Slack Communities:** High-intent for niche industries (e.g., DevOps, FinTech).
- **Cold Email:** AI optimizes subject lines and send times based on historical open rates.

**Automation Setup:**
1. **AI analyzes past engagement patterns** to recommend optimal send times.
2. **Auto-distributes messages** across channels based on buyer preferences.
3. **Tracks response rates** and adjusts strategy in real time.
