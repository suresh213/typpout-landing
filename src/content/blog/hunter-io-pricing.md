Hunter.io's pricing is straightforward compared to many competitors — no per-user fees, no hidden credit types, and no opaque enterprise quoting. But the simplicity masks a volume problem: Hunter's credit allocations are significantly smaller than alternatives at the same price points, which matters for teams doing high-volume prospecting.

## Hunter.io Pricing Plans (June 2026)

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Searches | Verifications | Connected Accounts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 25 | 50 | 1 |
| Starter | $49 | $34 | 500 | 1,000 | 3 |
| Growth | $149 | $104 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 10 |
| Business | $399 | $279 | 50,000 | 100,000 | 20 |

**Annual billing saves 30%**, which is a significant discount. If you are committing to Hunter for a full year, annual plans offer substantially better unit economics.

### Search Credits vs. Verification Credits

Hunter separates two credit types:

**Search credits** are consumed when you use Domain Search (finding emails for a company) or Email Finder (finding a specific person's email). Each company domain search or individual lookup costs one search credit.

**Verification credits** are consumed when you verify whether an email address is deliverable. You can verify emails found through Hunter or upload external lists for verification. Each individual email check costs one verification credit.

This separation means a team that finds emails through other tools (LinkedIn scraping, Apollo exports, conference attendee lists) can use Hunter purely as a verification engine — paying only for verification credits while ignoring search limits.

## What Hunter.io Pricing Includes

Every paid plan includes:
- **Email campaigns** — send cold email sequences directly from Hunter with open/click tracking.
- **API access** — integrate Hunter into custom workflows, CRMs, and automation tools.
- **Domain search** — find all known email patterns for a company domain.
- **Bulk tasks** — upload CSV files for batch email finding and verification.
- **Team access** — shared credit pool across connected team members (no per-user fee).

The absence of per-user pricing is a genuine advantage. A 5-person team on the Growth plan pays $149/month total, compared to $245-395/month for the same team on Apollo.

## What Pricing Does NOT Include

- Phone numbers (need Lusha or Cognism: $49-1,000+/month)
- Buying intent data (need Bombora or 6sense: enterprise pricing)
- Advanced sequencing (need Instantly or Lemlist: $37-59/month)
- AI personalization (need ChatGPT API or Lavender: $29+/month)

The supplementary tool cost for a complete outbound stack using Hunter as the data layer typically runs $100-200/month on top of the Hunter subscription.
