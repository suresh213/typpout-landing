Clearbit has long been a gold standard for B2B data enrichment, helping marketing and sales teams turn anonymous IP addresses into company profiles and enrich lead forms in real time.

## Why Teams Look for Clearbit Alternatives

* **Enterprise Pricing:** Clearbit's pricing is opaque and often prohibitive for early-stage and mid-market companies.
* **API Complexity:** Implementing and maintaining custom data enrichment logic requires engineering resources.
* **Acquisition Changes:** Following its acquisition by HubSpot, some stand-alone customers are exploring other platforms.
