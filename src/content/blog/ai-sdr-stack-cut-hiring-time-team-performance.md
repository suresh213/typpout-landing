## How to Deploy an AI SDR Stack (In 5 Steps)

You don’t need a full revamp. Start small, measure, and scale.

### Step 1: Audit Your Current GTM Funnel
Map every touchpoint where leads enter and where they stall.

- **Where do leads come from?** (Website, LinkedIn, ads, referrals?)
- **How long do they stay in nurture?**
- **What % convert to meetings?**

Use tools like **HubSpot, Salesforce, or Segment** to log lead velocity and conversion rates.

> 📌 Tip: If your SDRs are spending >40% of their time on manual prospecting or follow-ups, you have a **process gap**—not a people problem.

### Step 2: Integrate Real-Time Intent Data
The fastest way to cut hiring time? **Stop waiting for leads to show up.**

- **Typpout AI GTM** monitors LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit, and tech forums in real time.
- It alerts you when someone mentions a pain point your product solves.
- You reach out **within minutes**, not days.

> Example: A prospect posts on LinkedIn: “Frustrated with CRM data cleanup.”
> Typpout flags the post, crafts a personalized message, and schedules a follow-up.
> **Result**: 3x higher reply rate than cold outreach.

### Step 3: Automate 70% of Your Outreach
Use AI to draft, personalize, and send multi-channel sequences.

- **Typpout AI** generates emails, LinkedIn messages, and even **Loom-style video intros** based on prospect data.
- It dynamically inserts pain points, company news, or trigger events.
- No more writer’s block. No more waiting for “the right moment.”

> ✅ Pro Tip: Use AI to **A/B test messaging at scale**. Let the algorithm optimize what resonates.

### Step 4: Let AI Handle the First 3–5 Touches
Your human SDRs should only engage when:
- A prospect shows strong intent (demo request, pricing question)
- A deal is about to close
- A competitor is mentioned

> ❌ Don’t waste time on:
> - “Hi [Name], just checking in…”
> - Generic follow-ups
> - Objection handling 101

> ✅ Do this instead:
> - AI qualifies leads with a quick chatbot
> - AI books the meeting
> - SDR joins only for final qualification and demo

### Step 5: Measure, Optimize, Scale
Track these KPIs weekly:

| KPI | Target | Why It Matters |
|-----|--------|----------------|
| **Meetings Booked / Week** | +50% | Direct pipeline impact |
| **Reply Rate** | >30% | Engagement quality |
| **Time to First Response** | <24 hrs | Speed kills |
| **Cost per Meeting** | <$50 | Efficiency gains |
| **SDR Ramp Time** | <7 days | Reduced hiring friction |

> 💡 **Scaling Tip**: Once you hit 20+ meetings/month with AI, start hiring **hybrid SDRs**: reps who focus on closing, not prospecting.
