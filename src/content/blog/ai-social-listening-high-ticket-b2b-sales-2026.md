## Overcoming Common Pitfalls in AI Social Listening

While AI social listening is powerful, teams often stumble on these mistakes:

### **Pitfall 1: Over-Reliance on Automation**
**Problem**: Sending generic, AI-generated messages without human oversight.
**Solution**: Use AI to **augment**, not replace, human touch. Always have sales reps review high-priority prospects.

### **Pitfall 2: Ignoring Privacy & Compliance**
**Problem**: Scraping social data without consent (GDPR, CCPA risks).
**Solution**: Use tools like Typpout that **comply with data privacy laws** and only track publicly available signals.

### **Pitfall 3: Focusing on Volume Over Quality**
**Problem**: Blasting messages to 1,000 prospects hoping for a 1% response.
**Solution**: Prioritize **high-intent signals** and **personalized engagement**.
