## The 5-Step AI-Powered Social Selling Framework (2026 Edition)

This isn’t just theory. It’s a **battle-tested system** used by top-performing GTM teams at companies like Okta, Gong, and Asana.

### Step 1: Define Your Social Selling ICP (SS-ICP)

Not all LinkedIn users are equal. Your SS-ICP combines:

- **Firmographic filters**: Role, seniority, industry, company size
- **Behavioral signals**: Recent posts, engagement with competitors, job changes
- **Technographic fit**: Tools used, integrations, tech stack

**Example SS-ICP (2026):**
> *VP of Sales at mid-market SaaS companies (50–500 employees) who:*
> - *Recently posted about “CRM adoption” or “sales enablement”*
> - *Engaged with content from Gong or Clari in the last 30 days*
> - *Changed jobs in the last 6 months*

🛠️ **Tool Tip**: Use AI-powered audience tools like **Typpout’s Social Waterfall** to build and refine your SS-ICP in real time.

### Step 2: Build a Social Listening Waterfall

AI-powered social listening isn’t just “set up alerts.” It’s a **multi-layered signal pipeline**:

| Layer              | What It Tracks                     | AI Action                          |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Content**        | Posts, articles, comments           | Identify pain points, buying intent |
| **Engagement**     | Likes, shares, replies              | Detect trending topics             |
| **Profile**        | Job changes, role updates           | Trigger sequence on role change    |
| **Network**        | Who’s engaging with your content    | Leverage mutual connections        |

🔍 **Pro Tip**: Use **AI-powered sentiment analysis** to prioritize prospects showing frustration with current tools or processes.

### Step 4: Automate Multi-Touch Sequences with AI Orchestration

LinkedIn isn’t a one-and-done channel. It’s a **conversation over time**.

A 2026 AI-powered sequence might look like:

| Day | Channel      | AI Action                                      |
|-----|--------------|------------------------------------------------|
| 1   | LinkedIn DM  | Personalized message (above)                  |
| 3   | Comment      | AI replies to prospect’s recent post           |
| 5   | Connection   | AI sends connection request with custom note  |
| 7   | InMail       | If no response, AI drafts a value-driven InMail |
| 10  | Video Drop   | AI shares a Loom video tailored to their role  |

🤖 **AI Orchestration Requirements**:
- **Non-linear paths**: If a prospect engages, skip ahead. If they don’t, escalate.
- **Compliance checks**: AI flags sequences that might trigger LinkedIn’s spam filters.
- **Reply handling**: AI can triage and route replies to reps or auto-reply with next steps.

🚨 **Warning**: Do *not* use bot-like automation. In 2026, LinkedIn’s AI detects unnatural patterns and **shadow-bans accounts** within hours.
