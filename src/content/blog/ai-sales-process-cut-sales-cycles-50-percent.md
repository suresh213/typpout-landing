## The AI Sales Process: A Step-by-Step Blueprint

Here’s how to implement an AI-powered sales process that cuts cycles by 50%.

### Step 1: Real-Time Intent Detection (Before the First Touch)

**Problem**: You’re reaching out too late—or worse, to the wrong people.

**Solution**: Use AI to detect **buying signals in real time**.

#### How It Works:
- Scrape **LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit, and industry forums** for intent signals (e.g., job posts, hiring for a role, tech stack changes).
- Identify **trigger events** (e.g., a company just raised funding, launched a product, or hired a new executive).
- Score leads based on **timeliness and relevance**, not just firmographics.

> 🚀 **Typpout Insight**: Teams using our **real-time intent engine** see a **40% increase in qualified meetings** within 30 days.

#### Actionable Steps:
1. Integrate **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** + **CRM** (HubSpot, Salesforce).
2. Use tools like **Typpout’s AI waterfall** to track intent signals across the web.
3. Prioritize leads with **multiple signals** (e.g., funding + hiring + tech adoption).

---

### Step 2: AI-Powered Personalization (The Right Message, Every Time)

**Problem**: 80% of buyers ignore generic outreach because it doesn’t speak to their **current needs**.

**Solution**: Use AI to **dynamically generate hyper-personalized messages** based on intent data.

#### How It Works:
- **Dynamic templates** that adapt to:
  - The prospect’s **role** (e.g., CFO vs. CTO).
  - Their **company’s recent news** (e.g., "Congrats on your Series B!").
  - Their **pain points** (e.g., "I noticed you’re hiring for RevOps—how’s your pipeline visibility?").
- **A/B test messaging at scale** with AI-driven optimization.

> 📊 **Data Point**: Personalized emails see **3x higher reply rates** than generic ones (source: HubSpot).

#### Example Workflow:
```plaintext
Input: Prospect just announced a new product launch.
AI Output:
"Hi [Name],

Saw your team just launched [Product]—congrats! We helped [Similar Company] reduce their onboarding time by 40% with our [Solution]. Would love to share a quick case study.

Best,
Suresh"
```

#### Tools to Use:
- **Typpout’s AI Reply Handler**: Automatically crafts **context-aware responses** to inbound leads.
- **Lavender.ai** for email subject/body optimization.

### Step 3: Smart Sequencing (Automate What Should Be Automated)

**Problem**: Following up manually is **inefficient and inconsistent**.

**Solution**: Use AI to **determine the optimal sequence** (timing, channel, content).

#### How It Works:
- **Multi-channel sequences** (email, LinkedIn, SMS) with **adaptive timing**.
- **AI-prioritized follow-ups** based on engagement signals (e.g., opened email, visited pricing page).
- **Automated meeting booking** when interest is detected.

> ⚡ **Typpout Case Study**: A SaaS client reduced their **sequence length by 3 emails** (from 8 to 5) while increasing **meeting bookings by 50%**.

#### Sequence Framework:
| Step | Channel | Content | Trigger |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1 | LinkedIn | Intent-based intro | Funding/hiring news |
| 2 | Email | Case study | Email opened |
| 3 | SMS | Quick question | No reply after 3 days |
| 4 | Calendar Link | Meeting invite | Positive reply |

#### Pro Tips:
- **Use tools like Lemlist or Reply.io** for multi-channel sequencing.
- **Set up slack alerts** for high-intent leads (e.g., visited pricing page).

### Step 4: AI-Driven Qualification (Separate Signal from Noise)

**Problem**: **50% of meetings booked are unqualified** (source: TOPO).

**Solution**: Use AI to **score leads in real time** and route only the best to sales.

#### How It Works:
- **Predictive scoring** based on:
  - **Firmographics** (size, industry, tech stack).
  - **Behavioral data** (website visits, content downloads).
  - **Engagement signals** (email opens, link clicks).
- **Automated disqualification** of low-fit leads.

> 📈 **Typpout’s AI Waterfall**: Teams using our **qualification engine** see a **35% increase in SQL conversion rates**.

#### Qualification Checklist (AI-Powered):
✅ **Fit**: Does this prospect match your ICP?
✅ **Intent**: Are they actively researching solutions?
✅ **Authority**: Are they a decision-maker or influencer?
✅ **Timeline**: Are they in a buying window?

#### Tools:
- **6sense** for intent data.
- **Demandbase** for account-level insights.

---

### Step 5: Closing Faster with AI (From Demo to Contract)

**Problem**: **Proposal-to-close takes 3-6 weeks** on average.

**Solution**: Use AI to **accelerate the final stages** with smart nudges and automation.

#### How It Works:
- **AI-generated proposals** templated to the prospect’s needs.
- **Automated contract reminders** (e.g., "Your trial expires in 3 days—here’s how to upgrade").
- **Dynamic pricing quotes** based on usage or company size.

> 💡 **Typpout Tip**: Add a **calendly link for contract signing** in your proposal email.

#### Closing Playbook:
1. **Day 1**: Send proposal with **embedded Calendly link**.
2. **Day 3**: Follow up with a **short video** (Loom) summarizing key benefits.
3. **Day 7**: If no response, send a **limited-time discount** (AI-generated).
4. **Day 14**: Escalate to a **sales rep** if no movement.
