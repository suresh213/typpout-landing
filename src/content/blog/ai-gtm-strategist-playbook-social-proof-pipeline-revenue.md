## Tactic #1: Real-Time Social Listening → Immediate Outreach (The AI Watchdog)

**Problem:** Prospects mention your brand, competitors, or use cases in real time—but your team only finds out days later.

**Solution:** Use AI-powered social listening to detect **intent signals** tied to social proof.

### How It Works:

1. **Monitor** LinkedIn, Reddit, Twitter, and industry forums for:
   - Mentions of your brand, product, or competitors
   - Keywords like “best CRM for X,” “alternative to Y,” or “how does Z work?”
   - Questions or complaints about your category

2. **Detect intent** using AI:
   - Is the mention **positive, negative, or neutral?**
   - Is the person **comparing options, researching, or ready to buy?**

3. **Respond in real time** with **contextual social proof**:
   - If they’re comparing, share a **case study** or **G2 review**
   - If they’re researching, point to a **demo video** or **customer testimonial**
   - If they’re complaining, offer a **solution with a trusted customer reference**

### Example:
> **Prospect:** “Does anyone have experience with Typpout vs. [Competitor]? Need help choosing.”
> **Your AI assistant:** “Great question! Check out this G2 review from [Customer] who switched from [Competitor] to Typpout—saved 20% time and improved pipeline visibility.”

🔧 **Tool Stack:**
- **Typpout’s AI GTM Platform** – real-time social listening with intent scoring
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator + Alerts** – for direct engagement
- **G2 + Capterra APIs** – to pull live ratings

> ✅ **Outcome:** Turn passive mentions into **warm leads** before competitors even know the prospect exists.

## Tactic #3: Competitor Defection Campaigns (Using Their Customers as Social Proof)

**Problem:** Prospects are using your competitor—but you know their customers are unhappy.

**Solution:** Run **AI-driven defection campaigns** that use *their* customers’ voices against them.

### How to Execute:
1. **Identify at-risk competitors** using AI:
   - Monitor G2/Capterra reviews for keywords like “expensive,” “hard to use,” or “poor support”
   - Pull churned customers from your CRM or LinkedIn Sales Navigator

2. **Craft messaging using their own customers’ words:**
   > “We left [Competitor] because their dashboard was too slow—now we close deals 2x faster with Typpout. See what [Customer Name] said: [Link to G2 review]”

3. **Automate outreach** using AI reply handling:
   - If they reply asking for details, Typpout’s AI can send a **tailored case study**
   - If they object, AI provides **social proof rebuttals** like “92% of [Competitor] users say onboarding takes too long—our average is 4 days.”

🔧 **Data Source:**
- **G2 Transparency Reports** – show competitor weaknesses
- **Typpout’s Intent Data** – find prospects actively researching alternatives

> ✅ **Result:** Turn competitor churn into **your pipeline**—with social proof as the wedge.
