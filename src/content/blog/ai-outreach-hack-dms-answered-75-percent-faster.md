## The Root Cause: Why Your DMs Are Being Ignored

Before we jump into the hack, let’s diagnose the core problem.

Most outreach fails because it’s:
- **Generic** – “Hi [Name], I help companies like yours with [vague pain point].”
- **Timing-blind** – Sent at 8 AM when prospects are drowning in notifications.
- **Self-focused** – Written from the sender’s perspective, not the prospect’s.
- **Noisy** – Buried under 50+ similar messages.

Even when personalized, the timing and context are often off. Prospects don’t just ignore *bad* messages—they ignore *all* messages.

That’s where **AI-driven contextual outreach** changes the game.

## The AI Outreach Hack: 75% Faster DM Responses

### 🔍 Step 1: Real-Time Social Listening with AI

Before sending *any* message, you need to know:
- What pain points are they *actively* discussing?
- What content are they engaging with?
- When are they most active?

We built Typpout’s **Real-Time Social Listener** to track:
- LinkedIn posts, comments, and engagement
- Twitter/X threads and replies
- Industry-specific forums and communities

📌 **Actionable Tip:**
Run a **3-day social listening sprint** on your target ICP. Identify:
- Top 3 pain points trending in their posts
- Keywords they use (e.g., “burnout,” “scaling,” “ROI”)
- Sentiment around your solution

> 🔎 *Example:* A SaaS founder we worked with noticed her ICP kept complaining about “manual data entry” in Twitter threads. She pivoted her messaging to focus on “eliminating manual work” — and saw response rates jump from 4% to 12%.

---

### 🤖 Step 2: AI-Generated, Contextual DMs (No Templates)

The biggest mistake? Using static templates.

Instead, we use **AI to generate DMs in real time**, based on:
- The prospect’s recent posts
- Their company’s pain points
- Your solution’s fit

📌 **How it works:**
1. AI scans the prospect’s latest LinkedIn/Twitter activity.
2. It identifies relevant pain points or comments.
3. It crafts a **personalized, non-salesy DM** that feels like a reply, not a pitch.

> ❌ **Traditional DM:**
> “Hi Alex, I noticed you’re hiring SDRs. We help teams like yours scale with AI-powered outbound.”
>
> ✅ **AI-Powered DM:**
> “Alex, saw your post about struggling with SDR ramp time. We helped a similar team cut it from 90 to 45 days using AI coaching. Any interest in a quick chat?”

📌 **Pro Tip:**
Keep the DM under 3 sentences. AI can help condense complex ideas into concise, engaging messages.

Timing isn’t luck—it’s data.

Typpout’s **AI Scheduler** analyzes:
- Prospect’s past activity (likes, comments, posts)

### 📈 Step 4: Automate Follow-Ups (But Keep Them Human)

Automation doesn’t mean robotic.

We use **AI-powered follow-ups** that:
- Reference prior conversations
- Adapt to sentiment (e.g., if they’re busy, delay)
- Include subtle CTA (e.g., “No pressure—just wanted to share this resource”)

📌 **Follow-Up Framework:**
1. **Day 0:** Send AI-crafted DM
2. **Day 3:** Follow up with a relevant case study
3. **Day 7:** Share a quick tip or insight
4. **Day 10:** Soft ask: “Still thinking about this—any questions?”

> 📊 **Result:** Prospects who received AI-optimized follow-ups replied **2.3x faster** than those on static sequences.
