## Tactic #1: Real-Time Intent Detection — The AI Sentry

> “The fortune is in the follow-up—but only if it’s immediate.”

Most teams treat social listening as a weekly report. That’s too slow.

### How to Execute:
1. **Set up AI-powered intent detection** using tools like Typpout, Hootsuite Insights, or Brandwatch.
2. **Define trigger phrases** tied to your ICP:
   - “Looking for a better CRM”
   - “Evaluating [competitor] vs [your solution]”
   - “Frustrated with [pain point]”
3. **Use NLP models** to classify sentiment (positive, neutral, negative, urgent).
4. **Route high-intent signals to your GTM engine** within seconds.

| Trigger Example | Intent Score | Recommended Action |
|-----------------|--------------|--------------------|
| “Can anyone recommend a good AI email tool?” | 92% (High) | Auto-reply with a 1:1 video + case study |
| “Just got a demo from HubSpot… still not sold.” | 78% (Medium) | Send ROI calculator + invite to live demo |
| “Anyone using [Competitor]? Thinking of switching.” | 85% (High) | Share competitor comparison + pricing comparison |

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Use **Typpout’s AI waterfall** to enrich signals with firmographics, tech stack, and past engagement. No more guessing who’s worth reaching out to.

## Tactic #2: Hyper-Personalized 1:1 Outreach — The AI Copywriter

Generic LinkedIn messages die. Personalized ones convert.

But personalization at scale? That’s where AI shines.

### How to Execute:
1. **Pull the prospect’s latest social post** (e.g., a LinkedIn update or tweet).
2. **Use AI to generate a 1:1 reply** based on:
   - Their pain points
   - Your solution’s fit
   - Their tone (professional, casual, technical)
3. **Add a subtle CTA**—not “Let’s chat,” but “I built a tool that fixed this exact issue for [similar company]. Want the Loom?”

> Example:
> **Prospect’s Post:** “Tired of spending 8 hours a week on manual lead enrichment.”
> **AI-Generated Reply:**
> “Ouch. We cut that down to 30 mins/week for [Company X]. Here’s how: [typpout.com/demo] — no pitch, just a 60-sec walkthrough if it helps.”

## Tactic #3: Automated Meeting Booking — The AI Concierge

Every second you spend sending a Calendly link is a second you’re not engaging the next prospect.

### How to Execute:
1. **Integrate your calendar** (Google, Outlook).
2. **Let AI detect availability** from the prospect’s social bio or past posts (e.g., “Open to new opportunities”).
3. **Send a smart invite** with context:
   > “Hi [Name], saw your post about scalability challenges. We helped [Similar Company] go from 10K to 50K leads/month with 30% less effort. Here’s a 15-min slot to see if it fits you: [typpout.com/meet]”

> ✅ **Bonus:** Use **AI-powered objection handling** in replies. If they say “Not interested,” the AI can respond:
> “Totally get it. What part of your stack *is* working well for you right now?”

## Tactic #4: Multi-Channel Follow-Up with Context — The AI Orchestrator

Social isn’t just one channel. Prospects bounce between LinkedIn, email, and even Reddit.

### How to Execute:
1. **Map the prospect’s social footprint** using AI (LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub, Reddit).
2. **Orchestrate a 3-touch, context-aware sequence**:
   - **Day 1:** LinkedIn reply to a post
   - **Day 3:** Email with a relevant case study
   - **Day 7:** Twitter DM with a quick tip
3. **Use AI to detect which channel they engage with most** and double down.

| Channel | When to Use | Example Message |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| LinkedIn | Initial engagement | Reply to their post with a value-driven comment |
| Email | High-intent signals | Send ROI calculator or demo invite |
| Twitter | Casual or public | “Quick tip: If you’re using [Tool], try [Feature] to cut costs by 20%” |
| Slack/Discord | Niche communities | Share a template or invite to a workshop |

> 🚀 **Typpout does this automatically.** We ingest social signals, enrich them, and trigger the right sequence across channels—all in real time.

## Tactic #5: AI-Powered Deal Acceleration — The AI Coach

Not all social listeners are ready to buy. But **they’re all somewhere in the funnel**.

### How to Execute:
1. **Score leads** using AI:
   - Intent strength
   - Engagement history
   - Tech stack match
   - Budget signals (e.g., hiring, funding rounds)
2. **Route high-scoring leads to ABM plays**:
   - Invite to a private workshop
   - Share a tailored ROI report
   - Connect with a customer in their industry
3. **Use AI to predict next steps**:
   - “This prospect is likely to book a demo in 5–7 days.”
   - “This one needs more nurturing—send a case study.”

| 60–84 | Case study + ROI | Email + Twitter |
| 40–59 | Nurture sequence | Email + Slack |
