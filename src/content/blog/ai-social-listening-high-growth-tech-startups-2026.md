### Step 2: Train Your AI on Your ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

Not all social signals are equal. A founder complaining about scalability is more valuable than a junior dev asking about features.

#### 🎯 How to Train Your AI:
- Upload your **ICP list** (job titles, company sizes, tech stacks).
- Define **buying intent keywords** (e.g., “migrate from Salesforce,” “AI sales automation”).
- Set **response rules** (e.g., auto-reply to hiring managers with a case study).

> 📊 **Result:** Your AI learns to prioritize signals from **decision-makers with urgent pain points**—not just loud voices.
