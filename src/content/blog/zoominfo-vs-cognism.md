ZoomInfo and Cognism are the two heaviest hitters in the B2B contact data space. Both are enterprise-grade platforms, but they have distinct geographic strengths.

## ZoomInfo: The North American Giant

ZoomInfo is the undisputed market leader for B2B data in North America. It features a massive database, robust intent tracking, and deep integrations. However, its database is less accurate for European markets, and its pricing is steep.

## Cognism: The European Specialist

Cognism is the preferred database for European sales teams. It has a high density of mobile phone numbers in the UK and EU, and is fully compliant with GDPR regulations.
