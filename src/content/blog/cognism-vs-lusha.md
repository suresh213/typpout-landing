For sales teams targeting the European market, GDPR compliance and phone number accuracy are paramount. Lusha and Cognism are the two leading data providers in this region.

## Cognism: Premium GDPR Compliance

Cognism is widely considered the best provider for European B2B data. It offers manually verified mobile numbers and is highly compliant with GDPR regulations. It is preferred by enterprise sales teams targeting the UK and EU.

## Lusha: Fast and User-Friendly

Lusha is highly popular for its browser extension, which allows SDRs to quickly find phone numbers and email addresses while browsing LinkedIn. It is generally more affordable and easier to set up for smaller teams.
