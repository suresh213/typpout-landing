## Why Social Intent Data is the Missing Link in Modern Sales

Most sales teams operate on outdated signals—website visits (often anonymous), demo requests, or form fills. These are *retrospective*—they tell you what happened, not what’s about to happen.

**Social intent data flips the script.**

By monitoring professional networks like LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and industry forums, you gain access to *real-time behavioral signals* that reveal:
- Prospects researching your competitors
- Executives engaging with content about your solution
- Teams discussing pain points you solve *right now*

> 🔍 **Example**: A VP of Marketing at a SaaS company likes a competitor’s post about “scaling revenue operations.” A week later, they download your case study. Traditional systems would miss this nuance. Social intent data flags it *immediately*.

## The 4-Step Playbook: From Signal to Closed Deal in 30% Less Time

Follow this proven framework to integrate social intent into your outbound motion.

### Step 1: Build Your Social Intent Data Waterfall

Not all intent signals are equal. You need a **tiered system** to prioritize high-value activity.

| Tier | Signal Type | Example | Weight | Action |
|------|-------------|--------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Direct competitor comparisons | Prospect comments on “Why we switched from X to Y” | 95 | Immediate outreach |
| 2 | Industry pain point engagement | Likes/retweets about “marketing automation struggles” | 70 | Personalized sequence |
| 3 | Content consumption | Shares whitepaper, attends webinar | 50 | Nurture campaign |
| 4 | Passive engagement | Profile views, job title changes | 30 | Warm intro request |

💡 **Pro Tip**: Use AI-powered tools to **score and stack-rank** these signals in real time. Typpout’s platform, for instance, applies a proprietary intent score based on depth, recency, and relevance—letting you focus on the 5% of prospects showing *true* readiness.

### Step 2: Map Intent to Buyer Journey Stages

Intent isn’t static—it evolves. Your outreach must mirror that journey.

| Stage | Social Behavior | Outreach Strategy | Tool |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|------|
| Awareness | Follows industry leaders, shares trends | Share thought leadership content | LinkedIn, Typpout AI |
| Consideration | Engages with competitor comparisons | Soft comparison email + case study | Email sequence |
| Decision | Comments on pricing posts, joins peer discussions | Direct value proposition, ROI calculator | Twitter/X, Slack communities |
| Commitment | Shares decision frameworks, posts about “finalists” | Urgency-based offer, social proof | All channels |

📌 **Key Insight**: Prospects in the *Decision* stage are 3x more likely to convert if contacted within 24 hours of posting. Social intent gives you that window.

### Step 4: Close the Loop with Intent-Based Nurture

Not every prospect is ready to buy today. But they *are* signaling intent for the future.

**Build a nurture engine powered by intent:**

1. **Tag** prospects based on tier and stage
2. **Send** curated content based on their engagement
3. **Re-engage** when intent spikes again
4. **Escalate** to ABM or direct outreach when decision-stage signals appear

📊 **Example Nurture Flow**:
- Week 1: Like their post → Send LinkedIn connection request
- Week 3: Attend your webinar → Email case study
- Week 6: Comment on competitor post → Direct pitch + ROI calculator
