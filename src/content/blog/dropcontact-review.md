Dropcontact takes a radically different approach to B2B data enrichment. Unlike Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Hunter — which all maintain massive contact databases — Dropcontact does not store any contact data. Instead, it enriches data in real-time using algorithms and publicly available information, never purchasing or storing personal data in a centralized database. This "no-database" approach makes Dropcontact the most genuinely GDPR-compliant enrichment tool on the market.

## The No-Database Model

When you submit a contact (name + company domain) to Dropcontact, the platform:
1. Searches public sources in real-time for email patterns.
2. Applies algorithmic pattern matching based on known company email formats.
3. Verifies the predicted email address through SMTP validation.
4. Returns the verified email without storing the result in any database.

This means Dropcontact never maintains a database of personal contact information — eliminating the GDPR liability that comes with storing EU citizen data. For European companies where legal teams scrutinize every data vendor, this privacy-by-design approach is a significant advantage.

## Core Features

**Native CRM Enrichment:** Dropcontact integrates directly with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive. Rather than exporting contacts, enriching them externally, and re-importing, Dropcontact enriches records directly within your CRM. New contacts are automatically enriched as they are created.

**Duplicate Detection and Merging:** The platform identifies duplicate CRM records and merges them intelligently — cleaning your database while enriching it.

**Email Verification:** Every email found is verified before being returned. Dropcontact claims 98%+ verification accuracy for found emails.

**Company Data Enrichment:** Beyond email addresses, Dropcontact enriches company records with legal entity information, SIREN/SIRET codes (for French companies), VAT numbers, and corporate structure data — valuable for European B2B sales compliance.

## Limitations

**No Phone Data:** Email only. No direct dials, no mobile numbers.

**Lower Match Rates:** Because Dropcontact does not maintain a pre-built database, its match rates are lower than database-first tools like Apollo or ZoomInfo. For contacts in niche industries or at very small companies, the real-time algorithm may not find a verified email.

**French Market Focus:** Dropcontact was built in France and has strongest coverage for French and Western European companies. North American and Asian market coverage is thinner.

**No Intent Data or Outreach:** Pure enrichment tool. No buying signals, no sequencing, no campaign management.
