Sales teams looking to modernize their GTM stack often compare Amplemarket and Clay.com. Here is how these platforms differ.

## Clay: The Enrichment Specialist

Clay is a database orchestrator. It allows you to build custom enrichment pipelines, combining data from 50+ providers using waterfall logic. It is excellent for RevOps teams that want to build bespoke lead lists.

## Amplemarket: The All-In-One Platform

Amplemarket is a comprehensive sales platform. It includes a database, email verification, sequencing, and AI features to help manage replies and calendar scheduling. It is designed to be a complete sales stack in one tool.

## The Typpout Intent Loop

[Typpout](/) focuses purely on real-time intent. Instead of managing databases or email sequences, Typpout monitors social channels for active buying signals, enriching leads and automating personalized outreach in one seamless flow.

**Verdict:** Choose Clay for custom data enrichment, Amplemarket for an all-in-one outbound system, and [Typpout](/pricing) to automate your signal-driven sales pipeline.
