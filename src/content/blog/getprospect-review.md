GetProspect is a LinkedIn-focused email finder that differentiates itself with a consumer-friendly credit policy: you only pay for email addresses that are verified as valid. Invalid and unknown results do not consume credits. This "pay-for-valid-only" model makes GetProspect attractive for budget-conscious teams who have been frustrated by competitors charging credits for undeliverable results.

## Core Features

**LinkedIn Chrome Extension:** The primary interface. Install the extension, visit LinkedIn profiles or Sales Navigator search results, and extract email addresses. The extension works on individual profiles and supports batch extraction from search results pages.

**Bulk Email Finder:** Upload CSV files with prospect names and company domains for batch email discovery and verification. Results include verification status and confidence scores.

**Email Verifier:** Built-in verification checks deliverability before returning results. The pay-for-valid model means verification is effectively included in the credit cost.

**CRM Export:** Direct export to HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive. Contacts include email, name, company, job title, and LinkedIn URL.

## The Pay-for-Valid Advantage

GetProspect's credit model is genuinely more fair than many competitors:

| Tool | Credit Policy |
|---|---|
| **GetProspect** | Credits only consumed for valid results |
| **Skrapp.io** | Credits consumed for valid + catch-all results |
| **Lusha** | Credits consumed for all reveals regardless of status |
| **Apollo.io** | Credits consumed for all reveals |

This policy means your effective cost per usable email is more predictable and lower than tools that charge for every lookup attempt.

## Limitations

**LinkedIn Account Risk:** Like all LinkedIn extraction tools, GetProspect carries account restriction risk. Users report that aggressive extraction (100+ profiles/day) triggers LinkedIn's automated detection systems.

**Email-Only:** No phone numbers, no mobile data, no company enrichment. Purely an email finding tool.

**No Outreach Features:** No sequencing, no campaigns, no warmup. Export data to a separate tool for outreach.

**Coverage Gaps:** GetProspect's database is smaller than Apollo's or RocketReach's. Match rates for smaller companies and international contacts can be low.
