X (Twitter) is where the tech, startup, and VC communities discuss their businesses in real time. For B2B sales reps, it is a valuable platform for sourcing pipeline from public conversations.

## 1. Track Key Search Queries

Set up searches for keywords related to your niche. Focus on phrases like:
* *"Looking for a tool that..."*
* *"Clay vs Apollo"*
* *"Alternative to ZoomInfo"*
* *"Anyone know a good developer for..."*

## 2. Engage in the Comments

When you find a relevant thread, don't just send a generic link. Write a reply that adds to the conversation and provides a clear next step (like booking a demo).
