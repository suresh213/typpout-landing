### Step 3: Analyze Competitor Content & Engagement Strategies
Use AI to **reverse-engineer** what’s working (and what’s not):

#### **A. Content Performance Deep Dive**
- **Top-Performing Posts:** What topics, formats, and CTAs drive the most engagement?
- **Posting Frequency:** How often do they post? (AI can predict optimal timing.)
- **Hashtag & Keyword Strategy:** Which hashtags generate the most reach?

#### **B. Engagement Pattern Analysis**
- **Response Time:** How quickly do they reply to comments/DMs? (Faster = better.)
- **Sentiment Trends:** Are their posts generating **positive, neutral, or negative** reactions?
- **Audience Growth:** Are their followers **increasing or declining**?

#### **C. Ad & Campaign Intelligence**
- **Paid vs. Organic Split:** Are they boosting top organic posts with ads?
- **Audience Targeting:** What demographics/industries are they focusing on?
- **Ad Copy & Creative:** Which messaging resonates most with their audience?
