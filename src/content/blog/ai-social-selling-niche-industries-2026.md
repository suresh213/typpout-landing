### Step 1: Define Your Niche’s Social Selling DNA

Not all industries are created equal. Your AI strategy must align with your niche’s **unique behaviors, pain points, and buying triggers**.

#### **How to Do It:**
1. **Map Your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Signals**
   - **Industry-specific keywords**: e.g., "supply chain automation" for logistics SaaS.
   - **Job titles**: e.g., "VP of Operations" in manufacturing.
   - **Behavioral triggers**: e.g., a CFO posting about "cost optimization" in fintech.

2. **Identify High-Signal Platforms**
   - **LinkedIn** for B2B thought leadership.
   - **Twitter/X** for real-time industry debates.
   - **Industry forums** (e.g., Reddit groups, Slack communities).

3. **Create a "Social Selling DNA" Document**
   - Example for **healthcare SaaS**:
     > *"Our ICP is hospital CIOs who engage with posts about 'interoperability' and 'patient data security.' We prioritize prospects who follow @HIMSS or #HealthIT on LinkedIn."*

### Step 4: Scale Reply Handling with AI (No More "We’ll Get Back to You")

**Problem**: Prospects expect **instant responses**. Manual handling is impossible at scale.

#### **How to Do It:**
1. **AI-Powered Reply Templates**
   - **For FAQs**:
     > *"Thanks for your question! Most of our customers in [Niche] use [Solution] to solve [Pain Point]. Here’s a quick guide: [Link]. Let me know if you’d like a demo!"*
   - **For Objections**:
     > *"I understand pricing is a concern. Many of our customers started with a pilot program—would you be open to a 30-minute call to explore?"*

2. **Escalate Complex Queries to Humans**
   - Use AI to **triage responses** and route urgent ones to reps.

3. **Track Reply Metrics**
   - **Response time** (aim for <1 hour).
   - **Engagement rate** (e.g., % of replies leading to meetings).
