Klenty is a sales execution platform that helps reps send personalized emails, make calls, and automate LinkedIn tasks. It is designed to help reps scale their day-to-day activity, but it does not tell them who they should be reaching out to in the first place.

## The Problem with Raw Scale

Sending more emails and making more calls to cold prospects leads to fatigue and low conversion rates. Sales teams need to focus their energy on prospects who are in-market.

## Focus on In-Market Buyers with Typpout

[Typpout](/) scans the web for active buying signals, filtering out the noise to surface prospects who are looking for solutions right now. Typpout's GTM agents then write custom messages that address the prospect's exact post or query.

Instead of running broad cadences, Typpout executes laser-focused, intent-driven outreach.

## Sourcing vs. Scale Comparison

* **Outbound Pacing:** Klenty excels at automated calling and email cadences. Typpout focuses on fast, signal-driven replies.
* **Lead Accuracy:** Klenty uses uploaded lists. Typpout scores social profiles against your ICP in real-time.
* **AI Functionality:** Typpout provides full autonomous agents for both outreach and reply handling.
