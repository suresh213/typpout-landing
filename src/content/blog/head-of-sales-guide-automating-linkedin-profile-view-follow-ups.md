## The 3 Types of Profile View Follow-Ups (And When to Use Each)

Not all profile viewers are the same. Use **AI-driven segmentation** to tailor your approach.

| Type of Viewer | Likelihood to Convert | Best Follow-Up Strategy |
|----------------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Competitor Employee** | High | Mention competitor’s gap, offer differentiation |
| **Target Buyer (ICP fit)** | Very High | Reference shared pain point, case study |
| **Investor or Partner** | Medium | Acknowledge role, offer insights |
| **Job Seeker** | Low | Optional: share hiring resources |

📌 **Pro Tip:** Prioritize viewers who match your **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** and have visited multiple times. These are warm leads.

## Avoiding the Pitfalls: Compliance & Relevance

Automation must be **ethical and effective**.

⚠️ **Common Mistakes:**
- Sending too soon (within minutes)
- Using spammy language
- Ignoring LinkedIn’s automation limits

✅ **AI Best Practices:**
- **Wait 2–4 hours** before first message (respect timing)
- **Limit message frequency** (2–3 max)
- **Include unsubscribe or opt-out options**
- **Monitor engagement** and pause unresponsive leads

> 🛡️ **Compliance Note:** Always use tools that **respect LinkedIn’s terms** and integrate via official APIs or compliant browser extensions.
