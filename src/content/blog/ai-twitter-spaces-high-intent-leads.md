## How AI Identifies High-Intent Leads in Real Time

Modern AI tools (like **Typpout**) don’t just transcribe Spaces—they **listen, analyze, and act**.

Here’s how it works:

### 1. **Real-Time Audio & Chat Monitoring**
AI systems like Typpout integrate with Twitter/X’s API to:
- Join Spaces automatically based on keyword triggers (e.g., “CRM”, “AI automation”)
- Capture live audio and chat messages
- Transcribe in near real time with high accuracy (90%+)

> **Pro Tip**: Use keyword-based triggers like "pricing", "demo", "alternative to [Competitor]", or "integration with [Tool]".

### 2. **Intent Scoring with NLP**
Not all mentions are equal. AI models analyze:
- **Sentiment** (positive, negative, neutral)
- **Intent phrases** (“looking for…”, “need to solve…”, “any recommendations?”)
- **Urgency** (time-sensitive questions)
- **Role relevance** (e.g., “CTO asking about scalability” vs. “random user”)

This creates a **lead score**—letting you prioritize outreach.

### 3. **Data Enrichment & Qualification**
Once a high-intent user is identified, AI enriches their profile with:
- LinkedIn data (title, company, industry)
- Website behavior (if available)
- Previous interactions (social engagement, past leads)

Now you’re not reaching out to a stranger—you’re engaging a **pre-qualified prospect**.

### ✅ Step 2: Automate Space Discovery & Joining
Use AI tools to:
- Monitor trending Spaces in your niche
- Auto-join when keywords are spoken
- Capture transcripts and chat messages

👉 *Typpout does this automatically—no manual setup.*

### ✅ Step 3: Score & Filter High-Intent Participants
Apply an intent scoring model:

```yaml
Score Components:
  - Mentions pricing or demo: +20
  - Asks for recommendations: +15
  - Mentions competitor: +10
  - Shows urgency (“need this ASAP”): +10
  - Role matches ICP: +15
  - Company size fits target: +10
```

🔹 **Score ≥ 40** → High Intent → Auto-engage
🔹 **Score 20–39** → Medium Intent → Nurture via email
🔹 **Score < 20** → Low Intent → No action

### ✅ Step 4: Craft Contextual, AI-Generated Outreach

**Bad:**
> “Hi, I saw you in the Space. Want to schedule a demo?”

**Good (AI-Powered):**
> “Hi [Name],
> I heard you ask about B2B pricing in the GTM SaaS Space today—we help teams like [Company] cut lead gen costs by 35%. Happy to share a quick case study if helpful.
> Want to chat 15 mins this week?”

👉 AI tools (like Typpout) generate **personalized messages** using the Space context and prospect data.

### ✅ Step 5: Automate Follow-Up & Meeting Booking

Once a prospect engages:
- AI sends a **calendar link** (Calendly, Chili Piper)
- Handles **objections** (“What’s your pricing?” → “Happy to walk you through our ROI calculator—here’s a 15-min slot”)
- Syncs data to CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive)

> **Result**: From Space to booked meeting in **under 24 hours**.
