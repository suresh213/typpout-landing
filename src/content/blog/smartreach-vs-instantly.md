Instantly.ai and Smartreach are two of the most popular cold email tools on the market today. Both are designed to help sales teams scale their email outreach, but they approach the problem differently.

## Instantly: Multi-Inbox Scaling

Instantly is famous for its unlimited email accounts model. It allows you to connect as many sender accounts as you want for a flat fee, making it the go-to tool for high-volume cold email campaigns.

## Smartreach: Advanced Deliverability

Smartreach focuses on granular control and advanced deliverability features. It offers deep integration with CRMs, detailed analytics, and custom deliverability settings that give you more control over how and when emails are sent.

## Head-to-Head Comparison

* **Pricing Model:** Instantly charges flat rates for unlimited accounts. Smartreach charges based on seats and features.
* **Inbox Rotation:** Both tools rotate inboxes, but Smartreach provides more granular analytics.
