## Step 1: Build a Real-Time Data Waterfall (No More Stale Leads)

You can’t automate outreach if your data is outdated. Founders who scale globally use AI to **generate, enrich, and validate leads in real time**.

Here’s how:

### 1.1 AI-Powered Lead Sourcing (Beyond LinkedIn)

Most founders rely on LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Apollo. But AI tools like **Typpout** go further:

- **Real-time social listening**: AI scans Twitter/X, Reddit, and industry forums to detect intent signals (e.g., "looking for a CRM," "hate our current billing system").
- **Automated enrichment**: Pulls job titles, tech stacks, funding rounds, and even recent news (e.g., "raised $10M Series B") from public sources.
- **ICP scoring**: AI assigns a "fit score" based on your ideal customer profile—no more guessing.

#### Example:
A founder in SaaS notices a spike in chatter about "AI-powered email sequences." Typpout’s AI flags 150 companies that recently mentioned this phrase—then enriches their LinkedIn profiles, tech stacks, and hiring activity.

**Result:** You get **fresh, warm leads**—not cold dusty spreadsheets.

---

### 1.2 The 3-Layer Data Validation System

Before sending a single message, AI validates leads across three layers:

| Layer | Tool | What It Checks |
|-------|------|----------------|
| **Identity** | Clearbit / Apollo | Email format, domain validity, role accuracy |
| **Intent** | Typpout / Demandbase | Recent job posts, tech stack changes, funding news |
| **Engagement** | AI outreach platform | Has the prospect visited your pricing page? Opened past emails? |

**Key insight:** If any layer fails, the lead is dropped—no wasted outreach.
