Leadfeeder (now Dealfront) is a popular tool that identifies which companies are visiting your website by looking up their IP addresses. It is a great way to discover hidden interest, but it has several limitations.

## The Limits of Website Visitor Tracking

1. **Anonymous Accounts:** Leadfeeder tells you the *company* that visited, but not the *individual*. Your reps still have to guess who to contact.
2. **Passive Intent:** Someone visiting a blog post might just be doing research, not looking to buy.
3. **Low Conversion:** Cold calling a company because "someone from your office visited our site" is a low-converting pitch.

## Typpout: Sourcing the Actual Buyer

[Typpout](/) focuses on active social intent. Instead of tracking anonymous office IPs, Typpout finds the exact individual who is posting about their needs on LinkedIn, X, or Reddit. This gives your reps the exact contact, the exact context, and a warm reason to reach out.

**Verdict:** Use Leadfeeder for general website traffic analytics. Choose [Typpout](/pricing) to find and pitch the actual buyers expressing intent on social media.
