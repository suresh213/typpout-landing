LinkedIn is the single most important network for B2B sales. However, choosing between LinkedIn Premium (Business) and LinkedIn Sales Navigator can be confusing.

## LinkedIn Premium: For General Networking

LinkedIn Premium Business is designed for professionals who want to view more profiles, see who visited their profile, and send a limited number of InMails (usually 15/month). It is great for general job seekers and recruiters, but lacks advanced sales features.

## Sales Navigator: Built for Prospecting

Sales Navigator is a dedicated sales platform. It offers advanced search filters (like company size, geography, department size, and job changes), custom lead lists, and 50 InMails per month. It is essential for outbound sales teams.
