**Example Output:**

### 3. Automated, Multi-Channel Outreach

AI SDRs don’t just send emails. They orchestrate **omnichannel campaigns** that adapt based on prospect behavior.

| Channel | AI Role | Cost per Touch |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Email | Auto-personalize subject & body | $0.02 |
| LinkedIn | AI-generated connection notes | $0.05 |
| SMS (via AI assistant) | AI-crafted text with emoji personalization | $0.08 |

🔁 **Automation Workflow:**
1. Prospect shows intent → Typpout flags
2. AI drafts email + LinkedIn message + SMS
3. Sequencer sends based on response patterns
4. If no reply, AI adjusts tone or channel

**Result:** 3–5x faster outreach with 50%+ less manual effort.
