Clay and PhantomBuster are two popular tools for growth hackers and outbound sales teams. While both are used to build lead lists, they have completely different architectures.

## PhantomBuster: The Scraping Specialist

PhantomBuster is a cloud-based scraping tool. It runs automated scripts (called "Phantoms") that mimic human behavior to scrape data from LinkedIn, Google Maps, X, and Instagram. It is excellent for extracting members of a LinkedIn group or post commentators.

## Clay: The Enrichment Orchestrator

Clay is a data enrichment database. It does not scrape sites directly. Instead, it aggregates data from 50+ third-party providers (like Apollo, Lusha, hunter.io) and runs them in programmable waterfall sequences.
