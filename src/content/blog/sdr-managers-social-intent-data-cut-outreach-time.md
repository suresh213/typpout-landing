## What Is Social Intent Data—and Why It Matters for SDRs

**Social intent data** is a real-time signal that shows when a prospect is actively researching, engaging, or expressing interest in your industry, competitors, or solution—often **weeks before they fill out a form**.

Examples include:

- A CTO posting on LinkedIn: *“Struggling to scale our CI/CD pipeline—anyone using CircleCI vs GitHub Actions?”*
- A VP of Marketing commenting on a Reddit thread: *“We’re evaluating new CRM platforms—what’s everyone using post-HubSpot migration?”*
- An engineer retweeting a competitor’s post about AI-driven analytics.

These aren’t just “likes” or “views.” They’re **buyer-ready signals**—and they’re happening **outside your CRM**.

### Step 2: Prioritize High-Intent Prospects with AI Scoring

Not all intent signals are equal. Use a **scoring model** to rank prospects:

| Industry trend post (e.g., “AI in sales”) | 60 | Early-stage interest |
| Generic hashtag usage | 30 | Low intent |

📊 **Result:** Your SDRs focus only on the **top 20%**—those ready to talk.

## Why Social Intent Beats Traditional Intent Data

Traditional intent data (e.g., from Bombora, G2, or website visits) is **retrospective** and **incomplete**.

It tells you: *“This company visited your pricing page 3 times last week.”*

But it **doesn’t tell you**:
- Who specifically is researching?
- What are they saying about your space?
- When did they post about it?

🔥 **Social intent is proactive.** It shows **human behavior**—not just company-level signals.

And when combined with **AI-powered messaging**, it becomes a **predictive sales engine**.
