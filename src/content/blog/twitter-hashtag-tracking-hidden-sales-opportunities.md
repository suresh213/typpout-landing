### Step 3: Identify High-Intent Tweets (Filter for Sales-Ready Leads)
Not every tweet deserves a pitch. Focus on **signals that indicate buying intent**:

#### **Buying Intent Signals on X**
| **Signal** | **Example Tweet** | **Your Next Move** |
|------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Frustration with competitors** | "Frustrated with @Competitor’s pricing. Need a better alternative." | Reply with a helpful resource or demo offer. |
| **Job change in target role** | "Just joined as Head of Growth at [Company]. Excited to scale!" | Congrats + offer a quick chat about their goals. |
| **Looking for recommendations** | "Does anyone have experience with #SalesCRM tools?" | DM with a case study or free trial. |
| **Active research** | "Evaluating tools for #AccountBasedMarketing. Any recommendations?" | Share a comparison guide + offer a demo. |
| **Event-based engagement** | "Attending #SaaStr next week! Will be looking for new solutions." | Book a meeting before the event. |

**Pro Tip:** Use **negative sentiment keywords** like "hate," "frustrated," or "waste of money" to find prospects ready for a change.

### Step 4: Engage the Right Way (Avoid the Spam Trap)
Once you’ve identified a high-intent lead, **don’t pitch immediately**. Follow this engagement framework:

#### **The 3-Step X Engagement Strategy**
1. **Listen & Add Value**
   - Reply to their tweet with a **helpful comment** (not a sales pitch).
   - Example:
     > "Hey [Name], we’ve helped similar companies reduce [pain point] by 30%. Happy to share a quick tip if you’re exploring options!"

2. **Move to DM (If Appropriate)**
   - If they engage positively, slide into their DMs with a **personalized message**.
   - Example:
     > "Saw your tweet about [pain point]. We’ve helped [similar company] solve this—would love to share a quick case study. 15-min chat?"

3. **Book the Meeting (Without Being Pushy)**
   - Offer a **low-friction next step** (e.g., demo, resource, or quick call).
   - Example:
     > "Here’s a free guide we put together for companies in your situation: [link]. Happy to hop on a call if it’s useful!"

**Avoid:**
❌ Automated DMs (they feel impersonal)
❌ Overly salesy replies (focus on helping first)
❌ Ignoring follow-ups (most sales happen after multiple touches)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid with X Hashtag Tracking

| **Mistake** | **Why It’s Bad** | **How to Fix It** |
