Apollo.io's pricing structure is one of the most transparent in the sales intelligence market, which is refreshing compared to enterprise vendors like ZoomInfo that hide pricing behind demo requests. But transparent does not mean simple. Apollo uses a combination of per-user subscription fees, email credits, mobile credits, and feature gates that create a total cost profile more complex than the pricing page suggests.

### The Credit System Explained

Apollo uses three separate credit types, each with different allocation rules:

**Email Credits:** Used for revealing email addresses. The free plan includes 10,000 per month. Paid plans offer unlimited email credits, which sounds generous — but "unlimited" applies to email reveals, not email sends. Sending limits are governed by your connected email accounts and Apollo's daily pacing rules.

**Mobile Credits:** Used for revealing phone numbers. These are the most expensive credits in Apollo's system. At 75 credits on the Basic plan ($49/month), each phone number effectively costs $0.65 — significantly more expensive than dedicated phone data providers like Lusha ($0.10-0.20 per number at scale).

**Export Credits:** Used for downloading contacts to CSV. This is a frequently overlooked limitation. Even with unlimited email reveals, you can only export 1,000 contacts per month on the Basic plan. Teams doing large-scale list building hit this wall quickly.

## Hidden Costs That Scale Poorly

### Per-User Multiplication

Apollo charges per user, not per team. The math matters:

| Team Size | Basic Plan Total | Professional Plan Total | Organization Plan Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 user | $49/mo | $79/mo | $119/mo |
| 3 users | $147/mo | $237/mo | $357/mo |
| 5 users | $245/mo | $395/mo | $595/mo |
| 10 users | $490/mo | $790/mo | $1,190/mo |

A 10-person SDR team on the Professional plan pays $9,480 annually — entering ZoomInfo's lower pricing territory while receiving significantly less data depth.

### Verification Tax

Apollo's email data requires secondary verification for serious outbound campaigns. Budget an additional $16-50/month for ZeroBounce or NeverBounce to scrub lists before sending.

### Warmup Costs

Apollo's sequencer does not include native inbox warmup. Add $19-39/month per inbox for Warmbox or use Instantly's warmup feature — which means paying for a competing sequencer alongside Apollo.

## Who Gets Real Value from Apollo Pricing

Apollo's pricing delivers genuine value for **solo founders and 1-3 person teams** who use the free or Basic plan as their primary outbound tool. The free tier alone provides more functionality than most tools charge $49/month for.

The economics deteriorate for **teams above 5 users** or those needing high-accuracy phone data. At that scale, the per-user model combined with supplementary tool costs pushes total spending above $500/month — territory where more comprehensive platforms offer better unit economics.
