These aren’t theoretical templates. They’re **field-tested frameworks** pulled from Typpout’s real-time AI GTM engine, which processes **millions of LinkedIn interactions daily**.

---

### 🔥 Script #1: The "Icebreaker Trigger" – For First-Time Engagers

**Use Case**: Prospect liked/commented on your post or shared your content, but hasn’t replied to your initial message.

**Why It Works**: Acknowledges the engagement without being salesy. Builds rapport through shared attention.

```text
Hi [First Name],

I noticed you engaged with my post on [Topic]—thanks for taking the time. [Mention specific comment or reaction].

I’m working on a [solution/insight] for teams like yours dealing with [pain point], and I’d love to hear your thoughts—what’s your biggest challenge with [related topic] right now?

No pitch, just curious.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

> 🧠 **AI Optimization Tip**: Use Typpout’s **real-time social listening** to detect *which* post they engaged with and reference it directly. Our platform pulls engagement data in under 2 seconds.

### 🚀 Script #2: The "Time-Based Urgency" – For Delayed Replies

**Use Case**: Prospect replies after 24+ hours—your original message is now irrelevant or cold.

**Why It Works**: Acknowledges the delay and reframes the conversation with fresh relevance.

```text
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for getting back—I know things move fast, and I appreciate the reply.

Since my last message, [Company Name] announced [recent news: funding, product launch, hiring spike], which got me thinking: how are you handling [related challenge] with your new growth?

I’d love to share how [Competitor/Similar Company] solved this in 30 days—no pressure, just curious.

Would you be open to a quick chat?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

> 📊 **Conversion Booster**: Typpout’s **AI intent engine** scans company news feeds in real time. When a prospect replies late, we auto-inject relevant updates into the reply—boosting relevance by 34%.

### 🧩 Script #3: The "Curiosity Hook" – For Vague or Short Replies

**Use Case**: Prospect replies with "Interesting" or "Tell me more"—no clear next step.

**Why It Works**: Turns a passive reply into a two-way conversation using open-ended questions and social proof.

```text
Hi [First Name],

Glad you found it interesting!

Most [industry] leaders I’ve spoken with recently are focusing on [trending topic], but I’d love to hear what *you’re* prioritizing.

For example, at [Similar Company], they reduced [metric] by 40% using [approach].

What’s one goal you’re laser-focused on this quarter?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

> ✅ **Pro Tip**: Use **social proof** in every script. AI tools like Typpout auto-pull case studies from your CRM and matching industries in real time.

---

### 🧠 Script #4: The "Opposition Play" – For Skeptical or Critical Replies

**Use Case**: Prospect pushes back: "We’re already using X" or "This doesn’t apply to us."

**Why It Works**: Validates their current solution, then redirects to a higher-value conversation.

```text
Hi [First Name],

Fair point—[Current Tool] is solid for [use case].

I’ve seen teams like yours use it for [initial purpose], but hit a ceiling when it comes to [new challenge].

We helped [Company in Same Industry] go from [status quo] to [better result] in 6 weeks—would you be open to seeing how?

No commitment, just insight.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

> 🛠 **AI Defense**: Typpout’s **sentiment analysis** detects skepticism in tone and auto-tailors tone from pushback to partnership.

---

### 📈 Script #5: The "Next-Step Accelerator" – For "Maybe" Replies

**Use Case**: Prospect says "Can you send more info?" or "Let me think about it."

**Why It Works**: Turns passive interest into an actionable next step—meeting booking or demo request.

```text
Hi [First Name],

Absolutely—happy to send over [resource: case study, demo link, ROI calculator].

But before I do, I want to make sure this actually solves [their core problem].

Would you be open to a 10-minute call this week to confirm it’s a fit?

You can book directly here: [Calendly link]

No pressure—just want to save you time.

Best,
[Your Name]
```
