For Revenue Operations (RevOps) leaders, intent data is a powerful tool for optimizing sales efficiency. However, integrating intent data into your existing systems can be complex.

## 1. Standardize the Ingestion

Ensure that all intent signals (from website tracking, social listening, or third-party databases) are mapped to standard contact and account properties in your CRM.

## 2. Establish Routing Rules

Set up automated routing to assign high-intent leads to active sales reps immediately. The faster a rep follows up on a signal, the higher the conversion rate.
