## Step-by-Step: How to Use LinkedIn Live to Book Meetings

### Step 1: Choose a High-Value Topic (With a Meeting Intent Hook)

Your live session shouldn’t just be a webinar—it should be a **lead magnet disguised as a conversation**.

**Example Topics That Book Meetings:**
- *"How [Your Industry] Leaders Are Scaling in 2025"*
- *"The Biggest Mistake Founders Make When Hiring Sales Teams"*
- *"How We Landed [X] Customers in 3 Months—With [Guest]"*

**Pro Tip:** Invite a **thought leader** (even a micro-influencer) to co-host. This gives you instant credibility and a built-in audience to tap into.

### Step 2: Promote the Live Session Like a Rockstar

You can’t just hit "Go Live" and hope people show up. You need a **multi-channel promotion strategy**:

| **Channel**       | **Action Item**                          | **Expected Reach** |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| LinkedIn Posts    | Post a teaser + countdown (3x before)    | 500-2,000          |
| Email List        | Send a dedicated invite (if you have one)| 100-500            |
| Guest’s Network   | Ask your guest to share with their audience | 500-2,000       |
| LinkedIn DMs      | Send personalized invites to 50-100 people | 20-50 replies   |

**Best Time to Go Live:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM or 1-3 PM (EST).

### Step 3: Run the Live Session Like a Pro

During the session:
- **Start with a strong hook** (e.g., *"Most founders waste 80% of their time on the wrong leads—here’s how we fixed it."*)
- **Engage with comments** (answer questions live to build rapport).
- **End with a CTA** (e.g., *"Want to discuss this further? DM me ‘MEETING’—I’ll send a calendar link."*)

**Pro Hack:** If you have a guest, **ask them to mention a specific problem they solve**—this primes attendees to book a meeting with you.

### Step 4: Follow Up with Attendees (The Meeting Booking Machine)

Here’s where the magic happens. **Most founders drop the ball here.**

**Automated Follow-Up Sequence (Example):**

| **Day** | **Action**                          | **Template** |
|---------|-------------------------------------|--------------|
| **Day 0** | Send a thank-you DM to all attendees | *"Thanks for joining! Here’s the replay: [Link]. If you want to dive deeper into [topic], I’d love to chat—here’s my calendar: [Calendly Link]."* |
| **Day 3** | DM the most engaged attendees       | *"I noticed you asked about [X]—let’s discuss how we’ve helped others with this. Here’s a time that works: [Link]."* |
| **Day 7** | Post a recap + tag high-value attendees | *"Missed the live session? Here’s what we covered. [Tag relevant attendee]—I’d love your take on this!"* |
