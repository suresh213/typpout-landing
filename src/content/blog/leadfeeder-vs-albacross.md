Leadfeeder and Albacross are two of the leading tools for deanonymizing website traffic. Both work by tracking IP addresses and matching them to corporate databases, but they have distinct differences.

## Leadfeeder: Focus on CRM Integration

Leadfeeder (Dealfront) has excellent integrations with CRMs like HubSpot and Salesforce, making it easy to send identified companies directly to your sales pipeline. Its UI is designed to help sales reps filter traffic by pages visited and company size.

## Albacross: Focus on ABM and Ads

Albacross has a stronger focus on Account-Based Marketing (ABM). It allows you to sync identified companies with ad networks, so you can target visitors with display ads even if they didn't fill out a form.

## Sourcing Actual Buyers with Typpout

While website tracking tells you what companies are reading your blog, it doesn't give you the email of the person who read it. [Typpout](/) solves this by identifying specific individuals posting their requirements on social networks, giving you a warm, direct sales opportunity.
