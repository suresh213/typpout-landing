The Apollo.io Chrome extension is the fastest way to pull contact data from LinkedIn profiles, company websites, and Gmail conversations without switching tabs. It has earned strong ratings in the Chrome Web Store and is the most-used feature among Apollo's SMB user base. But the extension has daily limits, accuracy quirks, and LinkedIn compliance risks that are not obvious from the install page.

## Installing the Apollo.io Chrome Extension

Setup takes under two minutes:

1. Navigate to the Chrome Web Store and search for "Apollo.io."
2. Click "Add to Chrome" and approve the requested permissions.
3. Sign into your Apollo account through the extension popup.
4. Navigate to LinkedIn, any company website, or Gmail to see the overlay panel.

The extension requests read access to page content on LinkedIn, Gmail, and general web pages. It uses this to match visible profile information against Apollo's internal database.

## How It Works on LinkedIn

When you visit a LinkedIn profile, the Apollo sidebar appears automatically with:

- **Full name and current job title** (matched from LinkedIn profile data)
- **Verified email address** (from Apollo's database)
- **Phone numbers** (direct dial and/or mobile, when available)
- **Company details** (size, industry, revenue range, funding status)
- **"Save to List" button** to add the contact directly to an Apollo prospect list
- **"Add to Sequence" button** to immediately enroll the contact in an outreach cadence

On Sales Navigator search results pages, you can bulk-select multiple profiles and save them to Apollo lists in batch — processing up to 25 contacts per page.

## Daily Limits and Credit Consumption

The extension's usage is governed by your Apollo plan's credit system:

- **Email reveals:** Each email address displayed consumes one email credit. On the free plan (10,000 credits/month), this averages roughly 333 reveals per day.
- **Phone reveals:** Each phone number displayed consumes one mobile credit. On the Basic plan with 75 mobile credits/month, you get approximately 2-3 phone reveals per day.
- **LinkedIn rate limiting:** Independent of Apollo's credit system, LinkedIn monitors extension activity. Viewing more than 80-100 profiles per day through any data extension risks triggering LinkedIn's automated detection.

### Protecting Your LinkedIn Account

LinkedIn actively identifies and restricts accounts using automation tools. To minimize risk:

- Keep daily profile views below 80, regardless of how many Apollo credits you have.
- Mix extension usage with organic LinkedIn activity (posting, commenting, messaging).
- Avoid running Apollo alongside other LinkedIn extensions simultaneously.
- Use Sales Navigator accounts, which have higher activity thresholds than free LinkedIn.

## Common Extension Issues

**"No Data Found" Results:** For smaller companies, international contacts, or people who recently changed jobs, Apollo frequently returns no match. The database has strong coverage for US-based tech companies but thinner coverage for niche industries and EMEA markets.

**Stale Data Alerts:** The extension sometimes displays a previous employer's email even when the LinkedIn profile clearly shows a new role. Always cross-reference the displayed company name against the profile header.

**Extension Conflicts:** Running Apollo alongside Lusha, Kaspr, or Skrapp extensions causes conflicts — panels overlap, data mismatches, and increased LinkedIn detection risk. Use one extension at a time.
