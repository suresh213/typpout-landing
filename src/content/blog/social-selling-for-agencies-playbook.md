For B2B digital agencies, generating a consistent stream of high-ticket leads is a major challenge. Cold outreach conversion rates are dropping, and relying solely on referrals limits growth.

## Sourcing Agency Leads from Public Conversations

B2B buyers frequently ask their social networks for agency recommendations:
* *"Looking for a Webflow agency to redesign our marketing site..."*
* *"We are hiring a performance marketing agency. Send recommendations..."*
* *"Any recommendations for an agency that builds custom mobile apps?"*

These are high-intent buying signals. If your agency is the first to reply with a tailored response, you are highly likely to book a discovery call.
