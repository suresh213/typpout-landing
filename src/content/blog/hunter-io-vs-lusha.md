Hunter.io and Lusha solve different halves of the B2B contact data problem. Hunter excels at finding and verifying email addresses. Lusha excels at revealing phone numbers, especially direct mobile dials for decision-makers. Which tool you need depends on whether your outbound strategy leads with email or phone — and increasingly, modern teams need both.

## Core Capability Comparison

| Capability | Hunter.io | Lusha |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary strength** | Email discovery and verification | Phone numbers and contact enrichment |
| **Email accuracy** | Industry-leading (70-85% deliverable) | Good (60-75% deliverable) |
| **Phone data** | ❌ Not available | ✅ Direct dials and mobile numbers |
| **LinkedIn extension** | ✅ Available | ✅ Strong — primary interface |
| **Domain search** | ✅ Deep domain intelligence | ❌ Limited |
| **Email verification** | ✅ Best-in-class | ✅ Basic built-in |
| **Built-in sequencing** | ✅ Basic campaigns | ❌ Not available |
| **CRM integrations** | ✅ HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive | ✅ HubSpot, Salesforce, Outreach |
| **API** | ✅ Well-documented REST API | ✅ REST API available |

### The Core Trade-Off

**Hunter gives you accurate emails but zero phone data.** You can verify email deliverability with high confidence but cannot cold-call prospects.

**Lusha gives you phone numbers but less reliable emails.** You can reach decision-makers directly by phone but may experience higher email bounce rates.

## Pricing Comparison

| Plan Level | Hunter.io | Lusha |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 25 searches + 50 verifications/month | 50 credits/month |
| Entry | $49/month — 500 searches | $49/month — 160 credits |
| Mid-tier | $149/month — 5,000 searches | $79/month — 400 credits |
| Team/Scale | $399/month — 50,000 searches | Custom pricing |

Credits work differently between the tools. Hunter separates search and verification credits. Lusha uses a unified credit system where each credit reveals a contact's full information (email + phone when available). Lusha's per-credit cost is higher, but each credit delivers more data types.

## Use Case Mapping

### Choose Hunter.io When:
- Cold email is your primary outbound channel.
- Sender reputation and deliverability are non-negotiable priorities.
- You need domain-level intelligence to map email patterns across entire companies.
- Your budget is tight and you already have a phone data source.

### Choose Lusha When:
- Cold calling is a primary pipeline generation activity.
- You need mobile phone numbers for executive-level prospects.
- Your SDR team does LinkedIn-first prospecting and needs quick enrichment.
- Phone connect rates directly impact your team's quota achievement.

### When You Need Both

Many outbound teams end up subscribing to both tools — Hunter for email verification, Lusha for phone data — which creates a $100-230/month combined spend before adding sequencing, warmup, and CRM costs. This multi-tool approach works but introduces workflow fragmentation and data management overhead.
