## How Growth Teams Use X Bookmarks for Prospect Prioritization

X bookmarks aren’t just for saving tweets—they’re a **lightweight CRM layer** that helps you build a prioritized pipeline from social signals.

Here’s how top teams do it:

---

### Step 1: Define Your ICP Signals (What to Look For)

Before bookmarking anything, identify **intent signals** tied to your ICP.

Create a simple scoring system:

| Signal Type | Priority Score | Example |
|------------|----------------|--------|
| Job Change | 5 | “Just promoted to Head of Sales Ops” |
| Pain Point Mention | 4 | “Hate how our sales tool doesn’t integrate with HubSpot” |
| Competitor Mention | 3 | “We’re evaluating Salesforce but frustrated with pricing” |
| Topic Engagement | 2 | Liked 3 posts about AI in sales |
| Profile Visit | 1 | Viewed your website after engaging with your tweet |

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Use X’s advanced search to find these signals.
> Example: `“Head of Sales” AND “HubSpot” since:2024-08-01`
> Then save relevant tweets to a private list or bookmark them.

---

### Step 2: Build a Bookmark-Based Prospect Pipeline

Instead of exporting to a spreadsheet, use X bookmarks as a **live pipeline**.

#### 📌 Create a Bookmark Workflow:

1. **Find** a tweet with intent (e.g., job change, pain point)
2. **Bookmark** the tweet (not just the profile)
3. **Tag** the bookmark with the signal type (e.g., `job-change`, `pain-point`)
4. **Add notes** directly in the bookmark (e.g., “Looking for CRM alternatives”)
5. **Export weekly** as CSV (via tools like Typpout or TweetDeck) for outreach sequencing

> ✅ **Why bookmarking the tweet > just bookmarking the profile?**
> Because the **tweet itself is the context**—what they said, when they said it, and why it matters.

---

### Step 3: Prioritize Prospects Using a Scorecard

Turn your bookmarked tweets into a **prioritized list** using a simple framework:

| Prospect | Signal | Score | Contact Method | Next Action |
|--------|--------|-------|----------------|-------------|
| @SarahRevenue | “New VP of Revenue at Acme Corp” | 5 | LinkedIn DM | Send welcome message |
| @TechOpsGuru | “Migrating from Salesforce to HubSpot” | 4 | Email + Tweet reply | Offer migration guide |
| @AIinSalesFan | Liked 3 posts about AI tools | 2 | Retweet + follow-up | Share case study |

> 🔁 **Automate follow-ups with sequences:**
> - Day 1: Engage on their tweet
> - Day 3: Send personalized DM
> - Day 7: Follow up with value (case study, tool comparison)

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Bookmarking without context** – Just saving tweets without notes = wasted time.
✅ **Always add a reason** in the bookmark (e.g., “Looking for AI sales tools”).

❌ **Ignoring timing** – A job change from 6 months ago isn’t urgent.
✅ **Focus on recent signals** (use X’s “Latest” tab).

❌ **Treating X as a one-off channel** – Most deals require **multi-channel nurturing** (LinkedIn, email, retargeting).
✅ **Use X as a trigger, not the entire funnel**.
