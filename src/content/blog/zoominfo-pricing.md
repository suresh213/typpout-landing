ZoomInfo is one of the few major SaaS platforms that still refuses to publish pricing on its website. Instead, prospective buyers are funneled through a sales demo process to receive a custom quote — a strategy designed to maximize deal size based on perceived willingness to pay. Based on aggregated user reports, industry benchmarks, and public contract data from 2026, here is what ZoomInfo actually costs.

## Estimated ZoomInfo Pricing (2026)

| Tier | Estimated Annual Cost | Typical Seats | Key Inclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | $15,000 - $25,000/year | 2-3 users | Contact data, basic filters, company profiles |
| Advanced | $25,000 - $45,000/year | 5-10 users | Intent data, Engage sequences, advanced filters |
| Elite | $45,000 - $100,000+/year | 10-25+ users | Chorus.ai, custom workflows, API access |

**Important caveats:** These are estimates based on aggregated user reports and may vary significantly based on negotiation, contract length, and company size. ZoomInfo is known for aggressive sales tactics including limited-time discounts, multi-year lock-ins, and bundled add-ons that inflate the initial quote.

### Per-Seat Costs

ZoomInfo typically charges per user seat, with each additional seat adding $1,500-3,000 per year depending on the tier. A common pricing structure:

### Add-On Modules and Hidden Costs

ZoomInfo's base subscription rarely includes everything a team needs. Common add-ons with additional costs:

- **Chorus.ai (conversation intelligence):** $100-200/user/month on top of the base subscription.
- **FormComplete (visitor tracking):** Additional annual fee based on website traffic volume.
- **Data enrichment API:** Usage-based pricing on top of the platform subscription.
- **Custom intent topics:** Additional configuration fees for bespoke intent signal tracking.

## Why ZoomInfo Hides Pricing

The opaque pricing strategy serves ZoomInfo's sales process in several ways:

1. **Price discrimination:** Different companies pay different amounts for the same product based on perceived budget and urgency. A venture-backed startup gets quoted differently than a Fortune 500 company.
2. **Upsell leverage:** The demo process allows sales reps to identify which features the prospect values most and price accordingly, often bundling modules the prospect did not request.
3. **Competitive insulation:** By not publishing prices, ZoomInfo makes direct price comparisons harder, forcing buyers to commit time to a sales process before discovering costs.
