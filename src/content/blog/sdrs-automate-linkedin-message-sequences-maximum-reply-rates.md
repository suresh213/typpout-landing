## The 3 Principles of High-Reply LinkedIn Message Sequences

To maximize reply rates, your sequence must satisfy three core principles:

### 1. **Relevance: The Right Message to the Right Person**
Generic “Hi [Name], I help companies like yours…” messages get ignored.

Instead:
- Reference a recent post, job change, or company milestone
- Use data from real-time social listening (e.g., “I saw your article on AI in sales—great insights”)
- Segment by role, industry, and intent

> 🔍 *Typpout’s AI "Social Listening" engine scans LinkedIn in real time to detect triggers like promotions, funding rounds, or content shares—so your outreach is never outdated.*

### 2. **Timing: The Right Moment to Engage**
Timing isn’t just about “when you send.” It’s about **when your prospect is ready**.

- Connection requests sent during active hours (8–10 AM or 12–2 PM local time)
- Follow-ups spaced 3–5 days apart (not daily spam)
- Messaging aligned with real-time events (e.g., post-launch, funding, or hiring)

> 🚀 *Typpout uses AI to detect user activity and trigger sequences only when the prospect is online or engaged—dramatically increasing visibility.*

### 3. **Personalization: Not Just Names—Real Context**
Personalization = **data + timing + voice**.

Top-performing sequences:
- Use 3–5 data points per prospect (job title, company size, recent news, shared content)
- Mirror the prospect’s tone (professional, casual, technical)
- Include a clear CTA (e.g., “Would you be open to a 10-minute chat?”)

> ✅ *With Typpout, SDRs can auto-generate personalized intros using AI that pulls from LinkedIn profiles, company websites, and recent posts—without writing a word.*
