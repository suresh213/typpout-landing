When choosing a B2B prospecting tool, Reply.io and Apollo.io are two of the most common options. While they share some features, they have very different strengths.

## Apollo: The Database Giant

Apollo's main strength is its massive B2B contact database, containing over 275 million records. It is excellent for building large lists based on filters like job title, company size, and geography. However, its outreach features are relatively basic compared to dedicated sales engagement platforms.

## Reply.io: The Multi-Channel Specialist

Reply.io focuses heavily on sales engagement. It offers advanced multi-channel sequences (email, phone, LinkedIn, WhatsApp) and has integrated AI features to help write emails. But its database is not as large or accurate as Apollo's.
