You can’t engage high-intent prospects if you don’t know who they are—or where they’re listening.

Here’s how growth teams find their ideal audience on Spaces:

### 1. **Monitor Industry-Specific Keywords**
Use X’s search or third-party tools to track:
- `#SaaS`
- `#B2BMarketing`
- `#SalesTech`
- `#RevOps`
- `#FounderFriday`

Pro tip: Set up **real-time alerts** for phrases like “looking for,” “switching from,” or “evaluating” + your niche.

### 2. **Join Competitor & Thought Leader Rooms**
Follow influencers, CEOs, and competitors in your space. When they host Spaces, **high-intent prospects are likely listening**.

🔍 **Look for engagement cues**:
- High number of live listeners
- Q&A participation
- Follow-up tweets or replies from attendees

### 3. **Analyze Audience Demographics**
Use tools like **Typpout’s AI social listening engine** to:
- Identify job titles (e.g., “CRO,” “Head of Growth”)

## Step 2: Engage Prospects in Real Time—Without Being Creepy

The goal isn’t to hijack the space with a sales pitch. It’s to **add value, spark curiosity, and open a dialogue**.

Here’s a proven engagement framework:

### ✅ The 3R Method for Spaces Engagement

| Stage | Action | Example |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Recognize** | Acknowledge the speaker or topic | “Great point on pricing transparency, @SpeakerName!” |
| **Relate** | Share a relevant insight | “We’ve seen similar challenges with [Company]—here’s how we helped them...” |
| **Redirect** | Gently move the conversation | “Would love to hear your thoughts—let’s DM!” |

> ⚠️ **Avoid**: Pitching directly in the room. You’ll get muted or ignored. Instead, **plant a seed**—then follow up **1:1**.

### 🔁 Post-Space Follow-Up: The Warm Conversion Path

Once a prospect engages (even with a like or retweet), trigger a **contextual, timely follow-up**:

> “Hey [Name], really enjoyed your question in @Speaker’s Space on Tuesday about onboarding. We’ve helped [Similar Company] cut time to value by 40%. Would you be open to a 10-min chat on how we do it?”

📌 **Timing is critical**: Reach out within **24–48 hours** while the conversation is fresh.

Not every high-intent prospect will join live. But they’ll still engage with **repurposed content**.

- **Identify pain points** → Feed into ABM lists or ad targeting

Example:

> - Cold email body
> - LinkedIn carousel
> - Retargeting ad to visitors who watched >30s

📈 **Result**: 3x higher reply rates on emails that reference a shared Spaces moment.
