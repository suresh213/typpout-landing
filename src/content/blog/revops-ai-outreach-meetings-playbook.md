## The 4-Phase AI Outreach Playbook for RevOps Teams

This isn’t theory. It’s the playbook we’ve refined at Typpout to help RevOps teams **consistently hit 100+ meetings/month**. Let’s break it down.

---

### Phase 1: Data Waterfall — Build a Real-Time, Intent-Driven ICP

**Problem:** Your ideal customer profile (ICP) is outdated before it’s even built.

**Solution:** A **real-time data waterfall** that pulls from multiple intent signals.

#### How to Build It:
1. **Layer 1: Firmographics**
   Pull data from tools like Apollo, ZoomInfo, or Clearbit:
   - Industry, company size, tech stack, funding stage
   - Job titles of key decision-makers (e.g., CTO, VP of Sales)

2. **Layer 2: Behavioral Signals**
   Use tools like **Typpout’s social listening layer** to detect:
   - Website visits (especially pricing/trial pages)
   - LinkedIn engagement (comments, shares, profile visits)
   - Job postings (hiring for roles you target)

3. **Layer 3: Purchase Intent**
   Integrate with intent data platforms (e.g., Bombora, Demandbase) to flag accounts actively researching solutions like yours.

**Pro Tip:** At Typpout, we combine these layers into a **dynamic ICP score** that ranks accounts by readiness. This ensures your AI outreach only targets accounts with **high intent** — not just "good fits."

---

### Phase 2: AI-Powered Outreach — From Cold to Conversation

Now that you have a **real-time, intent-driven list**, it’s time to engage.

#### Key Components:
| **Component** | **Traditional Approach** | **AI-Driven Approach** |
|---------------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Messaging | Static templates | AI-generated, dynamic messaging with A/B testing |
| Personalization | Merge tags (e.g., {{FirstName}}) | Contextual hooks based on intent signals (e.g., "I saw you visited our pricing page") |
| Timing | Manual scheduling | AI optimizes send times based on recipient behavior |
| Follow-ups | Fixed cadence | AI adapts follow-up messages based on reply patterns |

#### Actionable Steps:
1. **Use AI to Generate Messaging**
   Tools like **Typpout’s AI Outreach Engine** can:
   - Analyze past high-performing emails
   - Generate personalized subject lines and body copy
   - Adjust tone based on recipient (e.g., technical vs. business-focused)

2. **Automate Multi-Channel Outreach**
   Don’t just send emails. Layer in:
   - LinkedIn InMails (AI-crafted, personalized)
   - Twitter/X replies (real-time engagement)
   - Cold calls (AI-generated scripts, but human-delivered)

3. **A/B Test Everything**
   AI doesn’t just send — it **learns**. Test:
   - Subject lines (e.g., "Quick question about [Company]’s [Pain Point]")
   - Hooks (industry-specific vs. pain-point-specific)
   - CTAs (meeting link vs. "Would you be open to a quick chat?")

**Pro Tip:** At Typpout, we’ve seen **3x higher reply rates** when AI personalizes based on **real-time intent signals** vs. static templates.

---

### Phase 3: AI Reply Handling — Turn Responses into Meetings

Here’s where most RevOps teams fail: **They spend hours sifting through replies**, only to book a handful of meetings.

AI changes that.

#### How AI Handles Replies:
1. **Categorization**
   AI sorts replies into:
   - "Interested" (e.g., "Yes, let’s chat")
   - "Objection" (e.g., "We’re not ready")
   - "Out of Office" (self-explanatory)
   - "Not Interested" (e.g., "We already use a competitor")

2. **Automated Next Steps**
   - For "Interested": Book meeting via Calendly link
   - For "Objection": AI crafts a response (e.g., "I get that. How about a 10-minute call to explore if this makes sense?")
   - For "Not Interested": Add to nurture sequence

3. **Handoff to SDRs**
   Only escalate human review for **high-intent accounts** (e.g., those with budget approval already signaled).

**Example:**
> **Reply:** "We’re evaluating solutions like yours. Can you share pricing?"
> **AI Response:** "Absolutely! How about a 15-minute call tomorrow to walk you through our pricing and answer any questions? [Calendly Link]"
> **Outcome:** Meeting booked. No human intervention needed.

---

### Phase 4: Real-Time Optimization — The AI Feedback Loop

AI-driven outreach isn’t a "set and forget" tool. It’s a **living system** that improves with every interaction.

#### How to Optimize:
1. **Track KPIs in Real Time**
   Monitor:
   - Open rates (are your subject lines working?)
   - Reply rates (is your messaging resonating?)
   - Meeting booking rate (is your AI converting replies?)
   - Pipeline velocity (is your outreach accelerating deals?)

2. **Adjust Based on Data**
   - If open rates drop, AI suggests new subject lines.
   - If replies stall, AI tests new hooks.
   - If meetings aren’t booking, AI adjusts CTAs or timing.

3. **Feed Insights Back to Marketing**
   RevOps isn’t siloed. Share data with marketing to:
   - Refine ICP (e.g., "Accounts visiting our pricing page are 5x more likely to book")
   - Optimize content (e.g., "CTAs like ‘See how we helped [Similar Company]’ convert 40% better")
