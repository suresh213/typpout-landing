Lusha carved its niche as the sales team's fastest path to direct dial phone numbers. While competitors focused on building massive databases or all-in-one platforms, Lusha kept things simple: install the Chrome extension, visit a LinkedIn profile, and get verified phone numbers and email addresses in seconds. In 2026, Lusha has expanded beyond its extension roots, but phone data remains its defining strength.

## What Lusha Does Best

**Phone Number Quality:** Lusha consistently delivers higher mobile phone accuracy than competitors like Apollo.io or Seamless.ai. The platform uses community-verified data, proprietary sourcing partnerships, and real-time validation to maintain mobile accuracy rates above 75% — significantly higher than the 30-45% typical of larger database platforms.

**Chrome Extension Speed:** The Lusha extension is the fastest contact reveal tool on the market. Visit a LinkedIn profile, click the extension, and see phone numbers and email addresses in under 2 seconds. No page reloads, no sidebar delays, no loading spinners. For SDRs making 50-100 calls per day, this speed directly impacts productivity.

**CRM Enrichment:** Lusha's bulk enrichment feature lets you upload CRM exports and fill in missing phone numbers and email addresses across your entire database. This is valuable for RevOps teams cleaning up messy CRM data that has decayed over time.

**Compliance Features:** Lusha offers GDPR and CCPA compliance tools, including opt-out management and Do-Not-Call list checking. For teams selling into regulated markets, these features reduce legal risk.

## Where Lusha Falls Short

### Credit Consumption Speed

Lusha's credit-based pricing means each contact reveal consumes credits regardless of quality. At $49/month for 160 credits, aggressive prospectors exhaust their allocation within days. The per-credit cost ($0.31 on the entry plan) is among the highest in the market for contact data.

### Email Accuracy Trails Specialists

While Lusha provides email addresses, independent benchmarks show its email deliverable rate runs 60-75% — below Hunter.io's 70-85% range. For teams that prioritize email outreach over phone calls, Lusha's email data requires secondary verification.

### No Sequencing or Outreach Tools

Lusha is a pure data tool. After revealing contacts, you must export them to a separate platform (Instantly, Lemlist, Outreach) to actually send messages. This creates a fragmented workflow with manual CSV transfers between systems.

### Limited Intent Data

Lusha provides contact data with basic firmographic filters but no meaningful buying intent signals. You know a person's phone number and email, but you have no indication whether they are currently evaluating solutions in your category.

## The Verdict

Lusha excels at one thing: getting you phone numbers quickly. If cold calling is your primary outbound channel and you need mobile numbers for mid-level professionals and executives, Lusha delivers better phone data than most competitors at an accessible price point.
