Instagram is traditionally seen as a B2C platform, but it is increasingly used for B2B sales, particularly by agencies, creative services, and startup founders.

## 1. Optimize Your Profile

Treat your Instagram bio as a landing page. Clearly state who you help, how you help them, and include a link to book a call.

## 2. Monitor Competitor and Industry Keywords

Follow key hashtags and competitors in your space. Watch for users commenting on their posts with questions or complaints, as these are high-intent buying signals.
