### Step 2: Analyze the Connection’s Influence (Not Just Existence)

Not all shared connections are equal.

A warm intro from the **CEO of your prospect’s company** is gold.
A cold intro from a junior employee? Not so much.

Use AI to **score shared connections** based on:

| Factor | Weight | Example |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Role | 30% | CEO > VP > Manager |
| Recency | 25% | Connected last week > 5 years ago |
| Frequency | 20% | Multiple interactions |
| Shared Industry | 15% | Same sector = higher trust |
| Mutual Acquaintances | 10% | More shared contacts = stronger tie |

**AI Tip:**
Typpout’s algorithm ranks shared connections from **Tier 1 (warmest)** to **Tier 3 (cooler)**. Prioritize Tier 1 for outreach.

### Step 3: Personalize the Script (AI + Human Touch)

Now, the magic: the **AI-generated shared connection outreach script**.

Here’s a **high-converting template** we’ve tested across SaaS, fintech, and AI startups:

---

### LinkedIn Connection Request (Tier 1 Shared Connection)

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I noticed you’re connected with [Shared Connection Name]—they mentioned you’re leading [specific project/initiative].
>
> We recently helped [similar company] reduce [pain point] by [result]. I’d love to share how we did it and see if it’s relevant to your work.
>
> No pressure—just a quick 15-minute chat. Would you be open to connecting?
>
> —[Your Name]

🔹 **Why it works:**
- Mentions a **real shared connection** by name
- References a **specific pain point** (not generic)
- Offers **value upfront**
- Keeps it **short and low-pressure**

---

### Follow-Up Email (Sent 3 Days Later)

> Subject: Quick question about [Shared Connection]

> Hi [First Name],

> [Shared Connection Name] and I were just talking about [industry trend]—specifically how [pain point] is impacting teams like yours.

> I saw you’re at [Company], which is doing great work in [specific area]. We’ve helped similar orgs [achieve X result] in just [timeframe].

> Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call to explore if this could help your team?

> —[Your Name]
> [Your Title]
> Typpout
> [Calendly Link]

🔹 **Pro Tips:**
- Use **AI to detect prospect pain points** from their LinkedIn posts or job descriptions
- Reference a **real shared connection** (e.g., “I saw you’re connected with [Name]—they’re doing amazing work at [Company]”)
- Include a **calendly link**—meeting bookings increase by 50% when you remove friction

---

### SMS Follow-Up (If No Response After 7 Days)

> Hey [First Name]! [Shared Connection Name] recommended I reach out—we helped [similar company] [achieve X]. Quick 15-min call? Reply YES or book here: [link].

🔹 **Why SMS works:**
- High open rates (98%)
- Feels personal and urgent
- Works even if email is ignored
