| Metric | B2B Reels Performance | Why It Matters |

## Step 1: Reverse-Engineer the Buyer Journey Using Reels

Most B2B brands post Reels about their product features or company culture. That’s like showing up to a networking event and handing out brochures before you’ve even introduced yourself.

To attract **high-intent buyers**, your Reels must align with the **research phase** of the B2B buyer journey.

### 🔍 The 3-Stage B2B Reels Funnel

| Stage | Buyer Mindset | Reel Strategy | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| **Awareness** | “I have a problem” | Educate, simplify, or debunk | “3 Signs Your SaaS is Losing Money to Churn” |
| **Consideration** | “I’m comparing solutions” | Compare, demo, or social proof | “How We Cut Onboarding Time by 50% with [Tool]” |
| **Decision** | “I’m ready to buy” | Testimonial, case study, or offer | “Book a demo—limited slots this month” |

**Actionable Tip:** Use **Instagram Insights** to see which Reels get the most **profile visits** and **link clicks**. These are your highest-intent signals.

## Step 2: Turn Viewers into Leads with the "Reels-to-Reply" Framework

Once you’ve captured attention, the next step is **conversion**. Here’s how to turn passive viewers into active leads:

### ✅ The 5-Second Hook Formula

Your first three seconds must answer:
> “Does this Reel matter to me?”

Examples:
- **Problem:** “Are you wasting $10K/month on unused SaaS licenses?”
- **Question:** “What’s the #1 mistake in B2B email sequences?”
- **Promise:** “The 90-second trick to get replies from cold leads”

> 📌 **Pro Tip:** Use **text overlays** (Instagram’s built-in tool) with bold, high-contrast fonts. 80% of users watch Reels on mute.

### 🔗 The Link-in-Bio Strategy (Works for B2B!)

Instagram doesn’t allow clickable links in captions (unless you’re verified). So growth teams use:

| Tool | How It Works | Best For |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Linktree / Carrd** | One link to landing page, calendar, or demo | Simple lead gen |
| **Typpout** | AI-powered reply-to-link automation | Full-funnel tracking |
| **Calendly** | Direct meeting booking | High-intent buyers |

**Pro Tip:** Add a **CTA in the caption** like:
> “🔗 Grab our free Churn Audit template (link in bio)”

Then **pin the comment** with the direct link.
