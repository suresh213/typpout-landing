## The Problem with Traditional Lead Scoring: It’s Blind to Urgency

Most RevOps teams rely on **demand gen funnels** where leads are scored based on:
- **Firmographics** (company size, industry, tech stack).
- **Past behavior** (website visits, content downloads, email opens).
- **Engagement scoring** (webinar attendance, demo requests).

**The issue?** These signals are **reactive**, not **proactive**. By the time a lead fills out a form or attends a webinar, they’ve already been exposed to your competitors. Meanwhile, **high-intent buyers** who mention your product on social media—without ever filling out a form—are **invisible to your scoring model**.

### The Cost of Missing Social Signals
| Scenario | Traditional Lead Scoring | AI-Powered Social Scoring |
|----------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Buyer Behavior** | Fills out a form after 14 days of research | Posts on LinkedIn: *“Struggling with [your product’s pain point]—any recommendations?”* |
| **Your Response** | Cold email after form submission (low context) | Instant AI reply with a case study (high context) |
| **Outcome** | 5% conversion rate | 35% conversion rate |

**The gap?** Traditional models miss **urgent, high-intent signals** because they only track *your* owned channels. Social conversations happen **where your buyers actually talk**—and AI can tap into that.

## How AI-Powered Lead Scoring from Social Conversations Works

AI-powered social listening doesn’t just track mentions of your brand—it **scores conversations based on intent, authority, and urgency**. Here’s how it breaks down:

### 1. **Real-Time Social Listening**
AI tools (like Typpout) monitor:
- **Direct mentions** (e.g., *“Does anyone use [Your Product] for X?”*).
- **Indirect signals** (e.g., competitors’ customers complaining about a gap your product fills).
- **Job postings** (e.g., *“Hiring a RevOps leader with experience in [your niche]”*).

**Example:** A prospect tweets:
> *“Our current CRM can’t handle multi-touch attribution. Any suggestions?”*

**Typpout’s AI instantly:**
✅ Detects the pain point (attribution tracking).
✅ Scores the lead as **high intent** (explicit need + public ask).
✅ Triggers a **personalized reply** (e.g., *“We helped [Similar Company] reduce attribution errors by 40%—here’s how”*).

### 2. **Intent & Authority Scoring**
Not all social signals are equal. AI evaluates:
| Factor | Weight | Example |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Buying Language** | 30% | *“Ready to switch,” “Need a solution ASAP”* |
| **Job Role** | 25% | Director of Sales/RevOps > Individual Contributor |
| **Engagement Level** | 20% | Active in industry groups vs. passive poster |
| **Mentions Competitors** | 15% | *“Frustrated with [Competitor]’s pricing”* |
| **Follower Count** | 10% | High follower = higher influence |

**Result:** A **weighted lead score** that prioritizes the most valuable prospects.

### 3. **Automated Outreach (Without the Creep Factor)**
The magic happens when AI doesn’t just *listen*—it **acts**:
- **Instant Replies:** AI drafts contextual responses (e.g., case studies, ROI calculators) based on the conversation.
- **Data Enrichment:** Pulls contact info from LinkedIn/email to sync with your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot).
- **Meeting Booking:** Automatically schedules demos for high-scoring leads.

**Why this works:**
- **No cold outreach** → Warm, contextual engagement.
- **No manual research** → AI does it in milliseconds.
- **No wasted time** → Only high-intent leads reach your reps.

## Step-by-Step: Implementing AI-Powered Lead Scoring in Your GTM Motion

Here’s how to roll this out in **30 days or less**:

### **Phase 1: Set Up Your AI Listening Stack (Week 1)**
1. **Choose a tool** with real-time social listening + CRM integration (e.g., Typpout, Apollo, Demandbase).
2. **Define your keywords** (brand names, competitors, pain points).
3. **Set up alerts** for high-intent phrases (e.g., *“looking for,” “recommend,” “switching from”*).

**Pro Tip:** Use **Boolean search** to narrow signals:
```
("need a solution" OR "ready to buy" OR "not happy with [Competitor]") AND ("RevOps" OR "sales ops")
```

### **Phase 2: Build Your AI Scoring Model (Week 2)**
1. **Assign weights** to signals (use the table above as a starting point).
2. **Integrate with your CRM** (e.g., HubSpot workflows triggered by high scores).

### **Phase 3: Automate Outreach & Follow-Up (Week 3-4)**
1. **Create AI reply templates** for common pain points (e.g., attribution tracking, CRM integrations).
2. **Set up automated sequences** (e.g., first reply → case study → meeting invite).
3. **Track performance** in a dashboard (conversion rates, reply times, revenue generated).
