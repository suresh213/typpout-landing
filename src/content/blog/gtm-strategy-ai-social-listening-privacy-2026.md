## The AI Social Listening Stack: What to Track in 2026

AI social listening in 2026 isn’t about scraping tweets. It’s about **extracting intent from unstructured data**, **classifying signals with NLP**, and **feeding actionable insights into your GTM motion**.

Here’s what your AI listener should monitor:

### 🔍 1. **Intent Signals Across Channels**
- **Public Forums:** Reddit, Quora, Indie Hackers
- **Private Communities:** Slack, Discord, Gitter (with opt-in compliance)
- **Review Platforms:** G2, Capterra, TrustRadius (real-time sentiment)
- **GitHub & Dev Communities:** Stack Overflow, Dev.to, GitHub Discussions

- Detect **buyer intent keywords** (“need a CRM”, “migrating from Salesforce”)
- Classify **sentiment** (frustration, curiosity, urgency)
- Identify **authority level** (decision-maker vs. researcher)
- Flag **compliance risks** (PII exposure, unsolicited contact)

## Privacy Compliance in 2026: Don’t Get Caught Off Guard

Privacy isn’t optional. Even if you’re not in Europe, state laws like **California’s CPRA**, **Virginia’s CDPA**, and **Canada’s CPPA** are tightening.

### 🔐 Must-Do Compliance Actions:
| Action | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|
| **Consent-Based Data Collection** | Only collect data from public or opt-in sources |
| **Automatic PII Redaction** | Scrub names, emails, phone numbers from logs |
| **Right to Be Forgotten** | Allow users to delete their data across systems |
| **Audit Trails** | Document data sources, processing, and sharing |
| **Vendor Due Diligence** | Ensure all tools (listeners, CRMs) are compliant |
