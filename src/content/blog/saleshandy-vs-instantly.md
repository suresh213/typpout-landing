Saleshandy and Instantly are the two most direct competitors in the unlimited-account cold email market. Both offer unlimited email connections, built-in warmup, and native B2B databases. But they differ meaningfully in pricing, feature depth, and target audience. This comparison breaks down the specifics to help you choose.

## Feature Comparison

| Feature | Saleshandy | Instantly |
|---|---|---|
| **Unlimited email accounts** | ✅ All plans | ✅ All plans |
| **Entry price** | $25/mo | $37/mo |
| **Native lead database** | ✅ 700M+ profiles | ✅ B2B Lead Finder (separate pricing) |
| **Built-in email verification** | ✅ Included | ❌ External tool needed |
| **Email warmup** | ✅ Included | ✅ Larger warmup network |
| **White-label agency portal** | ✅ Full white-label | ❌ Limited agency features |
| **AI reply categorization** | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Unified inbox** | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Spintax support** | ✅ | ✅ |
| **A/B testing** | ✅ | ✅ Higher plans |

### Where Saleshandy Wins

**Lower entry price:** $25/month vs $37/month for comparable base features. For budget-conscious teams, the $144/year savings adds up.

**Integrated lead database:** Saleshandy includes its 700M+ contact database within the platform at no additional cost (up to plan limits). Instantly charges separately for its B2B Lead Finder ($47-197/month), making the total cost for data + sending higher.

**Built-in verification:** Email verification is included in Saleshandy, eliminating the $16-50/month expense of ZeroBounce or NeverBounce that Instantly users typically incur.

**Agency portal:** Full white-label dashboard for agencies. Instantly's agency features are more limited, often requiring workarounds.

### Where Instantly Wins

**Larger warmup network:** More users in Instantly's warmup network means faster reputation building for new domains. For teams launching many new sending domains simultaneously, this matters.

**More polished UX:** Instantly's interface is cleaner and more intuitive. Campaign creation, analytics dashboards, and inbox management feel more refined.

**Ecosystem and integrations:** Instantly has a larger partner ecosystem and more third-party integrations, particularly with CRM and enrichment tools.

**Market trust:** Instantly has been in the unlimited-account market longer and has more case studies, community content, and third-party reviews. For risk-averse buyers, Instantly's track record provides confidence.

## Pricing Deep Dive

### Sending Platform Only

| Plan Level | Saleshandy | Instantly |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | $25/mo — 2,000 prospects | $37/mo — 1,000 active leads |
| Mid | $74/mo — 30,000 prospects | $97/mo — 25,000 active leads |
| Scale | $149/mo — 60,000 prospects | $358/mo — 500,000 active leads |

### Sending + Data (Total Platform Cost)

| Need | Saleshandy Total | Instantly Total |
|---|---|---|
| Entry + data | $25/mo (included) | $84/mo (sending + Lead Finder) |
| Mid + data | $74/mo (included) | $194/mo (sending + Lead Finder) |
| Scale + data | $149/mo (included) | $555/mo (sending + Lead Finder) |

When you include data costs, Saleshandy's total platform expense is significantly lower at every tier. This makes Saleshandy the clear budget winner for teams that need both data and sending infrastructure.

**Choose Saleshandy** if budget efficiency is your top priority, you need a built-in database and verification, and you run an agency needing white-label features.

**Choose Instantly** if you value product polish, a mature warmup network, and a larger community ecosystem — and are comfortable paying separately for data.
