Following up is essential for sales success, but sending too many automated emails too quickly will land your domain in the spam folder.

## 1. Space Out Your Follow-Ups

Avoid sending daily follow-ups. A standard sequence should feature:
* **Day 1:** Initial outreach
* **Day 4:** Second touch (different context)
* **Day 8:** Third touch (value add, e.g., case study)
* **Day 14:** Breakup email (asking to close file)

## 2. Vary Your Content

Do not send the same message repeatedly. Each follow-up should offer fresh value, such as a helpful resource, a case study, or a diagnostic question.
