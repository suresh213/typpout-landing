Findymail has carved a niche as the email finder that prioritizes deliverability above all else. While competitors race to offer the largest databases or the most features, Findymail makes a simple promise: sub-5% bounce rates or your credits back. For outbound teams that have been burned by high bounce rates damaging their sender reputation, this guarantee is a compelling differentiator.

## The Core Promise: Sub-5% Bounce Guarantee

Findymail's verification-first approach means it only returns email addresses that have passed rigorous deliverability checks. The platform runs multi-step verification including SMTP validation, mailbox existence confirmation, and catch-all domain detection before marking an email as "verified." If your campaign using Findymail-verified emails exceeds a 5% bounce rate, the company credits your account.

This guarantee addresses the biggest pain point with cheaper email finders: finding emails is easy; finding emails that actually reach inboxes is hard.

## Features

**Sales Navigator Integration:** Findymail integrates directly with LinkedIn Sales Navigator to find verified emails for profiles in your search results. The integration exports enriched contacts to your outreach tool without manual CSV work.

**Bulk Enrichment:** Upload CSV files with names and company domains for batch email finding and verification. Each result comes with a verification confidence score.

**Google Sheets Add-on:** Enrich spreadsheet rows directly within Google Sheets — useful for teams that build prospect lists in spreadsheets before importing to CRM.

**API Access:** RESTful API for integrating Findymail into custom workflows, CRM enrichment pipelines, and automation platforms like Clay or Make.

## Limitations

**No Phone Data:** Findymail is email-only. No phone numbers, no mobile data. For multi-channel teams, you need a separate phone data provider.

**Smaller Database:** Findymail's verification-first approach means its effective database is smaller than Apollo's or Seamless.ai's. The platform prioritizes accuracy over coverage, which means lower match rates for niche industries or small companies.

**No Outreach Tools:** No sequencing, no warmup, no campaign sending. Findymail is a pure data quality tool.

**Premium Pricing:**

| Plan | Monthly | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $49 | 1,000 |
| Standard | $99 | 5,000 |

At $0.017-0.049 per credit, Findymail's per-email cost is competitive. But the limited feature set means you are paying for one thing: email verification quality.

## When Findymail Wins

Choose Findymail when **email deliverability is your absolute top priority** and you have already experienced domain reputation damage from high bounce rates. The sub-5% guarantee provides confidence that cheaper tools cannot match.
