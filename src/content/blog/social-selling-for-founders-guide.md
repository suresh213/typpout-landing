For early-stage startup founders, building a sales pipeline is critical but challenging. Traditional outbound sales are expensive and time-consuming, while founder-led social selling is highly effective.

## 1. Share Your Build Journey

Post updates about what you are building, the challenges you are facing, and the lessons you have learned. This builds trust and positions you as an expert in your space.

## 2. Monitor Social Channels for Pain Points

Listen to what your target audience is complaining about. When you see a post discussing a problem your startup solves, reply with a helpful comment.
