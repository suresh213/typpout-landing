Outreach.io and Salesloft are the two dominant enterprise Sales Engagement Platforms (SEPs), and choosing between them often comes down to feature nuances, CRM integration preferences, and pricing. Neither publishes pricing transparently — both require you to go through a sales process for a custom quote. Based on aggregated user reports and industry data from 2026, here is what each platform actually costs.

## Estimated Pricing Comparison (2026)

| Pricing Component | Outreach.io | Salesloft |
|---|---|---|
| Per-user cost (estimated) | $100-150/user/month | $100-125/user/month |
| Minimum seats | 5-10 users typical | 5-10 users typical |
| Contract terms | Annual (12-24 months) | Annual (12-24 months) |
| Setup/onboarding fees | $1,000-5,000 (varies) | $1,000-3,000 (varies) |

## The Value Question

Both platforms deliver measurable value for enterprise sales teams — improved rep productivity, consistent outbound execution, and pipeline visibility. The question is whether your team's size, deal values, and operational complexity justify the investment.

### Enterprise SEPs Make Sense When:
- Your team has 15+ reps running structured outbound programs.
- Average deal values exceed $25,000.
- Sales cycle complexity requires multi-touch, multi-channel cadences.
- Revenue forecasting and deal intelligence are strategic priorities for leadership.
- You have dedicated RevOps or Sales Ops staff to manage the platform.

### Enterprise SEPs Are Overkill When:
- Your team has fewer than 10 salespeople.
- You are still iterating on your ICP and outbound messaging.
- Your deals close in under 30 days.
- You do not have a dedicated admin to configure and maintain the platform.
- Your annual tool budget is under $15,000.
