## 🤖 How AI-Powered Lead Qualification Works (With Real Data)

AI lead qualification doesn’t guess. It **learns**.

It analyzes **hundreds of signals** in real time—**not just firmographics, but behavioral, intent-based, and even sentiment data**—to determine whether a lead is truly ready to buy.

### The AI Qualification Stack: What’s Under the Hood?

| Layer | Data Source | AI Technique | Outcome |
|-------|-------------|--------------|--------|
| **Intent Detection** | Website visits, content engagement, search queries | NLP + Predictive modeling | Identifies **high-intent buyers** |
| **Behavioral Scoring** | Click patterns, email opens, time on page | Reinforcement learning | Ranks leads by **purchase likelihood** |
| **Firmographic Alignment** | Company size, tech stack, funding stage | Clustering algorithms | Ensures **ideal customer profile (ICP) fit** |
| **Sentiment Analysis** | Social media, support tickets, review sites | Sentiment scoring | Detects **urgency or pain points** |
| **Predictive Routing** | Historical close rates, rep performance | Decision trees | Routes leads to **best-fit rep** |

This isn’t sci-fi. It’s **real-time, automated qualification** powered by AI models trained on your best customers.
