When doing outbound sales, direct mobile numbers are crucial for booking meetings. Lusha and Seamless AI are two popular tools for finding contact details, but they use different methods.

## Seamless AI: Real-Time Sourcing

Seamless AI uses a search engine model to find contact details in real time from public web data. It is highly effective for finding up-to-date B2B emails and phone numbers, but data quality can vary.

## Lusha: Database Accuracy

Lusha relies on a pre-verified database. It generally has higher accuracy rates, particularly for direct mobile phone numbers, but may have less coverage in niche industries compared to Seamless AI.
