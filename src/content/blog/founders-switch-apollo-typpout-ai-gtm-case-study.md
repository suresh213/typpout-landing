## The GTM Problem Apollo Wasn’t Built to Solve

Apollo.io launched as a powerful sales intelligence and engagement platform. It excels at:
- Contact and company database enrichment
- Email sequencing and cadence automation
- Basic intent data from third-party sources

But here’s the catch: **Apollo is a transactional tool built for the pre-AI era**.

Founders today face three critical challenges that Apollo can’t fully address:

1. **Static, Outdated Data**
   Apollo’s data decays at ~3% per month. That means over 30% of your contact list could be inaccurate in a year. Founders waste time reaching out to wrong roles, outdated emails, or defunct companies.

2. **No Real-Time Intent Signals**
   Apollo relies on third-party intent data (e.g., Bombora) that’s delayed, diluted, and often irrelevant. In a world where buyers signal intent in real time—on LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit, or even product communities—founders need **live, contextual insights**.

3. **Generic Outreach at Scale**
   Apollo enables automation, but not intelligence. Sending the same cold email to 5,000 prospects doesn’t scale trust—it scales spam. Founders today need **personalized, context-aware messaging** that adapts to buyer behavior.

> “We were hitting 30% reply rates on Apollo, but only 2% of those replies were qualified. We realized we weren’t solving the *right* problem—we were just spamming more people.” — **Sarah Chen, CEO of a Series B SaaS startup**
