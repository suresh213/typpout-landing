## The GTM Problem: Why Traditional Meeting Booking Fails in 2026

### 1. **The Scheduling Bottleneck**
Even in 2026, sales teams spend **17% of their time** just scheduling meetings (source: Harvard Business Review). This inefficiency is exacerbated by:
- **Time zone mismatches** between global teams and prospects.
- **Overlapping calendars** that require constant rescheduling.
- **Low response rates** from buyers who ignore generic meeting invites.

### 2. **The Personalization Paradox**
Buyers expect **hyper-personalized** outreach. Yet, most teams rely on:
- **Static email templates** that fail to adapt to prospect behavior.
- **Manual segmentation** that can’t keep up with dynamic buyer intent.
- **Delayed follow-ups** that miss the window of opportunity.

### 3. **The Data Deluge Problem**
GTM teams are drowning in data—CRM records, website interactions, social signals—but struggle to **translate insights into action**. Without AI, this data remains **underutilized**, leading to:
- **Missed opportunities** due to slow response times.
- **Inefficient prioritization** of high-intent leads.
- **High customer acquisition costs (CAC)** from poorly targeted outreach.

### 4. **The Revenue Leakage Crisis**
Every minute spent on manual scheduling is a minute **not spent closing deals**. The result?
- **Longer sales cycles** (average B2B sales cycle is now **102 days**).
- **Lower win rates** due to lost momentum.
- **Stagnant pipeline growth** despite increased ad spend.
