## **How AI Detects Intent in Instagram Comments (Step-by-Step)**

Not all comments are equal. AI helps you **filter noise from signal** using three layers of intelligence:

### **1. Sentiment & Intent Scoring**

AI models analyze comments in real time and assign scores based on:

- **Purchase intent keywords**: “buy”, “pricing”, “demo”, “free trial”
- **Urgency**: “ASAP”, “immediately”, “urgent”
- **Authority signals**: “I’m a founder”, “CTO at [Company]”
- **Pain points**: “struggling with”, “need a solution for”

For example:

> “We’re a 50-person SaaS company looking for a better CRM. Do you offer integrations with HubSpot? @typpout” → **High Intent (Score: 94/100)**

> “Great post!” → **Low Intent (Score: 5/100)**

### **2. Entity & Context Recognition**

AI doesn’t just look at words—it understands **context**.

- Recognizes company names, job titles, and tech stacks
- Detects emoji intent (e.g., 🔥 = high interest, 😅 = casual)
- Maps comments to product pages, pricing pages, or support tickets

### **3. Real-Time Qualification**

Using NLP and historical data, AI predicts:

- Who is most likely to book a meeting?
- What’s their role and company size?
- What objection might they have?

This turns a comment like:

> “Is this GDPR compliant? @typpout”

Into a **qualified lead** with a known objection: **Compliance concern**.

## **The 5-Step AI Workflow to Turn Comments into High-Intent Leads**

Here’s how top growth teams implement this at scale:

### **Step 1: Capture Every Comment in Real Time**

Use a **social listening tool** that integrates with Instagram’s API (or webhooks for public comments).

✅ **What to capture**:
- Comment text
- Username
- Timestamp
- Post URL
- Media type (image, video, reel)
- Engagement count

❌ **What to ignore**:
- Spam or bot comments
- Emoji-only responses
- Comments from non-target geos

> 🔧 **Pro Tip**: Use **Typpout’s Social Waterfall™** to ingest comments from Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, and TikTok in one unified stream—with AI-powered deduplication and enrichment.

---

### **Step 2: Score & Prioritize with AI**

Apply an **intent scoring model** (like Typpout’s AI Intent Engine) to each comment.

**Scoring Framework Example**:

| Factor | Weight | Example |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Keyword Match | 30% | “pricing”, “demo” → high score |
| Role Mention | 20% | “Founder”, “CEO”, “CTO” → priority |
| Company Size Clue | 15% | “50-person team”, “Series A” → scaled outreach |
| Urgency | 10% | “ASAP”, “immediately” → fast response |
| Emoji Sentiment | 5% | 🔥🚀 → boost score |
| Negative Tone | -20% | “Worst support ever” → disqualify |

**Result**: Only comments scoring **>75/100** are routed to sales.

### **Step 3: Automate Personalized Outreach**

Once a high-intent lead is detected:

1. **Auto-reply** with a **contextual message** (not a generic DM)
   > “Hey [Name], great question about GDPR compliance! We actually just published a whitepaper on how Typpout handles data privacy—want me to send it over? Also, we’ve helped SaaS teams like yours reduce onboarding time by 40%. Can I book a quick 10-min call to show you how?”

2. **Trigger follow-up sequences** based on intent:
   - Pricing → send case study + pricing page

   - “Integration with HubSpot” → send integration doc + demo invite
