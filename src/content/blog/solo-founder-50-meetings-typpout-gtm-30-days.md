## Case Study: Alex’s 30-Day GTM Sprint (Step-by-Step)

Alex was launching **FlowSync**, a no-code workflow automation tool for mid-market SaaS teams.

He had:
✔ A working MVP
✔ A vague ICP: “SaaS ops managers at companies with 50–500 employees”
✔ $3k budget for GTM
✔ Zero sales team

He needed meetings fast.

### 🚀 Week 1: Define ICP & Listening Strategy

Alex used Typpout to define his **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)** with precision:

```yaml
ICP:
  Role: "SaaS Operations Manager" OR "Revenue Operations Lead"
  Industry: SaaS, Fintech, MarTech
  Company Size: 50–500 employees
  Tech Stack: Uses Zapier, Airtable, or custom scripts
  Pain Point: "Manual workflows slow down sales cycles"
```

He set up **real-time alerts** in Typpout for keywords:
- "Zapier too slow"
- "Salesforce automation bottleneck"
- "We need better workflow tools"
- "Ops team overwhelmed"

Within **24 hours**, Typpout surfaced **47 people** who had posted or commented on these topics.

📌 **Actionable Tip**: Use negative keywords to filter out noise (e.g., exclude “I love Zapier”).

---

### 📧 Week 2: Hyper-Personalized Outreach at Scale

Typpout’s AI crafted **individualized messages** based on each prospect’s recent posts.

Example:

> **Prospect Post**: “Our sales team spends 20 hours/week manually updating Salesforce. This can’t be sustainable.”
> **Typpout AI Draft**:
> > Hi [Name], saw your post about Salesforce time sinks. We’re helping teams like yours cut that by 70% with FlowSync. Would love to show you how—30-min demo next week?
> > https://flowsync.com/demo

Typpout sent **150 personalized emails** + **150 LinkedIn messages** over 5 days.

📌 **Why This Works**:
- Not generic. Based on *their* words.
- Addresses pain directly.
- Offers immediate value (demo).

---

### 🤖 Week 3: AI Handles Replies, Books Meetings

After sending, Typpout monitored replies.

For a reply like:
> “Interesting. How much does this cost?”

Typpout replied:
> “Happy to share pricing after a quick call—here’s my calendar: [Calendly link]”

For a “Not interested”:
> “Understood. If your workflows ever slow you down, feel free to reach out.”

Typpout booked **32 meetings** in 7 days.

📌 **Result**: 21% reply rate—5x higher than cold email averages.
