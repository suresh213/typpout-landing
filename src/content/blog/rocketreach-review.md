RocketReach has built a massive contact database — over 700 million professionals and 35 million companies — making it one of the largest B2B data sources available. The platform is straightforward: search by name, company, job title, or location, and RocketReach returns email addresses, phone numbers, and social profiles. In 2026, it remains popular among recruiters, sales teams, and marketers who need bulk contact access without the complexity of a full sales intelligence platform.

## Core Strengths

**Database Scale:** 700M+ professional profiles is a genuinely large database. RocketReach provides broad coverage across industries, company sizes, and geographies — though depth varies by segment.

**Bulk Lookup:** Upload a CSV with names and companies, and RocketReach returns enriched contact data in batch. This bulk processing capability is valuable for teams building large prospect lists from conference attendees, webinar registrants, or imported CRM records.

**LinkedIn Chrome Extension:** The extension reveals contact information directly on LinkedIn profiles, similar to Lusha and Skrapp. It supports both individual lookups and batch extraction from Sales Navigator search results.

**Personal and Professional Emails:** Unlike many B2B tools that only return professional addresses, RocketReach also surfaces personal email addresses. This is valuable for reaching founders, freelancers, and executives who may not use corporate email for all communications.

## Where RocketReach Falls Short

### Accuracy Variability

RocketReach's email accuracy varies significantly by data type and segment. Professional email accuracy runs 65-78%, but personal email accuracy is lower — and the platform sometimes returns personal emails when professional ones are unavailable, which can feel intrusive for B2B outreach.

### No Outreach Tools

RocketReach is purely a data platform. No sequencing, no email sending, no warmup, no CRM. After finding contacts, you must export and import into a separate outreach tool.

### Credit-Based Pricing

| Plan | Monthly | Lookups |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | $53 | 80/month |
| Pro | $107 | 200/month |
| Ultimate | $269 | 500/month |

At $0.54-0.66 per lookup on entry plans, RocketReach is among the more expensive per-contact data tools. The credit volume at each tier is also conservative compared to competitors.

### No Intent Data

RocketReach provides contact information without any buying signal context. You know how to reach someone but have no indication whether they are interested in your category.

## The Verdict

RocketReach serves **recruiters and marketers** who need reliable contact data for specific individuals and value the personal email access that most B2B tools do not provide. For sales prospecting, it is a decent data source but lacks the outreach tools, intent signals, and workflow automation that modern outbound requires.
