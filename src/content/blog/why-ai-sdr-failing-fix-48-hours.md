## 🔍 The Top 5 Reasons Your AI SDR Is Failing (And How to Spot Them)

Before jumping into fixes, you need to identify *why* your AI SDR is underperforming. Here are the **five most common failure points**, ranked by impact:

| **Failure Point**               | **Symptoms**                              | **Tools to Diagnose**                     |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| **Poor Signal Detection**     | Outreach to wrong personas, outdated lists | CRM data, intent signals, LinkedIn Sales Navigator |
| **Weak Personalization**      | Generic messages, low reply rates         | A/B test tools, reply analysis, tone detectors |
| **Timing & Sequencing Issues**| Messages sent at wrong time, no follow-up | Email tracking, sequence analytics        |
| **Bad Data Hygiene**          | Bounces, spam flags, low deliverability   | Email validation tools, blacklist checks  |
| **Misaligned ICP**            | Leads from wrong industries/roles         | Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) scoring, firmographics |

## 🚨 48-Hour AI SDR Rescue Plan

You now know *what* could be wrong. Now, let’s fix it—**fast**.

### 🕒 Hour 0–2: Audit Your Data & Signals

**Goal:** Ensure your AI SDR is targeting the *right* people at the *right* time.

✅ **Actions:**
- Run a **data audit** of your prospect list:
  - Are they still active on LinkedIn?
  - Do they match your updated ICP?
  - Are their emails valid?
- Use **intent data tools** (e.g., G2, Bombora, Typpout) to detect recent web visits, downloads, or social engagement.
- Filter out accounts with no activity in the last 90 days.

📌 **Quick Fix:** Remove 30–50% of stale or irrelevant leads. Replace them with fresh, intent-rich prospects from real-time social listening.

---

### 🕒 Hour 3–12: Fix Personalization & Timing

**Goal:** Make every message feel human and arrive when your prospect is most receptive.

✅ **Actions:**
- **Personalize at scale using real-time triggers:**
  - Reference recent LinkedIn posts, job changes, or company milestones.
  - Use **dynamic fields** in your AI SDR tool (e.g., [Typpout](/)).
- **Optimize send times** using engagement data:
  - Send emails when prospects are most active (check open/click patterns).
  - Use **multi-channel sequences** (LinkedIn → email → follow-up).
- A/B test subject lines and first lines using a small cohort (50–100 prospects).

📊 **Benchmark:** Aim for a **25%+ reply rate** on your best sequence. If you're below 10%, your messaging or timing is off.

---

### 🕒 Hour 13–24: Clean Up Deliverability & Compliance

**Goal:** Avoid spam filters and ensure your outreach lands in the inbox.

✅ **Actions:**
- Run your email list through a **validation tool** (e.g., NeverBounce, ZeroBounce).
- Remove hard bounces and spam traps.
- Check your **domain reputation** using tools like [Mail-Tester](https://www.mail-tester.com/) or Google Postmaster Tools.
- Ensure your **unsubscribe link is visible** and working.
- Warm up your domain if needed (use gradual email volume increases).

⚠️ **Red Flag:** If your spam complaint rate exceeds **0.1%**, pause and rebuild your list.

---

### 🕒 Hour 25–36: Refine Your ICP & Triggers

**Goal:** Only engage prospects who are *ready* to buy.

✅ **Actions:**
- Update your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) using **behavioral data**:
  - Which industries have the highest conversion?
  - Which roles respond fastest?
  - What triggers (e.g., funding rounds, leadership changes) correlate with meetings?
- Use **predictive scoring** to prioritize warm leads.
- Integrate your AI SDR with **real-time intent platforms** like Typpout to trigger outreach *immediately* when a prospect shows interest.

🎯 **Result:** Focus on the **top 10% of engaged prospects**—they generate 70% of your meetings.

---

### 🕒 Hour 37–48: Launch, Monitor, and Iterate

**Goal:** Deploy your optimized AI SDR and track performance in real time.

✅ **Actions:**
- Deploy your refined sequence to the cleaned list.
- Set up **real-time dashboards** (e.g., Typpout’s AI GTM dashboard) to track:
  - Response rates
  - Meeting bookings
  - Follow-up engagement
- Assign a **human SDR** to handle replies that require nuance or empathy.
- Schedule a **post-campaign review** in 7 days to double down on what works.

📈 **Success Metric:** Aim for **3–5% meeting booking rate** from outbound sequences. If you hit this, your AI SDR is now a high-performing revenue engine.
