## Why Video Comments Are Your Hidden Pipeline Goldmine

Video content on LinkedIn generates **3x more engagement** than static posts. Comments—especially on videos from thought leaders or company pages—are where **warm leads congregate**. These aren’t random passersby; they’re people who’ve already shown interest in your niche.

But not all comments are equal.

| Comment Type | Intent Level | Likelihood to Convert |
|--------------|--------------|------------------------|
| "Great insight!" | Low | 5% |
| "Can you share the data behind slide 12?" | Medium | 30% |
| "This is exactly what we’re struggling with—any chance for a call?" | High | 65% |

The last type? That’s a **hot lead** disguised as a comment.

Yet most teams miss it because:
- They don’t monitor comments in real time
- They reply too late or not at all
- They use generic responses

**AI changes that.**

Here’s a battle-tested framework used by top-performing sales orgs to automate discovery and response to high-intent LinkedIn video comments.

### Step 1: Real-Time Social Listening with AI

You need **instant visibility** into comments on videos from your target accounts or ICP (Ideal Customer Profile).

You can’t just blast a generic “Thanks!” reply. The response must be **context-aware, relevant, and valuable**.

AI enables:

### Step 3: Trigger AI-Powered Outbound Sequences from Comments

Once a comment shows intent, **don’t stop at the reply**. Use it to trigger a **personalized outbound sequence**.

Here’s how it works:

3. Follow-up with a **calendar link** or demo offer
