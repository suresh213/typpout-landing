2. **Higher Response Velocity**

### **Step 2: Craft a High-Converting DM Sequence (That Doesn’t Feel Spammy)**

**Rule #1:** **No hard pitches in the first DM.** Instagram is a **social platform**, not a sales channel.

#### **The 3-DM Sequence That Works in 2026**
| **DM #** | **Purpose** | **Example Script** | **Response Rate Boost** |
|----------|------------|--------------------|-------------------------|
| **DM 1** | **Break the ice** (no ask) | *"Hey [Name], saw your post on [topic]—really insightful! What’s your take on [related trend]?"* | +50% replies vs. direct pitch |
| **DM 2** | **Add value** (share a resource) | *"Quick tip: If you’re exploring [pain point], this guide from [Industry Leader] was helpful for me: [Link]."* | +30% engagement |
| **DM 3** | **Soft CTA** (if they engage) | *"Would love to hear your thoughts—if you’re open to it, I’d love to jump on a quick call to discuss [pain point]."* | +20% meeting bookings |

**Why This Works:**
- **No immediate ask** = prospect feels **valued, not sold to**.
- **Social proof** (mentioning industry leaders) builds **credibility**.
- **Gradual rapport-building** leads to **higher trust**.
