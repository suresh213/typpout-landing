For freelancers, finding clients is a constant struggle. Traditional methods like cold calling, cold emailing, and applying on marketplaces like Upwork are time-consuming and often yield low-paying gigs.

## Sourcing Clients via Social Intent

The most effective way for freelancers to get high-quality clients is to listen for intent. Every day, founders and project managers post on LinkedIn and Twitter looking for help:
* *"Looking for a freelance Next.js developer for a quick project..."*
* *"Need a B2B SaaS copywriter. Drop your portfolio..."*
* *"Anyone know a freelance designer who specializes in SaaS landing pages?"*
