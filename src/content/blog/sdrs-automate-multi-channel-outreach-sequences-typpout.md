## How Typpout Automates Multi-Channel Outreach for SDRs

Typpout isn’t just another outreach tool—it’s an **AI-driven GTM platform** that **orchestrates, personalizes, and optimizes** your multi-channel sequences in real time. Here’s how it works:

### **1. AI-Powered Personalization at Scale**
Typpout’s **AI analyzes prospect data** (LinkedIn profiles, company websites, recent news) to **generate hyper-personalized messages** for each channel.

✅ **Email:** AI crafts subject lines and body copy based on prospect pain points.
✅ **LinkedIn:** Generates connection requests and follow-up messages referencing shared interests.
✅ **SMS:** Sends concise, engaging texts (e.g., *"Hi [Name], saw [Company] just raised funding—congrats! How’s your [specific challenge] going?"*).

**Example:**
Instead of a generic:
> *"Hi [First Name], I’d love to connect and discuss how we can help."*

Typpout delivers:
> *"Hi Sarah, I noticed Company X just expanded its team in APAC—how’s your hiring process for [specific role] going?"*

### **2. Real-Time Social Listening & Trigger-Based Outreach**
Typpout **monitors prospect activity** (e.g., LinkedIn posts, job changes, funding announcements) and **triggers automated sequences** when relevant events occur.

**Use Cases:**
- **Job Change:** *"Congrats on the new role at [Company]! How’s the transition going?"*
- **Funding Round:** *"Saw [Company]’s recent Series B—exciting! What’s your biggest priority right now?"*
- **Post Engagement:** *"Loved your post on [topic]—thought this might be relevant: [link]."*

### **3. Data Waterfalls: Smart Channel Selection**
Not all prospects engage the same way. Typpout’s **Data Waterfalls** **dynamically adjust** the outreach mix based on **historical engagement patterns**.

**How It Works:**
1. **First Touch:** Email (if prospect is responsive to emails).
2. **Follow-Up:** LinkedIn (if no reply in 48 hours).
3. **Final Touch:** SMS (if still no response).

**Result:** **Higher reply rates with fewer manual adjustments.**

### **4. AI Outreach & Reply Handling**
Typpout doesn’t just send messages—it **handles replies intelligently**:
- **Auto-replies** to common questions (e.g., *"What’s your pricing?"*).
- **Escalates complex replies** to SDRs for personalized follow-ups.
- **Books meetings automatically** when prospects show intent.

### **5. Meeting Booking in One Click**
Once a prospect engages, Typpout’s **AI scheduler** sends a **Calendly or HubSpot link** for instant booking—**no back-and-forth emails**.

## Step-by-Step: Setting Up Typpout for Multi-Channel Outreach

Ready to automate? Follow this **5-step framework** to launch your first Typpout sequence.

### **Step 1: Define Your Ideal Prospect Profile**
Use Typpout’s **ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) builder** to segment prospects by:
✔ **Job Title** (e.g., "VP of Sales," "Head of Marketing")
✔ **Industry** (e.g., "SaaS," "FinTech")
✔ **Trigger Events** (e.g., funding, hiring sprees)

**Pro Tip:** The more granular your segmentation, the higher your engagement rates.

### **Step 2: Create Your Multi-Channel Sequence**
In Typpout’s **Sequence Builder**, design a **3-5 touch sequence** across channels.

**Example Sequence:**
| Day | Channel       | Message Type               | Example Message                          |
|-----|---------------|----------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 1   | Email         | Cold Outreach              | *"Hi [Name], saw [Company]’s growth—how’s [specific challenge]?"* |
| 3   | LinkedIn      | Connection Request         | *"Hi [Name], I help [similar companies] with [pain point]—would love to connect."* |
| 5   | SMS           | Follow-Up                  | *"Quick question: How’s your [specific initiative] coming along?"* |
| 7   | Email         | Case Study                 | *"How [Company X] reduced [metric] by 40%—see how."* |
| 10  | Call          | High-Touch Follow-Up       | *"Hi [Name], just checking in—did you get a chance to review?"* |

**Typpout Tip:** Use **merge tags** (`{{First Name}}`, `{{Company}}`) to personalize every message.

### **Step 3: Enable AI Personalization & Triggers**
- **AI Personalization:** Turn on Typpout’s **AI Subject Line Generator** and **Body Copy Optimizer**.
- **Trigger-Based Outreach:** Set up alerts for **job changes, funding rounds, or social engagements**.

### **Step 4: Launch & Monitor Performance**
Track key metrics in Typpout’s **Dashboard**:
📊 **Open Rates** (Email/LinkedIn)
📞 **Reply Rates** (SMS/Calls)
📅 **Meeting Bookings**
⏳ **Sequence Drop-off Points**

**Optimization Hack:** If a channel underperforms (e.g., SMS replies are low), **adjust the sequence mix** in real time.

### **Step 5: Scale with AI Optimization**
Typpout’s **AI continuously learns** from your data:
- **A/B tests subject lines** and message templates.
- **Adjusts send times** based on prospect activity.
- **Recommends new sequences** based on what works.
