## How AI Personalization Solves the Problem: The Typpout Approach

Typpout’s AI doesn’t just tweak a subject line—it **dynamically personalizes every element of outreach** based on real-time data. Here’s how it works:

### 1. **Real-Time Social Listening: The Data Foundation**
Before sending a single message, Typpout’s AI scans prospects’ public social profiles (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, etc.) to extract:
- **Recent posts** (e.g., "Just launched our new feature!")
- **Engagements** (e.g., commented on a post about SaaS pricing)
- **Job changes** (e.g., "New role at [Company] as Head of Growth")
- **Content consumption** (e.g., shared an article on "AI in sales")

**Actionable Insight:**
> *"If a prospect recently posted about ‘scaling sales teams,’ your outreach should reference their growth challenges—not your product’s features."*

**Typpout’s Edge:**
Our AI parses **unstructured social data in real time**, unlike static tools that rely on outdated CRM fields. This ensures your messaging is **always relevant**.

### 3. **Dynamic Outreach: AI Writes, You Approve**
Typpout’s AI doesn’t just pull data—it **generates personalized messages** in real time. Here’s the process:

1. **Input:** Prospect’s LinkedIn URL or email.
2. **AI Processing:** Scans social profiles, website activity, and intent signals.
3. **Message Generation:** Creates a **unique, human-like message** tailored to the prospect.
4. **Human Review:** Sales rep reviews and approves (or tweaks) the message.
5. **Automated Sending:** Uses your preferred channel (email, LinkedIn, or both).

**Example Outputs:**

| **Prospect Signal**               | **AI-Generated Message**                     |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Posted about "remote sales teams" | *"Hi [Name], remote sales teams are killing it with async workflows—our platform helped [Company] reduce ramp time by 40%. Mind if I share how?"* |
| Engaged with pricing page         | *"Saw you’ve been checking out our pricing—many of our customers in [Industry] use us to [Solve X Problem]. Happy to jump on a quick call?"* |
| Shared an article on "AI tools"   | *"Great piece on AI tools—we’re seeing [Industry] teams use ours to [Achieve Y]. Would love to hear your thoughts!"* |

**Why This Works:**
- **90% of prospects respond to messages that feel handwritten** (source: [Reply.io](https://reply.io)).
- Typpout’s AI achieves **reply rates 5-10x higher** than static templates.

Most tools stop at sending messages—but Typpout **handles replies intelligently**:

- **Auto-respond to common questions** (e.g., "What’s your pricing?").
- **Escalate urgent responses** to your team in real time.
