## 🔍 What Is the “Dark Social” in B2B Sales?

The term “Dark Social” originated in consumer marketing, referring to traffic sources that can’t be tracked by standard analytics tools—think WhatsApp shares, Slack DMs, or email forwards.

In **B2B**, Dark Social takes on a more strategic meaning:

> **Dark Social in B2B = Private conversations where buyers share unfiltered opinions about products, vendors, and pricing.**

These include:
- Direct Messages (DMs) on LinkedIn between colleagues
- Private LinkedIn Groups with 50+ high-intent professionals
- Closed Slack communities like “RevOps Leaders” or “SaaS Growth”
- WhatsApp or Telegram channels used by decision-makers
- Email chains where CXOs recommend tools to each other

Unlike public posts, these channels are **invisible to standard social listening tools**. But they are where **real influence happens**.

According to a 2025 Gartner study, **47% of B2B buyers rely on peer recommendations from private networks** before engaging with a vendor. Yet fewer than 10% of sales teams have a strategy to tap into this.
