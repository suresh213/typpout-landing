Albacross is an account identification platform that helps B2B companies track who is visiting their website, generate leads, and target them with personalized ads.

## Where Albacross Falls Short

* **Lack of Individual Contacts:** Identifies visiting companies but does not reveal the specific decision-maker.
* **Complex Setup:** Requires placing script tags and integrating with multiple CRM systems to route leads.
* **Passive Signals:** Web visits are often top-of-funnel research rather than bottom-of-funnel buying signals.
