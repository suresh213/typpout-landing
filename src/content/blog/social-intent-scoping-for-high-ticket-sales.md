For high-ticket and enterprise sales, finding the right entry point is crucial. Sourcing deals based on social intent allows you to identify accounts with active budgets and pressing needs.

## 1. Map Enterprise Accounts

Identify the key decision-makers at your target accounts. Set up social listening filters to monitor their public posts on LinkedIn and X.

## 2. Watch for Internal Shifts

Look for signals such as team expansions, technology switches, or corporate complaints. When a VP of Engineering complains about their current database on social media, it is a high-value entry point.
