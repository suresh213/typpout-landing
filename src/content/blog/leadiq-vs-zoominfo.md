Sales teams often look for tools that help them capture leads directly from LinkedIn. LeadIQ and ZoomInfo are two prominent options.

## LeadIQ: Built for LinkedIn Prospecting

LeadIQ is specifically designed to work with LinkedIn Sales Navigator. It allows SDRs to capture contact data and sync it to their CRM (like Salesforce) in a single click, reducing manual data entry.

## ZoomInfo: The All-In-One Platform

ZoomInfo offers a comprehensive suite of tools, including a massive contact database, intent tracking, and website visitor deanonymization. It is much larger in scope than LeadIQ but is more complex and expensive.
