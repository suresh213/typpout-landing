Bombora is famous for its B2B intent data, which tracks "company surge" behavior across a co-op of business websites. It tells you when a company is researching specific topics more than usual.

## The Issues with Company Surge Data

1. **Lack of Contact Context:** Surge data only tells you that *someone* at the company is researching a topic. It does not provide the name or email of that person.
2. **Delayed Signals:** Surge data is historical, aggregated weekly. By the time you see a surge, the buyer may have already chosen a vendor.
3. **High Minimum Costs:** Bombora is an enterprise tool with high annual contract values.
