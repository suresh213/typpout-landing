## Why Cold Outreach is Dead (And What’s Replacing It)

### The Death of Cold Outreach
Cold outreach has always been inefficient:
- **Low Response Rates:** Even with "personalized" emails, response rates hover around **1-3%** (source: Reply.io).
- **High Churn:** 60% of inbound leads go cold within **5 minutes** if not engaged immediately (HubSpot).
- **Brand Damage:** Prospects associate cold outreach with spam, hurting long-term trust.

### The Shift: AI-Powered, Intent-Driven Engagement
The new GTM model flips the script:
✅ **Proactive, Not Pushy:** AI listens for **real-time buying signals** (job postings, funding rounds, tech stack changes).
✅ **Contextual, Not Generic:** Outreach is **hyper-personalized** based on the prospect’s recent activity.
✅ **Fully Automated:** No manual follow-ups. AI handles **engagement, nurturing, and meeting booking** 24/7.

**Example:** A founder using Typpout’s AI GTM platform saw a **42% increase in pipeline** in 30 days—**without sending a single cold email**.
