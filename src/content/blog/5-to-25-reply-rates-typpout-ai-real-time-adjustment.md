## The Problem: Static Outreach Doesn’t Scale

Most B2B outreach is *static*. You write a message. You send it to 100 prospects. You wait. You follow up. You hope.

But the modern buyer doesn’t behave that way.

- A CFO just closed a funding round? They’re actively hiring.
- A startup just launched a new product? They’re reviewing tools.
- A company just posted about a leadership change? They’re re-evaluating priorities.

These are *real-time signals*—golden opportunities hiding in plain sight. Yet 95% of outreach ignores them. Why? Because humans can’t process thousands of signals per second and adjust messaging dynamically. That’s where AI comes in.

## How AI Real-Time Adjustment Works

Typpout’s AI doesn’t just send messages—it *listens* and *adapts*. Here’s how it works in three layers:

### 1. **Social Listening & Contextual Data**
The AI taps into a real-time stream of public signals:
- LinkedIn posts, comments, and profile updates
- Company hiring pages, funding announcements, and tech stack changes
- Press releases, news articles, and event participation

It classifies these signals into intent buckets:
- **High Intent**: Hiring a sales leader → likely evaluating tools
- **Medium Intent**: Posting about a product launch → evaluating competitors
- **Low Intent**: Generic status update → low priority

> 📊 *Example*: If a prospect posts, “Just joined Acme Corp as VP of Product,” the AI recognizes this as a high-intent signal (new role = budget + need) and adjusts the outreach message to focus on onboarding pain points—*within minutes*.

### 2. **AI-Powered Message Generation**
Instead of a single template, the AI generates *context-specific variants* in real-time.

| Signal Type | Static Message | AI-Adjusted Message |
|-------------|----------------|---------------------|
| General | “Hi [Name], hope you're well.” | — |
| Hiring VP of Sales | — | “Heard you’re scaling the team—we helped [similar company] reduce ramp time by 40%. Want to chat?” |
| Raised funding | — | “Congrats on the $15M round! Many of our customers use our solution to scale post-raise. Quick chat?” |
| Tech stack change | — | “Noticed you’re using Salesforce—many teams switch to us for better [X]. Happy to share a case study.” |

The AI doesn’t just swap variables—it *rewrites* the message to match the signal, the role, and the company stage.

### 3. **Real-Time A/B Testing & Feedback Loop**
Every message is A/B tested across tone, length, and CTA. The AI measures:
- **Reply rate per variant**
- **Time to first reply**
- **CTA click-through rate**

It then *reinforces* the winning variant and phases out underperforming ones—all within hours, not weeks.

> 🔁 *Result*: Your top-performing message gets sent to similar prospects, while the underperformer is retired immediately.

## How to Implement AI Real-Time Adjustment (Even Without Typpout)

You don’t need a platform to start benefiting from real-time signals. Here’s how to build it:

### Step 1: Automate Signal Detection
Use tools like:
- **LinkedIn API** (via tools like Phantombuster or Expandi)
- **RSS feeds** for company news
- **Crunchbase** for funding alerts
- **Google Alerts** for keyword monitoring

Set up a dashboard (e.g., using Zapier + Airtable) to log signals in real time.

### Step 2: Score Signals by Intent
Create a simple scoring system:

| Signal | Score |
|--------|-------|
| Hiring VP of Marketing | 90 |
| Launched new product | 80 |
| Raised Series B | 75 |
| Posted about hiring challenges | 70 |
| Generic post (e.g., “Happy Monday!”) | 10 |

Focus outreach on signals scoring 70+.

### Step 3: Generate Contextual Messages
Use AI tools like:
- **Jasper.ai** or **Copy.ai** for message generation
- **GPT-4** via API to rewrite messages based on signal

Prompt example:
> “Write a LinkedIn message to a VP of Sales who just posted about scaling their team. Mention [your value prop]. Keep it under 100 words.”

### Step 4: Test and Optimize in Real Time
Run small batches (50–100 prospects) and measure:
- Reply rate
- Response time
- Meeting booking rate

Kill underperformers within 24–48 hours.

> ⚠️ *Warning*: Avoid over-automation. Always include a human review for tone and ethics—especially on sensitive signals (e.g., layoffs, crises).
